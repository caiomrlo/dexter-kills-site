**Matt Caldwell:** **Obnoxious Poacher**

**Matt Caldwell** is one of the three overarching antagonists (alongside [Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell") and [Oliver Saxon](/wiki/Oliver_Saxon "Oliver Saxon")) of the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime") spin-off series _**[Dexter: New Blood](/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood")**_. He was a poacher and the son of [Kurt Caldwell](/wiki/Kurt_Caldwell "Kurt Caldwell"), a serial killer who lived a double life as a respected member of the community.

Before arriving in [Iron Lake](/wiki/Iron_Lake "Iron Lake"), Matt was responsible for the deaths of five people in a deliberate boat crash. Often thought of as annoying and entitled by several Iron Lake citizens, Matt was the first person that [Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/New_Blood "Dexter Morgan/New Blood") killed after a nearly ten year hiatus.

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMatt_Caldwell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[Cold Snap](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_101:_Cold_Snap "New Blood Episode 101: Cold Snap")"

[Dexter](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/New_Blood "Dexter Morgan/New Blood") (as [Jim Lindsay](/wiki/Jim_Lindsay "Jim Lindsay")) opens up [Fred's Fish & Game](/wiki/Fred%27s_Fish_%26_Game "Fred's Fish & Game") shop where he’s worked for the past two years. The first customers through the door are two young men, soon identified as Matt Caldwell and [Bill Harris](/wiki/Bill_Harris "Bill Harris").

Immediately, Matt walks over to the counter. He reads Jim's name tag and Dexter asks if he can help him. Matt states that he "just rolled into town with his posse" and is going to do a little hunting and he needs a new knife and a "big-ass gun." When Dexter shows him the knives, Matt picks out the largest one and slashes the air in front of Dexter, who backs away with “Hey! Easy there!” Matt laughs and asks, "Is there a problem?" When Dexter says that he has "kind of a thing about blood," Matt laughs again, mocking him with "Oh, Ooh!"

Matt directs his attention to the rifles and Dexter recommends one. However, Matt whispers that his friend Bill already has that one, and he wants a gun that's going to make his gun "look like a Super Soaker." Matt then points to a large gun prominently displayed on the wall, and Dexter says it costs $9,000. Matt brags that he got a "nice Christmas bonus at Morgan Stanley." Dexter, though, is reluctant to sell him the rifle and remarks that it has more firepower than he needs to hunt in the area. Matt sarcastically asks "Jim" if he hunts and Dexter says he hasn't killed any animals since he was a teenager.

When Dexter asks Matt for his ID for a federal background check, Matt thinks it's ridiculous. He points to a photo on the wall of when he was on Fred Senior's Little League team when he was six, adding that his father bought him his first rifle in the store when he was ten. The computer beeps, showing a 24-hour hold while the FBI checks Matt's record. Matt pressures Dexter to sell him the rifle anyway, but Dexter holds firm. After a brief stare down, Matt swears and buys the knife. He then creeps up on Bill in another part of the store and holds the knife to his throat. Matt laughs at Bill's startled reaction and they leave to go drinking. Dexter thanks them for shopping at Fred's Fish & Game.

That night at Iron Lake Tavern, Dexter tells Tess to put his drinks on his tab. She says that he doesn't need to because Matt Caldwell is buying everyone a round. Dexter looks over at Matt who pounds the table and repeatedly shouts, "Jim! Jim! Jim!" as his friends join in. Dexter tells Tess to put his drinks on his own tab, anyway. Back at his table, Dexter and Angela watch a rowdy Matt drinking heavily with his friends, and she says, "The last thing that guy needs is more liquor. Or more firepower."

While Dexter is alone at the table, Matt comes over and invites him to a party at his [dad](/wiki/Kurt_Caldwell "Kurt Caldwell")'s house the next day. Dexter says dismissively, "I’ll see." Matt suggests that they leave the tavern so "Jim" can open up the shop and give him the gun so he can hunt in the morning. However, Dexter says he must wait until his background clears, but "his buds will be really impressed with the size of his gun." He gives Matt a smug smile as Matt angerly glares at him. Abruptly, Matt breaks into laughter and calls Dexter an "Eagle Scout." He advises "Jim" to come to his party anyway to have a good time, but not to bring his [cop girlfriend](/wiki/Angela_Bishop "Angela Bishop") because there will be a lot of "red flags" there.

The next day, [Fred Jr.](/wiki/Fred_Jr. "Fred Jr.") tells Dexter to sell the gun to Matt because the background check cleared and his [dad](/wiki/Kurt_Caldwell "Kurt Caldwell") is a friend of the store. He asks Dexter to deliver the gun and, reluctantly, Dexter agrees. A party is happening at Matt's house. His friend, [Bill](/wiki/Bill_Harris "Bill Harris"), greets Dexter and brings him upstairs to get a signature for the gun. Matt is having sex with someone in a bedroom and Bill tells him to wait.

Bill is upset when he learns that Matt is having sex with [Becka](/wiki/Becka "Becka"), the girl he brought to the party. He snorts coke and opens up to Dexter about the boat accident, revealing that Matt deliberately rammed another boat and five people died. Bill admits he covered for Matt at the trial, though Matt was really culpable for what happened that day. Bill says he's receiving two grand a week to keep quiet. Matt finally walks out and signs the paperwork to get the gun. As a prank, Matt points the gun at Dexter and Dexter imagines slamming Matt's face with it.

Later on, Matt goes hunting and shoots the white buck that Dexter has seen around the forest and its blood sprays over Dexter’s face. Dexter impulsively hits Matt over the head with his rifle. He takes Matt’s knife but resists using it, thinking, “It’s been a long time.” He cuts the buck’s neck so its blood will cover Matt's blood. Dexter wipes the knife and returns it to Matt’s pants.

Back at home, Dexter hangs up plastic in the shed and lays Matt on a kill table, still alive. Dexter apologizes for the mess because he’s out of practice. Dexter takes a blood slide, which causes Matt to wake up. Dexter then orders Matt to own the bad things he’s done. If not, he’ll kill him. Initially, Matt is defiant to Dexter, not believing he will kill him. When Dexter mentions it wouldn't be the first time, and he's gotten away with it before, Matt quietly asks if he's a serial killer and Dexter says that he used to be - then he corrects himself, “I am." This terrifies Matt and he admits to his wrongdoings on the boat, as well as desperately apologising for the Deer and the accident, insisting he won't ever do it again. In his defense, Matt screams, “It’s not my fault” and blames it on a shitty childhood. When Dexter mentions his early life, Matt asks, "So you're not gonna kill me!" Calmly, Dexter says, "We're past the point of no return on that one. " Matt angrily warns Dexter that his father will find out and destroy him. Dexter answers, "Like you said... life is short... dude." He stabs Matt in the chest, stating, "Hello, Dexter Morgan." He then dismembers and conceals Matt's body, disposing of the blood slide as he decides he's moved past the point where he needs a trophy.

### Murder Investigation\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMatt_Caldwell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[Storm of Fuck](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_102:_Storm_of_Fuck "New Blood Episode 102: Storm of Fuck")"

Matt's dismembered body, his rifle and knife are revealed to be hidden under the stones of Dexter's firepit.

-   "[Smoke Signals](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_103:_Smoke_Signals "New Blood Episode 103: Smoke Signals")"

The [search for Matt](/wiki/Matt_Caldwell_Case "Matt Caldwell Case") continues using Dexter's cabin as a base of operations. Ironically, Matt's body is literally right under the search team's feet as they're cooking breakfast.

Later, it's discovered that Reservation Land Management has footage of Matt killing the deer, surprising Dexter as he had no idea that there were cameras in the wilderness. The cameras turn out to be infrared showing Matt's heat signature and Dexter is able to identify his rifle. [Sam](/wiki/Sam_\(Zoologist\) "Sam (Zoologist)") reveals footage of another man 20 seconds earlier in the next quadrant over. However, luckily for Dexter, there's a blind spot preventing the police from seeing where Dexter went. Angela has Teddy call the tribal police, CSI and secure the crime scene. This new development changes Matt's disappearance from a Missing Persons' case to a possible crime.

Crime Scene Investigator [Damian Church](/wiki/Damian_Church "Damian Church") investigates the White Deer Crime Scene where he finds human blood mixed in with the deer's which Dexter had done to try to cover up the blood spilled by Matt. Dexter points out that Matt could've cut himself dressing the deer, but Angela points out that it could also be the blood of the other person. Damian is able to perform an accurate recreation of the altercation between Dexter and Matt and officially rules it to be foul play causing Dexter to realize that he has become rusty in his inactivity. Angela has Damian take a DNA sample from Kurt to determine if the blood is actually Matt's.

The DNA test confirms that the blood is Matt's and Angela guesses that someone saw Matt shoot the deer on sovereign Seneca land -- a crime with a sentence of at least several thousand dollars -- and wasn't too happy about it. Angela reassures Kurt that more volunteers are coming, and they were going to search as far as [Clarke Caves](/wiki/Clarke_Caves "Clarke Caves") for Matt if that was what it took.

Dexter realizes he needs to move Matt's body to throw the police tracker dogs off the scent. He spends the entire night dragging Matt's hunting jersey through the forest to leave a scent that leads to the motorway.

Later, Dexter attempts to move the body to an abandoned iron mine, but abandons the idea after encountering a very angry bear. He eventually burns Matt's remains in a local sanitation incinerator. Unbeknownst to Dexter, while the incinerator burned most of Matt's remains, the titanium screws he'd received after recovering from the accident did not melt. On his way back home, Dexter learns from a drunk Kurt that he had heard from Matt over FaceTime despite Matt's death. As Dexter drives Kurt home, Matt's ashes fall around them.

-   "[H is for Hero](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_104:_H_is_for_Hero "New Blood Episode 104: H is for Hero")"

Although Matt has seemingly just skipped town, Dexter and [Deb](/wiki/Deb "Deb") suspect that Kurt just wants to look for the killer himself and he provides vague answers about what Matt had actually said when questioned by Angela. Kurt claims that Matt was high and just wanted money, so Kurt might not hear from him again for awhile. In order to close the case, Angela has Logan track Matt's credit cards so that they can find where he was staying.

When Kurt brings Harrison a drone as a gift, he and Dexter talk about their sons and Dexter makes the mistake of revealing that he knows the truth about Matt's boat accident.

Later, Kurt looks at pictures of his son before he abducts [Chloe](/wiki/Chloe "Chloe").

-   "[Runaway](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_105:_Runaway "New Blood Episode 105: Runaway")"

Angela and [Molly Park](/wiki/Molly_Park "Molly Park") visit the hotel in [New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") that Matt's credit cards were used at only to learn that the "Matt Caldwell" who checked in is actually [someone else](/wiki/Elric_Kane "Elric Kane") and Kurt was lying.

-   "[Too Many Tuna Sandwiches](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_106:_Too_Many_Tuna_Sandwiches "New Blood Episode 106: Too Many Tuna Sandwiches")"

Molly confronts Kurt about his lie which he admits to, but covers it up with another lie that Matt had showed up at his lodge drunk and Kurt was just trying to protect him from getting into trouble over the white deer and with people angry that they had spent a week searching for him for no reason. Kurt offers to take Molly to his cabin where Matt was hiding out to talk to him if she kept quiet about it. Following the two, Dexter stops Molly from becoming Kurt's next victim and realizes that he was the serial killer in the Runaways Case that Angela was investigating. Kurt claims that Matt must've left before they reached the cabin to cover up for his absence.

Looking into possible reasons for the lie, Angela realizes that Kurt had Matt found alive and thus the search called off the day before they were to search [Clarke Caves](/wiki/Clarke_Caves "Clarke Caves"), suggesting that there was something there that he had to hide. Angela and [Teddy Reed](/wiki/Teddy_Reed "Teddy Reed") search the caves for Matt's body, but they instead find the body of [Iris Broussard](/wiki/Iris_Broussard "Iris Broussard").

-   "[Skin of Her Teeth](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_107:_Skin_of_Her_Teeth "New Blood Episode 107: Skin of Her Teeth")"

As Dexter helps her solve Iris' case, Angela reveals that she suspects that Kurt had killed her and pretended Matt was in New York to get them off of the case.

Kurt later sends one of Matt's titanium screws to Dexter through Harrison and reveals that he figured out that Dexter had murdered Matt and burned his body. Kurt had realized that he had ash, not snow on his jacket after being picked up at the tavern and titanium doesn't melt so he was able to find the screws from Matt's broken leg from the accident.

-   "[The Family Business](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_109:_The_Family_Business "New Blood Episode 109: The Family Business")"

Before being killed by Dexter, Kurt reveals to [Harrison](/wiki/Harrison_Morgan/New_Blood "Harrison Morgan/New Blood") that his father had murdered Matt and Kurt has been seeking revenge. Kurt also admits that he's kind of jealous because Kurt wishes that Matt was more like Dexter's son. Dexter tells Harrison that Matt had fit the [Code of Harry](/wiki/Code_of_Harry "Code of Harry") for the boat accident and taunts Kurt that "when Matt was on my table, he blamed you for everything. Just like you blamed your dad. Like father, like son."

Later, [Angela Bishop](/wiki/Angela_Bishop "Angela Bishop") finds a note in her mailbox that "[Jim Lindsay](/wiki/Jim_Lindsay "Jim Lindsay") killed Matt Caldwell" as well as some of Matt's titanium screws.

-   "[Sins of the Father](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_110:_Sins_of_the_Father "New Blood Episode 110: Sins of the Father")"

After finding more of Matt's screws in the ruins of [Dexter's Cabin](/wiki/Dexter%27s_Cabin "Dexter's Cabin") -- which had been burned down by Kurt in his revenge quest -- Angela and [Sergeant Logan](/wiki/Sergeant_Logan "Sergeant Logan") arrest Dexter for Matt's murder. Angela reveals the screws which she has confirmed via the serial numbers as the ones used to repair Matt's leg. Angela offers a pretty accurate theory of Matt's murder, but Dexter counters by pointing out Kurt's lies about Matt being in New York and stripping his cabin down a day after Dexter and [Molly Park](/wiki/Molly_Park "Molly Park") were there. Dexter offers up the theory that Kurt killed Matt for any number of accidental or intentional reasons and then chose Dexter to frame as he was the boyfriend of the Chief of Police who had just arrested and humiliated Kurt over the murder of [Iris Broussard](/wiki/Iris_Broussard "Iris Broussard"). Dexter guesses that Kurt burned down his cabin just so that Angela would find Matt's screw. After pulling Angela out of the interview, Logan tells her that the theory is plausible to him and it would be to a jury and the DA as well.

Angela later admits that Dexter might get away with Matt's murder because she's not sure that the case will hold up enough to convince the DA and then the jury. Regardless, Angela still intends to try and convict Dexter before, win or lose, having [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/New_Blood "Angel Batista/New Blood") extradite him to Miami to be tried as the [Bay Harbor Butcher](/wiki/Bay_Harbor_Butcher_Case "Bay Harbor Butcher Case"). However, Dexter escapes and is seemingly killed by his son Harrison before this can happen.

-   "[A Beating Heart...](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_101:_A_Beating_Heart... "Resurrection Episode 101: A Beating Heart...")"

After waking up from a ten-week coma following his near-fatal shooting, [Dexter](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection") learns from Acting Sheriff [Teddy Reed](/wiki/Teddy_Reed/Resurrection "Teddy Reed/Resurrection") that murder of Matt Caldwell was pinned by Angela on his father Kurt who has now been publicly exposed as a serial killer. Along with Sergeant Logan's death being deemed self-defense, Dexter is cleared of any wrongdoing in Iron Lake.

## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMatt_Caldwell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   Matt was 30 while [Becka](/wiki/Becka "Becka") was a teenager when they slept together. If she were under the age of 17 \[New York law\], it would be statutory rape.
-   An intriguing fact is that Matt was born in 1991, which is exactly when [Dexter](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") started killing.
-   Much like [Jeremy Downs](/wiki/Jeremy_Downs "Jeremy Downs"), while not attempting to kill Dexter on purpose, he very much endangers his life. The difference is that Matt does it twice: firstly with a knife (in an incident very similar to one with Downs in "[Popping Cherry](/wiki/Episode_103:_Popping_Cherry "Episode 103: Popping Cherry")") and then a second time, recklessly shooting a white buck when Dexter was in close proximity and could have been hit with the same bullet.
-   Matt was 30 when he died. His father [Kurt](/wiki/Kurt_Caldwell "Kurt Caldwell") killed [Iris Broussard](/wiki/Iris_Broussard "Iris Broussard") 25 years before. Therefore, Matt was five years old when his father killed Iris.
-   It is unknown if Matt knew that his own father was a serial killer and responsible for all of the 'runaways' in Iron Lake. It is unlikely, however, as while he threatened that his father would come after Dexter once he discovered what had happened, Kurt later indicated that Matt never knew about his serial killings.
-   In _[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")_, it's revealed that Matt's death was ultimately blamed on [Kurt](/wiki/Kurt_Caldwell "Kurt Caldwell") and Dexter was cleared of any suspicions thanks to [Angela](/wiki/Angela_Bishop "Angela Bishop") as a sign of thanks for helping to solve [Iris's](/wiki/Iris_Broussard "Iris Broussard") murder.
