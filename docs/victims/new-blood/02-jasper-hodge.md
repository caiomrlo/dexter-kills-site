**Jasper Hodge** **Fentanyl Pill Producer**

**Jasper Hodge** is a character in the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime") special event series _[Dexter: New Blood](/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood")_.

He is a drug dealer who makes the pills himself.

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJasper_Hodge%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[Runaway](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_105:_Runaway "New Blood Episode 105: Runaway")"

Jasper deals drugs to [Miles O'Flynn](/wiki/Miles_O%27Flynn "Miles O'Flynn"), who is another drug dealer who distributes them. However, many of the drugs contain fatal amounts in a single dosage due to Jasper's carelessness or lack of knowledge. When Dexter finds out that Miles bought the pills from Jasper that caused [Harrison](/wiki/Harrison "Harrison") to almost die, he goes to Jasper's [house](/wiki/Jasper_Hodge%27s_House "Jasper Hodge's House") and breaks in. Dexter injects him with a syringe of ketamine to knock him out.

Jasper wakes up and Dexter shows him photos of people who have died from the drugs he gave them. Despite his customer's death, Jasper refuses to take any responsibility, blaming their addictions for causing their death. When Dexter said that his son hadn't been an addict, he angrily asked what type of dad Dexter was if his son was weak enough to drugs, angrily shouting Dexter was a weak dad: this led to Dexter stuffing tissue into his mouth, gagging him, and declaring himself to be a 'great fucking dad'. Dexter is about to stab him when he hears a car pull up. It's [Sergeant Logan](/wiki/Sergeant_Logan "Sergeant Logan"). He still kills Jasper, just not in the way he wanted.

Later on, when the police raid Jasper’s house, they find him sitting in his chair, dead from a drug overdose (forced by Dexter).

-   "[Unfair Game](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_108:_Unfair_Game "New Blood Episode 108: Unfair Game")"

After [Miles O'Flynn](/wiki/Miles_O%27Flynn "Miles O'Flynn") shows [Angela](/wiki/Angela_Bishop "Angela Bishop") the needle mark on his neck inflicted by Jim (Dexter), she visits the morgue to view Jasper's body. The coroner informs her that Jasper died from a fentanyl overdose, which doesn’t point to murder since he was the one making the pills. He adds that Jasper also had Ketamine in his system, which he likely took to "mellow out." Angela asks, "So, there's nothing to suggest that Jasper's death was anything other than an accidental overdose?" The coroner replies, "Well, not unless someone put a gun to his head and made him snort a line." Angela takes a picture of a puncture mark on Jasper's neck.

-   "[The Family Business](/wiki/New_Blood_Episode_109:_The_Family_Business "New Blood Episode 109: The Family Business")"

Jasper's body appears in a picture on Angela's computer as she compares the needle mark on his neck to those on the bodies of the [Bay Harbor Butcher 18](/wiki/Bay_Harbor_Butcher_18 "Bay Harbor Butcher 18").

## Trivia\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJasper_Hodge%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   Fentanyl pills are often called "blues" because of their blue color.
-   Though Jasper fit the Code due to the deaths caused by the drugs he produced, Dexter primarily killed him due to Harrison overdosing on the pills.
-   As per [Marcos Siega](/wiki/Marcos_Siega "Marcos Siega"), Dexter set up the kill room in Jasper's attic.
-   Angela linked Jasper's death to the Bay Harbor Butcher's methods due to comparing the wheal mark to the marks found on the Bay Harbor Butcher 18. Coincidentally, Jasper was responsible for 18 overdoses indirectly.
-   RETCON - Wheal marks found on Bay Harbor Butcher victims is a retcon since none were discovered in the original series, nor was it even known then that tranquilizers were used. To hide evidence, Dexter "melted" the bodies by turning off the field morgue's cooling unit before examinations were completed.
-   Although not directly mentioned in "[A Beating Heart...](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_101:_A_Beating_Heart... "Resurrection Episode 101: A Beating Heart...")" of _[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")_, Dexter was cleared of Jasper's murder as well because Angela chose to drop her allegations that Dexter was the Bay Harbor Butcher.
