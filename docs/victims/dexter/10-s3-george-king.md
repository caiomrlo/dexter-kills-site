**George King** **aka The Skinner**

**George King** (originally **Jorge Orozco**) aka **The Skinner** is the secondary antagonist in [Season Three](/wiki/Season_Three "Season Three") of the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime") series _**[DEXTER](/wiki/Dexter_\(show\) "Dexter (show)")**_.

King was an Indigenous Nicaraguan [Serial Killer](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer") that became the prime target for [Miami Metro Homicide](/wiki/Miami_Metro_Homicide "Miami Metro Homicide"). He only interacted with [Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") near the end of the season. Rather, Dexter's sister, [Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan"), was the Protagonist in this situation and the main focus of the storyline (while Dexter paid little to no attention to the case). In contrast, the Main Antagonist for Dexter was [Miguel Prado](/wiki/Miguel_Prado "Miguel Prado"), who enlisted King's services before he was murdered, thus forcing King to go after Dexter.

## Personality\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGeorge_King%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

King had a bit of an ego, as he took it as a major sign of disrespect when he thought that [Freebo](/wiki/Fred_Bowman "Fred Bowman") had cheated him. He made it clear that it was more about respect than money. He was willing to skin people alive to get information on Freebo's whereabouts. While he had his flimsy justifications, Dexter implied that King may simply have liked skinning people.

## Early Life\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGeorge_King%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Jorge Orozco was born in Nicaragua. He was conscripted into his country's army, where he rose to the rank of Captain in the Resistencia Nicaragüense (Popularly known as the "Contras"); heading an interrogation unit. He tortured and killed people for a living, to the point he was affectionately referred to in his homeland as "The Blade." It is implied that his army experiences caused him to be a control freak who strongly valued respect.

Orozco left the army and moved to the United States. He changed his name to George King and settled in [Miami](/wiki/Miami "Miami"), founding and becoming boss of his own tree-trimming company. Orozco was nicknamed "The Skinner" named after his [M.O.](/wiki/M.O. "M.O.") of removing the skin from his victims as a method of torture.

When one of his criminal associates, [Freebo](/wiki/Fred_Bowman "Fred Bowman"), disappears while owing him a large amount of money (he was actually killed by Dexter), King begins hunting for him throughout Miami, feeling that he has been disrespected.

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGeorge_King%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[The Lion Sleeps Tonight](/wiki/Episode_303:_The_Lion_Sleeps_Tonight "Episode 303: The Lion Sleeps Tonight")"

[Javier Garza](/wiki/Javier_Garza "Javier Garza") is found dead with his skin peeled off and it's assumed that [Freebo](/wiki/Fred_Bowman "Fred Bowman") killed him. However, Dexter knows that isn't the case since he had killed Freebo before Javier was murdered.

[Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan") asks George King, a tree trimmer, to climb down from the top of a palm tree so that she can question him. King admits to calling in the body but says that another tree trimmer, [Mario Astorga](/wiki/Mario_Astorga "Mario Astorga"), found it.

The police soon find evidence that Freebo may have fled town.

-   "[Turning Biminese](/wiki/Episode_305:_Turning_Biminese "Episode 305: Turning Biminese")" (appears in shadows)

Knowing the police are also looking for Freebo, King trails Detective [Debra](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan") around town as she meets with Freebo's associates in an attempt to find him. He covertly watches their homes under the cover of trimming their trees.

While Debra is talking with [Wendell Owens](/wiki/Wendell_Owens "Wendell Owens"), King watches them, using binoculars.

-   "[Sí Se Puede](/wiki/Episode_306:_S%C3%AD_Se_Puede "Episode 306: Sí Se Puede")" (does not appear)

The body of [Wendell](/wiki/Wendell_Owens "Wendell Owens") is found after King kidnaps, tortures, and skins him.

-   "[The Damage a Man Can Do](/wiki/Episode_308:_The_Damage_a_Man_Can_Do "Episode 308: The Damage a Man Can Do")"

King is called to the station and agrees to provide a list of his employees in a polite and respectful manner.

King is again questioned, but deflects attention to Mario, his employee. Mario then comes under suspicion when King reveals that Mario killed a man with a knife back in Nicaragua.

Suspicion, though, soon centers on King when Mario has a near-panic attack when he learns that King is talking to the police.

Debra is worried about [Anton Briggs](/wiki/Anton_Briggs "Anton Briggs") when she goes to his apartment, finds him missing, and notices the nearby trees are trimmed. She previously warned him that he was in danger because the police set him up to appear as if he knew the location of Freebo.

-   "[About Last Night](/wiki/Episode_309:_About_Last_Night "Episode 309: About Last Night")"

The police determine that Anton has been kidnapped by The Skinner and alerts are sent out.

Quinn is annoyed when Debra breaks into King's shed before the warrant arrives. While there, King pulls into his driveway and they take him back to the station for more questioning. He is uncooperative, causing Debra to lose her temper. The police end up releasing King after [Miguel Prado](/wiki/Miguel_Prado "Miguel Prado") the ADA has a private talk with him.

King is stopped from finishing Anton off and forced to flee when Debra and [Joey Quinn](/wiki/Joey_Quinn "Joey Quinn") learn from Mario where Anton is being held captive.

-   "[Go Your Own Way](/wiki/Episode_310:_Go_Your_Own_Way "Episode 310: Go Your Own Way")"

From nearby, King watches [Dexter](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") and Miguel argue on the roof of the police station. When Dexter leaves, Miguel calls King to make sure he got a good look at Dexter. He tells King that Dexter is the only one who knows the location of Freebo.

-   "[I Had a Dream](/wiki/Episode_311:_I_Had_a_Dream "Episode 311: I Had a Dream")"

Debra and Quinn find King hiding in a vacant house, but he escapes, injuring Quinn in the process.

-   "[Do You Take Dexter Morgan?](/wiki/Episode_312:_Do_You_Take_Dexter_Morgan%3F "Episode 312: Do You Take Dexter Morgan?")"

The man-hunt resumes when Dexter frames King for [Miguel Prado](/wiki/Miguel_Prado "Miguel Prado")'s murder. Before the police can locate King, he abducts Dexter and takes him to an abandoned tobacco warehouse. He intends to torture and skin Dexter for information, but Dexter effectively strips him of his control by revealing that he already killed Freebo and dumped his body parts in the [Gulf Stream](/wiki/Gulf_Stream "Gulf Stream").

King is visibly thrown off by these facts, especially when Dexter correctly guesses that King simply enjoys torturing and killing people and has convinced himself otherwise. Dexter tells King that his search for Freebo is only a justification for selecting his victims. King walks away to get his torture tools but, before he returns, Dexter successfully frees himself of his bindings, but breaks his hand in the process. The two engage in a brutal fight that ends when Dexter snaps King's neck.

The police arrive on scene, having tracked down King's hideout. Dexter avoids detection by dropping King's body off the upper level of the warehouse just as a police car drives through, appearing to be suicide. King's body is dragged several feet, ironically causing his face to be skinned. After seeing his body, Debra states, "What goes around comes around."

## Trivia\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGeorge_King%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   The Skinner is based on a villain from the novel _[Dearly Devoted Dexter](/wiki/Dearly_Devoted_Dexter "Dearly Devoted Dexter")_, Dr. Danco. They both have the same past as torturers who turned into serial killers, and one of their victims is [Deborah](/wiki/Deborah_Morgan "Deborah Morgan")'s lover. Yet, in the novel, Danco does worse than skinning his victims: he cuts their limbs off, one by one. Still, in the book, it isn't Dexter who kills him, but Deborah.
-   Despite being based on [Dr. Danco](/wiki/Dr._Danco "Dr. Danco"), there are several differences. For one, Danco only kills one person; the rest he turns into "stubs," which drives them insane.
-   King is the first main antagonist to have virtually no emotional connection to [Dexter.](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") [Brian Moser](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser") was Dexter's long lost brother, [Lila West](/wiki/Lila_West "Lila West") was his lover, and [Miguel Prado](/wiki/Miguel_Prado "Miguel Prado") was considered his first true friend.
-   Interestingly, one of the cases being worked on in [Season Two](/wiki/Season_Two "Season Two") is called the "Orozco Case."
-   [The Skinner Case](/wiki/Skinner_Case "Skinner Case") is never referred to in later seasons, while the Bay Harbor Butcher Case and [Ice Truck Killer Case](/wiki/Ice_Truck_Killer_Case "Ice Truck Killer Case") receive mentions (especially in [Season Six](/wiki/Season_Six "Season Six")), as well as the later [Trinity Case](/wiki/Trinity_Case "Trinity Case"). This shares a commonality with the [Santa Muerte Case](/wiki/Santa_Muerte_Case "Santa Muerte Case") which is not mentioned again after the case is closed.
-   Due to [Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan")'s impressive work on this case (which included saving the life of Anton Briggs and identifying the Skinner), Batista recommends that she receive her detective shield. However, a problem arises when it comes to light that Debra had slept with Anton. In the end, LaGuerta gives Deb her shield and praises her as "one of the smartest, hardest-working, most annoying pain in the ass cops" in her department.
-   [Miguel Prado](/wiki/Miguel_Prado "Miguel Prado")'s murder (for which Dexter copied the Skinner's [M.O.](/wiki/M.O. "M.O.")) is said by Sergeant [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista "Angel Batista") to be an "Officially HUGE" case, but it is treated as minor, even compared to the [Santa Muerte Case](/wiki/Santa_Muerte_Case "Santa Muerte Case") later.
-   Many fans debate whether The Skinner or [Miguel Prado](/wiki/Miguel_Prado "Miguel Prado") is the true main antagonist of season 3.
-   Because [Felipe Cepeda](/wiki/Felipe_Cepeda "Felipe Cepeda"), [Tilly](/wiki/Tilly "Tilly"), [Lookout Kid on Bike](/wiki/Lookout_Kid_on_Bike "Lookout Kid on Bike") and [Zack Adelman](/wiki/Zack_Adelman "Zack Adelman") were associates of Freebo like Teegan, Garza and Wendell were, King would've kidnapped, tortured (By skinning) and killed them too.
    -   If Dexter didn't kill Oscar, because Oscar was also an associate of Freebo, King would've kidnapped, tortured (By skinning) and killed him as well.
