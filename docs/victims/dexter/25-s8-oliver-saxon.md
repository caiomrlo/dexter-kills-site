**Oliver Saxon****  
The Brain Surgeon**

**Oliver Saxon** (born as **Daniel Vogel**) is a recurring character and **major antagonist** in **[Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime")**'s _**[DEXTER](/wiki/Dexter_\(show\) "Dexter (show)")**_ franchise.

In the original [Dexter](/wiki/Dexter "Dexter"), He serves as the main antagonist of [Season Eight](/wiki/Season_Eight "Season Eight"). He also serves as one of the three overarching antagonists (alongside [Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell") and [Matt Caldwell](/wiki/Matt_Caldwell "Matt Caldwell")) of the spin off series [Dexter: New Blood](/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood").

He is the **[Serial Killer](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer")** known as **The Brain Surgeon** (due to scooping out a piece of brain from each of his victims), who moved to [Miami](/wiki/Miami "Miami") in 2008, after claiming lives during his travels from city to city (established in ["Goodbye Miami](/wiki/Episode_810:_Goodbye_Miami "Episode 810: Goodbye Miami")"). Months following the death of **Captain [Maria LaGuerta](/wiki/Maria_LaGuerta "Maria LaGuerta")**, his victims start cropping up around the city - leading **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan")** on a pursuit that leads to a former patient named **[A.J. Yates](/wiki/A.J._Yates "A.J. Yates")** whom Dexter killed, Saxon finally introduces himself as **[Cassie Jollenston](/wiki/Cassie_Jollenston "Cassie Jollenston")'**s boyfriend - a seemingly regular guy dating his neighbor. Following Cassie's death he plays the grief-stricken lover while secretly getting closer to his mother **Dr. [Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel")**.

Saxon is the **Main Antagonist** of **[Season Eight](/wiki/Season_Eight "Season Eight")** and the **Final Antagonist** of the original series. He is directly responsible for killing **[Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan")**, Dexter's adoptive sister and causes the separation between Dexter and his son **[Harrison Morgan](/wiki/Harrison_Morgan "Harrison Morgan")** as a result - not reuniting until **[Dexter: New Blood](/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood")**.

## Personality\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOliver_Saxon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Originally portrayed as a charming man who was dating Dexter's neighbor, [Cassie Jollenston](/wiki/Cassie_Jollenston "Cassie Jollenston"), Saxon was later revealed to be a psychopath and serial killer who could mimic normal human emotions.

As a "perfect psychopath" (coined by [Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan")), Saxon was devoid of empathy, remorse, and fear. His other traits included being very intelligent, manipulative, cunning, and ruthless.

Dexter was able to deduce that Saxon's real name was Daniel Vogel, the son of Dr. [Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel"). Daniel was born to be a killer. At age fourteen, he drowned his younger [brother](/wiki/Richard_Vogel_Jr. "Richard Vogel Jr.") and didn't feel an ounce of guilt. He was subsequently committed to a psychiatric hospital in England which used overly harsh treatment methods. To escape, he set a fire that killed seven of the children. Thus, he became a mass murderer, and later a serial killer. Daniel then faked his death and changed his name to Oliver Saxon, a name which belonged to a young boy who died at age 10 at the Harrington Youth Facility in Oxforshire, U.K. in 1989.

He was sadistic toward his victims, torturing them both physically and psychologically. Saxon found killing to be a cathartic experience and genuinely enjoyed it. Lacking a moral code, Saxon could murder anyone without a second thought, even his own family members. Dexter considered him a complete monster, and wondered how many people Saxon had actually killed, since he had lived in several different cities.

Despite his death, Saxon completely destroyed Dexter's life and ended his reign as the Bay Harbor Butcher for ten years.

**Public Persona**

Saxon appeared to be a normal individual by smiling and using friendly gestures, and would speak in a polite and calm manner. He held a steady, responsible job and lived in a [rented apartment](/wiki/Oliver_Saxon%27s_Apartment "Oliver Saxon's Apartment").

He treated his girlfriend, [Cassie Jollenston](/wiki/Cassie_Jollenston "Cassie Jollenston"), respectfully and appeared to be a man in love. After her death, he took on a solemn personality and expressed his sorrow. Spoken to, he referred to how happy they were together and their plans for the future. Assuredly, these emotions were false to aid his alibi against being Cassie's killer.

## Early Life\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOliver_Saxon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Daniel Vogel and [Richard Vogel Jr.](/wiki/Richard_Vogel_Jr. "Richard Vogel Jr."), his younger brother, were born to Dr. [Richard Vogel](/wiki/Richard_Vogel "Richard Vogel") and [Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel"). The family lived a normal lifestyle in Miami for the most part. However, Daniel grew jealous and resentful of Richard Jr. At the age of fourteen, Daniel acted on this rage by killing his brother.

Evelyn found her youngest son dead at the bottom of their swimming pool. Initially, she believed that he had drowned, as he wasn't a strong swimmer. She and her husband, however, soon accepted that Daniel had killed Richard Jr., but they covered it up as an accident.

Evelyn had already recognized that Daniel was a psychopath and pursued a career as a psychiatrist, hoping to help him. When Daniel killed his brother, she realized that her efforts were futile and he was sent to a mental health institute in England. Unfortunately, the staff practiced harsh treatments, such as strapping youths to an examination table in order to force-feed their medications. Three years passed at this psychiatric hospital, with Daniel growing more bitter because his mother never came to pick him up.

When he was seventeen, Daniel set a fire at the facility. A nurse was blamed, as it was thought that she went on break while leaving a door locked. This prevented the children from escaping the flames and seven children perished. Daniel was believed to be one of them. His father, [Richard](/wiki/Richard_Vogel "Richard Vogel"), identified his son's body, even though it was burned beyond recognition. In reality, Daniel had faked his death.[\[1\]](#cite_note-1)

## History\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOliver_Saxon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Now free in the world, Daniel assumed the identity of a deceased boy by the name of Oliver Saxon who had died 26 November 1989. The "new Saxon" lived his life traveling between at least a dozen cities in Europe and North America. Dexter suspected that Saxon had killed unknown others in these places and then would move on to avoid being caught.

In the United States, Saxon's known residences include:

-   2003: Galveston, Texas, for three years
-   2006: Tallahassee, Florida, for two years
-   2008: [Miami](/wiki/Miami "Miami"), Florida, until death

Since Saxon had lived in Miami since 2008, he was present during the [Skinner Case](/wiki/Skinner_Case "Skinner Case"), [Trinity Case](/wiki/Trinity_Case "Trinity Case"), [Barrel Girls Case](/wiki/Barrel_Girls_Case "Barrel Girls Case"), and the [Doomsday Killer Case](/wiki/Doomsday_Killer_Case "Doomsday Killer Case").

On 15 August 2010, Saxon was employed as a building inspector by Miami Prime Building Inspections.[\[2\]](#cite_note-2)

In 2011, [Dr. Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel"), Saxon's mother, moved back to Miami (established in "[A Beautiful Day](/wiki/Episode_801:_A_Beautiful_Day "Episode 801: A Beautiful Day")").

In 2012, Saxon began to anonymously leave excised brain-matter on his mother's doorstep to send her a message regarding empathy. Evelyn informed [Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") that the killer could be one of her former patients. She finally discovered that he was her own son, Daniel Vogel (under the alias of Oliver Saxon).

## Season Eight\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOliver_Saxon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[A Beautiful Day](/wiki/Episode_801:_A_Beautiful_Day "Episode 801: A Beautiful Day")"

[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") is called to a scene where the corpse of a man was found lying near a body of water. Appearing to have been simply shot and dumped, the man is identified as [Robert Bailey](/wiki/Robert_Bailey "Robert Bailey"), a mechanic. Upon further examination, it's revealed that the skull had been sawed into and a portion of the brain scooped out.

Back at [Miami Metro Homicide](/wiki/Miami_Metro_Homicide "Miami Metro Homicide") Department, Deputy Chief [Thomas Matthews](/wiki/Thomas_Matthews "Thomas Matthews") debriefs detectives involved in the case. He introduces a consultant named Dr. [Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel"), a neuropsychiatrist with whom Matthews had worked with before. She tells the team that the killer carefully extracted a specific portion of the brain - the anterior insular cortex. It's the part that processes empathy, and she finds it ironic that the very hallmark of a psychopath is that they have no empathy. She says it was either an experiment or a message, which wouldn't be the first time a killer has used a victim to communicate.

Details of the way Bailey died are revealed via the medical examiner's report; he was first shot and the surgical removal of the insular cortex was done after his death. The victim had been kept in captivity in an enclosed space for about five days since no contents were present in his stomach, and he was dehydrated. In her educated opinion, this was likely not the killer's first murder, as such a carefully thought out ritual suggests he has claimed lives before, and will likely strike again.

-   "[Every Silver Lining...](/wiki/Episode_802:_Every_Silver_Lining... "Episode 802: Every Silver Lining...")"

[Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel") informs Dexter about her knowledge (and involvement) in regards to his past. Knowing exactly who he is, she shows him a videotape of [Harry](/wiki/Harry_Morgan "Harry Morgan")'s sessions with her. Ultimately, she steers the conversation towards a favor she needs. From a nearby cupboard she pulls out a jar containing brain matter (from Robert Bailey), and says that it was left on her door step. Her concern is that it may be a warning or a threat on her life by one of her former patients. Uncertain of who it is, she asks Dexter to aide her and he states that he doesn't take "requests".

Elsewhere, in a tiled room, a man named [Leonard Welks](/wiki/Leonard_Welks "Leonard Welks") is strapped to a chair with medical supplies stored in nearby cabinets. The song "Make Your Own Kind of Music" by Mama Cass Elliot is playing in the background. Another man by the name of [Lyle Sussman](/wiki/Lyle_Sussman "Lyle Sussman") approaches Welks, who can't speak because duct tape covers his mouth. Sussman apologizes to the man and says he doesn't deserve this, but he doesn't have a choice. Sussman then puts a plastic bag over Welks's head and suffocates him.

The following day, Welks' body is found in a skate park. Dexter arrives and shifts the head, revealing the same separation of the skull with a piece of the brain scooped out. Observations of the corpse reveal strangulation, and the plastic bag used to kill Welks if found nearby by Dexter.

Dexter returns to the station and links a fingerprint on the bag to [Lyle Sussman](/wiki/Lyle_Sussman "Lyle Sussman"). Vogel does not recognize the man and says he was not one of her former patients which gives her some relief. Dexter investigates the empty house belonging to Sussman, finding that he's a very neat person, much like Dexter himself. He also learns that he's a big game hunter, due to a photo featuring the name of a lake, and he decides to further investigate Sussman.

When Dexter does arrives at Sussman's cabin, he finds a selection of knives and other hunting equipment, including an obscure metal chair that could be capable of keeping someone strapped down. Before Dexter can look further, he hears a thumping noise outside and heads out through the back door onto the patio...where he discovers Sussman hanging by a hook.

He returns to tell Vogel this news, and suggests that an accomplice killed him. Vogel states that it's highly unlikely and she's certain that the killer does everything alone. After a lengthy talk, Dexter departs. Not long after, Vogel fearfully calls Dexter to say that someone is in her house. Concerned for her life, she waits for Dexter to arrive, who searches the house and finds no one inside. However, a DVD was left on Vogel's desk. When played, it shows Sussman being forced to kill Welks, only to be brutally shot in the back of the head with a pistol. The true criminal is unseen except for his blue gloves.

-   "[What's Eating Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Episode_803:_What%27s_Eating_Dexter_Morgan%3F "Episode 803: What's Eating Dexter Morgan?")"

When the police investigate Sussman's crime scene, Dexter realizes that the body had been relocated when he finds Sussman on the ground with a gigantic sniper-rifle wound in the back of his head. The police rule it a suicide, but Dexter knows the true killer re-staged the whole scene. Elsewhere, Vogel receives a mysterious text telling her to look outside. When she opens the door, she finds two more brain pieces inside tiny boxes, labeled "His" and "Hers." Since these parts are responsible for vision, Dexter states that the killer must now be aware of both of them.

-   "[Scar Tissue](/wiki/Episode_804:_Scar_Tissue "Episode 804: Scar Tissue")"

Dexter now suspects [A.J. Yates](/wiki/A.J._Yates "A.J. Yates") of being The Brain Surgeon, a former patient of Vogel who had seemed beyond her help. As Dexter spies on him, he notices that Yates has a marking on the back of his head in the exact place where the killer cuts open his victims. When Dexter asks Vogel about the scar, she explains that Yates had a lesion on his brain, and the hospital conducted surgery to remove it, leaving a permanent scar.

-   "[This Little Piggy](/wiki/Episode_805:_This_Little_Piggy "Episode 805: This Little Piggy")"

Thing become complicated concerning A.J. Yates, including his kidnapping of Vogel. However, she is rescued by Dexter and Debra before she is tortured and killed, and Dexter ends Yates life. Believing that The Brain Surgeon is dead, Dexter disposes of the body while bonding with Vogel and Debra on his boat.

-   "[A Little Reflection](/wiki/Episode_806:_A_Little_Reflection "Episode 806: A Little Reflection")"

With the apparent death of The Brain Surgeon (presumed by Dexter and [Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel") to be [A.J. Yates](/wiki/A.J._Yates "A.J. Yates")), Dexter focuses on training a protege killer by the name of [Zach Hamilton](/wiki/Zach_Hamilton "Zach Hamilton"). He also has lunch with his most recent neighbor, [Cassie Jollenston](/wiki/Cassie_Jollenston "Cassie Jollenston"), after her friend, [Jamie Batista](/wiki/Jamie_Batista "Jamie Batista"), insists on it. However, Dexter's awkward answers on their date and lack of conversational topics leaves Cassie available to go out with a new man.

For Jamie's birthday, [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista "Angel Batista") throws a beach party at [Papa's Cafe](/wiki/Papa%27s_Cafe "Papa's Cafe") to celebrate. Dexter observes Cassie talking to an unknown man at the party and she seems taken with his charm. Jamie notices the two flirting with one another and tells Dexter that she doesn't know who the guy is, but if he wants a shot at Cassie, now is his chance.

A little later, Dexter moves closer to Cassie and starts another awkward conversation with her. Almost immediately, the unknown man walks up to them with beers in hand. Cassie introduces the two, with Dexter learning that the man's name is Oliver Saxon.

Saxon extends his hand to shake Dexter's with a generous smile. He tells Cassie how he wasn't sure which beer to grab, but decided to go with the one with the "Moose" on it, which humors her. Dexter looks for an out and says that he is going to check on his son, [Harrison.](/wiki/Harrison_Morgan "Harrison Morgan") He then walks away. leaving Cassie and Saxon to drink their beer and talk.

-   "[Dress Code](/wiki/Episode_807:_Dress_Code "Episode 807: Dress Code")"

Saxon is seen alongside Cassie outside of her apartment (the two are about to go swimming) while Dexter is talking with his new disciple, [Zach Hamilton](/wiki/Zach_Hamilton "Zach Hamilton"), about patiently controlling his urges until the opportunity arises to kill an evil person. However, once Cassie is murdered the following night, Dexter suspects that it was Zach's doing (because of the similar bludgeoning to [Norma Rivera](/wiki/Norma_Rivera "Norma Rivera"), whom Zach had previously killed).

-   "[Are We There Yet?](/wiki/Episode_808:_Are_We_There_Yet%3F "Episode 808: Are We There Yet?")"

Dexter manages to track Zach down to the Keys, where the latter has apparently fled. Once he discovers that Zach has prepared a [kill room](/wiki/Kill_Room "Kill Room") inside of his [motel](/wiki/Windy_Keys_Motel "Windy Keys Motel") (he replicated Dexter's style), Dexter believes that Zach is planning to kill another innocent person. Upon chastising Zach, he realizes that Zach was proactively hunting [Shawn Decker](/wiki/Shawn_Decker "Shawn Decker"), a former classmate of his, who had bragged about about killing a woman during sex. After learning of Zach's worthiness, Dexter is convinced that Zach was framed for Cassie's murder.

After returning to Miami, Dexter enters his [apartment](/wiki/Dexter%27s_Apartment "Dexter's Apartment") to find the corpse of Zach sitting in a chair with his brain cut open (proving that The Brain Surgeon is NOT [A.J. Yates](/wiki/A.J._Yates "A.J. Yates")). The song "Make Your Own Kind of Music" is playing. Dexter and Vogel realize for the first time since Yates's demise, that "The Brain Surgeon" is still out there and still a critical threat.

-   "[Make Your Own Kind of Music](/wiki/Episode_809:_Make_Your_Own_Kind_of_Music "Episode 809: Make Your Own Kind of Music")"

Dexter resumes his hunt for The Brain Surgeon, intent on ending this cat-and-mouse game once and for all. He finds several hairs in Zach's studio, which he thinks Zach instinctively pulled from his assailant. He takes this hair to the police department to perform DNA tests on it.

Upon testing the hair, he realizes that Zach's killer is related to Dr. [Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel"). When he questions her, Dexter learns of Vogel's early life and the fact that she had two sons, Daniel and [Richard](/wiki/Richard_Vogel_Jr. "Richard Vogel Jr.") (the latter Daniel had drowned in a swimming pool). After this, Vogel sent Daniel away to a psychiatric hospital in England. After three years, a large fire broke out (apparently due to a nurse's carelessness) that killed seven of the children inhabiting the facility. Daniel was apparently one of these, because his [father](/wiki/Richard_Vogel "Richard Vogel") identified a burned body as his son. Dexter, however, suspects that due to his intelligence and lack of empathy, Daniel started the fire himself, and later assumed the identity of the actual Oliver Saxon. Vogel gives Dexter a photograph of young Daniel, which Dexter takes to run facial tests. He inserts the picture into a computer and updates Daniel's age by 40 years. It then reveals him to look exactly like Oliver Saxon, whom Dexter recognizes instantly.

From DNA on a can in Saxon's trash, Dexter is able to him match him to Zach's killer. This confirms that Saxon is definitely Vogel's son. Vogel is appalled by this news, but refuses to allow Dexter to kill him (believing that her son deserves a second chance). Dexter pretends to agree with Vogel, but is determined to kill Saxon.

Concluding that Saxon is using spyware to monitor Vogel's computer activity, Dexter has write in her online journal that she is going to [King's Bay Cafe](/wiki/King%27s_Bay_Cafe "King's Bay Cafe") the next morning. It's the place where she used to take her son years ago. Before she leaves for the café, Dexter spikes her tea with a sedative and she passes out. He then goes alone to King's Bay Café, intending to capture Saxon.

Dexter stays outside and watches through the window as Saxon repeatedly plays a certain song ("Make Your Own Kind of Music" by Mama Cass Elliot). Saxon grows annoyed when his mother does not show up. After he exits the café, Dexter follows him to his truck, readying himself with a [tranquilizer](/wiki/Etorphine "Etorphine"). However, he discovers that his own tires have been deflated by Oliver, who was evidently aware of Dexter's presence, and is now seemingly on his way to [Evelyn's house](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel%27s_House "Evelyn Vogel's House"). Dexter manages to reach her house before she is harmed and he warns her of the threat that her son poses. Vogel is angered about being drugged earlier and orders Dexter to leave the matter to her. As Dexter departs, the scene pans and it's revealed that Saxon is hiding inside of Vogel's house. She reassures Saxon that she wasn't lying to him and the two sit down together in the kitchen.

-   "[Goodbye Miami](/wiki/Episode_810:_Goodbye_Miami "Episode 810: Goodbye Miami")"

Picking up after the previous episode, Saxon proceeds to accuse his mother of having imprisoned him in the mental facility. She responds that it was her only choice (because Daniel had killed his younger brother). Saxon continues to upbraid her, and expresses his jealousy of her attention to [Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan"). Vogel strongly professes her desire to help her son, but the situation begins to escalate, and Saxon storms out of the house.

When Vogel returns home from a visit to [the department](/wiki/Miami_Metro_Police_Department "Miami Metro Police Department"), she finds Saxon nonchalantly waiting for her arrival. He opens his truck door for her to enter, and convinces her that he will cause no harm. Vogel warily enters the vehicle, and the two drive off to the abandoned Rendell Psychiatric Hospital, where Saxon leads her into [The Treatment Room](/wiki/The_Treatment_Room "The Treatment Room") -- the very room where he kills his victims.  

He informs his mother of what he endured while living in the psychiatric facility in England. He describes how the doctors would strap him to an examination table (like his own kill table) and force him to take his medications. He explains that he chose this room because it reminds him of the room where he was "tortured." Saxon begs her to help him to freely exist as a murderer, like Dexter does. Vogel, feeling that her son is sincere, promises him that she will find a solution, so they share a hug for the first time in many decades.

After Dexter proves Saxon's guilt with a video tape of him killing Zach with a bone saw, he convinces Vogel that her son is unchangeable and he must be killed. Although disheartened, Vogel reluctantly agrees with the plan of meeting Saxon in an isolated area, where Dexter can sedate and capture him.

However, Vogel wants to say a final goodbye and she lets Saxon into her house instead. While drinking tea, Saxon notices her nervous behavior and questions her. He recognizes her fear and determines what she has planned. When Dexter arrives outside, Saxon calls him, saying that he wants to show him something. He turns on a light revealing Vogel in front of a window with a knife to her neck. Dexter orders him not to touch her, but Saxon mercilessly slits her throat, while Dexter looks on in horror. Dexter breaks open the door, only to find Vogel bleeding out on the floor. Dexter embraces her in deep sorrow as she takes her final breaths, while Saxon uses this time to flee.

-   "[Monkey in a Box](/wiki/Episode_811:_Monkey_in_a_Box "Episode 811: Monkey in a Box")"

Dexter quickly removes her DVDs and files on him, and washes his blood-covered hands to avoid himself becoming a suspect in her death. He then calls the police and pretends that Vogel called him to her house where he found her already dead.

The following day, Saxon goes to [Miami Metro](/wiki/Miami_Metro_Homicide "Miami Metro Homicide") to clear his name of being a suspect in the murder of [Cassie](/wiki/Cassie_Jollenston "Cassie Jollenston"). He speaks with Quinn, providing excuses for every question that he is asked. Dexter soon arrives to perform a DNA cheek swab, and is stunned to see Saxon there. Saxon asks if the DNA test is really necessary, but he cooperates and then departs. Dexter, knowing that he can now tie Saxon to Vogel, gives Saxon a fake smile as he leaves.

To meet with Dexter face to face, Saxon contacts [Sylvia Prado](/wiki/Sylvia_Prado "Sylvia Prado") who has offered to sell [Dexter's apartment](/wiki/Dexter%27s_Apartment "Dexter's Apartment"). She calls Dexter, notifying him of an interested buyer. Dexter returns home, once again appalled to see Saxon, who politely asks if Dexter can personally show him around the apartment, and Sylvia instantly agrees. This gives the two men a chance to speak alone.

After Sylvia exits, Oliver proposes a truce, suggesting that he and Dexter should part ways and go on with their respective lives. Saxon warns Dexter that he has much more to lose than himself. He reinforces this by mentioning the members of Dexter's family, including his step-children, who currently live in Orlando. Dexter takes a moment to think about it and then pretends to agree to the truce. After Saxon leaves, Dexter goes to Deb's house to warn her and Hannah about Saxon's "veiled threat."

Dexter, never intending to go along with the truce, provides the police with evidence from the DNA swab which reveals that Saxon is Vogel's son. Also, Dexter locates Saxon's kill room inside an [abandoned hospital](/wiki/Rendall_Psychiatric_Hospital "Rendall Psychiatric Hospital"). There, he finds a computer incriminating him in the murders of several people (as revealed by recordings he took of himself killing his victims). Dexter uploads the footage and anonymously sends it to the media, which makes Saxon a wanted man.

While at a gas station, Saxon notices the news about the manhunt for himself and immediately knows that Dexter is the one who is responsible. He is enraged and rushes over to Dexter's apartment to kill him (just as Dexter planned). He approaches an apparently sleeping Dexter, knife-in-hand, ready to strike. However, Debra suddenly approaches from behind with a gun to Saxon's head, and Dexter rises to inject him with a dose of [M99](/wiki/Etorphine "Etorphine").

After sedating Saxon, Dexter takes him to [The Treatment Room](/wiki/The_Treatment_Room "The Treatment Room") and straps him to Saxon's own kill table. As Dexter prepares for his [Kill Ritual](/wiki/Kill_Ritual "Kill Ritual"), they discuss the appeal of [Miami](/wiki/Miami "Miami") and the reasons Saxon wants to stay there, while Dexter desires to leave. During their conversation, Dexter realizes that he has more of a drive to be with Hannah than to kill Saxon. Saxon begins to think that he will live, until Dexter tells him that he will perish by an electric chair.

Dexter calls Debra so that she can arrest Saxon, which will make her a hero. Debra arrives as Dexter is leaving to start his new life with [Hannah](/wiki/Hannah_McKay "Hannah McKay") and his son, [Harrison](/wiki/Harrison_Morgan "Harrison Morgan"), as quickly as possible. Debra embraces Dexter goodbye, hoping that he does well in [Argentina](/wiki/Argentina "Argentina").

Unfortunately, Debra was secretly tailed to the hospital by [Deputy Marshal Clayton](/wiki/Max_Clayton "Max Clayton") because of her involvement with Hannah. Clayton enters The Treatment Room and finds Saxon gagged and strapped down. Saxon pretends to be an innocent victim and convinces Clayton to free him. Saxon then grabs one of Dexter's knives and stabs Clayton to death. Debra rushes back into the room, her gun drawn, but Saxon quickly grabs Clayton's pistol and shoots Debra in the gut. Debra fires back, maiming Saxon's arm, but he escapes.

-   "[Remember the Monsters?](/wiki/Episode_812:_Remember_the_Monsters%3F "Episode 812: Remember the Monsters?")"

Saxon steals a truck from a man after knocking him out, and drives around looking for a place where he can get his lacerated arm healed. He finds [a veterinarian](/wiki/Dr._Turner "Dr. Turner") and forces him to stitch up his wound by holding him at gunpoint. He decides that he wants to kill Debra once and for all, and so goes to [Miami Central Hospital](/wiki/Miami_Central_Hospital "Miami Central Hospital") where she is being treated. Being a wanted man, he creates a scene by cutting out the vet's tongue (whom he has held hostage) and letting him loose in the hospital. This allows him to move in without trouble as he searches for Debra.

Dexter senses his presence based on the event that just took place, picks up a fork to kill Saxon before he can further harm Debra. However, the police, led by Lieutenant [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista "Angel Batista"), are prepared for Saxon's arrival and arrest him on the spot.

## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOliver_Saxon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   The events of [Season Eight](/wiki/Season_Eight "Season Eight") take place in 2012.
-   Saxon is the one Main Antagonist responsible for killing the most major recurring characters in a season, including a **Main Character** (and deuteragonist of the series). The accolade of killing a Main Character also goes to **[Lila West](/wiki/Lila_West "Lila West")** with **[James Doakes](/wiki/James_Doakes "James Doakes")** and **[Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell")** with **[Rita Morgan](/wiki/Rita_Morgan "Rita Morgan")**, below is a list of major deaths Saxon is responsible for:
    -   [Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan") - shot in the hip, reduced to vegetative state and taken off life support by her brother
    -   [Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel") - Neck slit
    -   [Zach Hamilton](/wiki/Zach_Hamilton "Zach Hamilton") - Back of skull removed, killed by procedure.
    -   [Max Clayton](/wiki/Max_Clayton "Max Clayton") - U.S. Marshall who Saxon stabs in the chest by manipulating him into releasing Saxon from Dexter's table.
-   Despite the prominence of his actions in **[Season Eight](/wiki/Season_Eight "Season Eight")** and overall impact on Dexter's life, he has yet to be directly mentioned in **[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")** other than in reference to Debra's fate, the same as **[Dexter: New Blood](/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood")**. In contrast whenever Rita's death is mentioned it is often accompanied by the fact that the Trinity Killer (or Arthur Mitchell by name) was responsible as well as twice alluding to the event and showing John Lithgow each time.
-   He is one of few main antagonists to not appear to Dexter as a hallucination, along with [Jordan Chase](/wiki/Jordan_Chase "Jordan Chase"), [Travis Marshall](/wiki/Travis_Marshall "Travis Marshall") and [Isaak Sirko](/wiki/Isaak_Sirko "Isaak Sirko")/[Maria LaGuerta](/wiki/Maria_LaGuerta "Maria LaGuerta"). Isaak's actor Ray Stevenson unfortunately passed away in 2023 which would prevent his reprisal but all other actors are alive and well.  
    This may be due to [Clyde Phillips](/wiki/Clyde_Phillips "Clyde Phillips") choosing to not use much of anything from Seasons 5 to 8 outside of key events, with only a few points referenced in his sequel shows: Debra's death (outside of showing the gunshot wound it doesn't directly mention Saxon), Hannah leaving with Harrison to Argentina (setting up the premise of him seeking Dexter in New Blood) and Dexter faking his death while taking Debra out to sea on the boat (conversation with Batista in the hospital following waking from his coma).  
    Regardless of this fact [Travis Marshall](/wiki/Travis_Marshall "Travis Marshall") still has a place in [Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection") though this is not directly mentioned in the show.
-   Saxon is one of few prolific killers to not have any trophies or evidence in [Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection"), though other main antagonists such as [Brian Moser](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser"), [Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell") and [Travis Marshall](/wiki/Travis_Marshall "Travis Marshall") all have spots within the collection.
-   Saxon is considered a perfect psychopath by Dexter, saying that had he lived his own life free of family and attachments he too might be like Saxon. As such he's a dark parallel to the type of person Dexter became - while he's able to lie and trick people through his emotional displays, he's as cold as Dexter is in reality though he is driven by rage - not an element present in Dexter's need to kill.
-   There are some parallels with Arthur Mitchell, [Season Four](/wiki/Season_Four "Season Four")'s main antagonist - the infamous Trinity Killer:
    -   Both killers have long, successful careers - although Trinity has killed far more victims.
    -   Saxon offers Dexter a "way out", much in the way that Arthur did. Both killers know who Dexter is and confront him - they both desire to go their separate ways with no need for further bloodshed but Dexter refuses both offers - then fails to finish off both killers in time.
    -   Due to Dexter's actions, both killers are responsible for the murders of the two most important women in his life: Rita Morgan (Arthur) and Debra Morgan (Saxon). As such both killers are murdered post-humorously from their victims, though Dexter was only aware with Saxon.
-   There are also some parallels to Brian Moser, [Season One](/wiki/Season_One "Season One")'s main antagonist - the Ice Truck Killer:
    -   Both killers play a cat and game mouse with Dexter - although Dexter's demeanor by [Season Eight](/wiki/Season_Eight "Season Eight") has dropped to where he doesn't draw a deep fascination with the killings. Though this may also be due to the fact that Dexter felt an attachment to the killer that he didn't understand (as they were brothers).
    -   Both killers are around Dexter's age and build
    -   Both killers reveal themselves half-way through the season, taking on a boyfriend role of someone in Dexter's life (Debra for Brian and Cassie for Saxon, albeit the latter was simply his neighbor).
    -   Both killers broke into Dexter's home at different points.
    -   Similar mannerisms and polite attitude holding back a significant amount of rage tied to their mother.
    -   Both killers have recorded their murders, though Brian did it as part of a game with the police...Saxon's recordings acted as a secondary trophy outside of the brain portions he sent to his mother.
    -   Both killers have stolen the identity of someone else, although it's unknown if Saxon was responsible for killing the original person. Brian assumed the name of [Rudy Cooper (plumber)](/wiki/Rudy_Cooper_\(plumber\) "Rudy Cooper (plumber)") after murdering him while Daniel Vogel assumed the name of Oliver Saxon (child) - a child said to have died from a structure fire, smoke inhalation in Oxford, United Kingdom. Given the fact that Daniel was responsible for a fire that killed seven children, it's possible the original Saxon is one of these boys.
    -   Both killers were driven by rage to kill [Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan"), throwing away caution and a chance to escape to track her down to a location where Dexter or others could stop them. Only Saxon was successful in killing Debra, albeit through a vegetative state after she had been shot by him prior.
-   According to [Thomas Matthews](/wiki/Thomas_Matthews "Thomas Matthews"), his [mother](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel") had moved back to [Miami](/wiki/Miami "Miami") from England one year ago (in 2011). Since Saxon had moved to Miami in 2008, he had lived there for three years prior to his mother's return. ("[A Beautiful Day](/wiki/Episode_801:_A_Beautiful_Day "Episode 801: A Beautiful Day")")
-   Saxon was the only main antagonist that fully destroyed Dexter's life, as well as ending his reign as The Bay Harbor Butcher. [Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell") partially destroyed Dexter's life by killing Rita, but Dexter was able to recover from her death.
    -   However, _[New Blood](/wiki/Dexter:_New_Blood "Dexter: New Blood")_ showed that Dexter was eventually able to recover from Deb's death and eventually began killing again, albeit in Iron Lake instead of Miami.
    -   This extends further when he's "resurrected" in **[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")** and has now resumed his killing in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")**. In addition his name has been restored officially, allowing Dexter to start anew as he pleases without hiding behind an alias.
-   [Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell"), [Travis Marshall](/wiki/Travis_Marshall "Travis Marshall"), and Oliver Saxon were the only main antagonists to have killed their first victim when they were children. Saxon killed his brother when he was fourteen and then burned seven children alive when he was seventeen. At a young age, Travis Marshall killed his parents in a deliberate car accident. Saxon and Travis, having already killed a family member, also killed the remaining member of their family later in their lives. (Travis killed his [sister](/wiki/Lisa_Marshall "Lisa Marshall"); Saxon killed his mother). (While he was in school, [Isaak Sirko](/wiki/Isaak_Sirko "Isaak Sirko") pushed his music teacher down the stairs, but it's unknown if he died.)
-   Like [George King](/wiki/George_King "George King"), Saxon displayed a polite, respectful, and well-mannered attitude while in the public.
-   Oliver Saxon is the only character in the series to be a [serial killer](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer") and also a mass murderer.
-   The murder of Oliver Saxon was the first one committed by Dexter to be witnessed by a member of [Miami Metro Homicide](/wiki/Miami_Metro_Homicide "Miami Metro Homicide") (other than Debra). [Joey Quinn](/wiki/Joey_Quinn "Joey Quinn") and [Detective Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista "Angel Batista") watched the security footage, while Batista couldn't comprehend what happened, Quinn believed Saxon was killed in self-defense and Batista went along with it.
    -   Despite the fact that this murder happened in front of both of them, this killing was never mentioned by Dexter or Batista in **[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")**.
-   Saxon was the most personal adversary of Dexter since he was responsible for the deaths of both [Evelyn Vogel](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel "Evelyn Vogel") and [Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan"). Arthur Mitchell also killed someone close to Dexter - his wife, [Rita Morgan](/wiki/Rita_Morgan "Rita Morgan").
-   Saxon was the only main antagonist to be killed by Dexter in a confrontation. Dexter did kill a few others in this manner before (such as [George King](/wiki/George_King "George King"), [Stan Liddy](/wiki/Stan_Liddy "Stan Liddy"), [Andrew Briggs](/wiki/Andrew_Briggs "Andrew Briggs"), etc.), but they were lesser antagonists.
-   The Brain Surgeon is the fourth serial killer to not be immediately identified, following the example of [The Ice Truck Killer](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser"), [The Skinner](/wiki/George_King "George King"), and [The Phantom Arsonist](/wiki/Joe_Jensen "Joe Jensen"). The Trinity Killer was identified, though he remained nameless until halfway through the fourth season.
-   He is the second main antagonist to play the 'cat and mouse game" with Dexter (the first being The Ice Truck Killer).
-   Saxon is the only antagonist to immediately fake his death, doing so in [S8E3](/wiki/Episode_803:_What%27s_Eating_Dexter_Morgan%3F "Episode 803: What's Eating Dexter Morgan?") by framing [Lyle Sussman](/wiki/Lyle_Sussman "Lyle Sussman") as The Brain Surgeon, making it appear as suicide. This threw off the police; however, Dexter and Vogel knew that he was still alive until after they killed Yates whom they assumed was The Brain Surgeon. This resolution ended when [Zach Hamilton](/wiki/Zach_Hamilton "Zach Hamilton") was found dead, killed in a similar manner as the previous Brain Surgeon victims.
-   He is one of the most elusive and mysterious killers in the series.
-   All but one of his victims were killed prior to having their skulls sawed into for the postmortem operation. [Zach Hamilton](/wiki/Zach_Hamilton "Zach Hamilton") was alive and conscious when his brain was sawed into which caused his death.

## Similarities With Brian\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOliver_Saxon%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   Oliver Saxon and [Brian Moser](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser")'s true identities were not revealed during their initial appearances.
-   Both were highly intelligent individuals, and appeared as charming, successful men.
-   They toyed with the police, while playing a cat and mouse game with Dexter.
-   Both took the name of a deceased person as an alias (Brian became 'Rudy Cooper' and Daniel became 'Oliver Saxon').
-   Both characters were institutionalized during their youth. Brian was released at age 21, while Daniel escaped at age 17 by faking his own death.
-   The moment when Brian sat down with Dexter at the table in [their mother's](/wiki/Laura_Moser "Laura Moser") house was similar to the moment when Saxon sat down with his mother at her [home](/wiki/Evelyn_Vogel%27s_House "Evelyn Vogel's House").
-   Both Brian and Oliver dumped their victims in public locations. However, Brian fully dismembered his victims, whereas Oliver only removed a piece of their brains.
-   They both set up one of their victims to look like a killer. Brian framed [Tony Tucci](/wiki/Tony_Tucci "Tony Tucci") and Saxon framed [Lyle Sussman](/wiki/Lyle_Sussman "Lyle Sussman") (although in Tucci's case, [Debra](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan") and [Angel](/wiki/Angel_Batista "Angel Batista") always believed him to be innocent).
-   At one point, they were both thought to be someone else. [The Ice Truck Killer](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser") was presumed to be [Neil Perry](/wiki/Neil_Perry "Neil Perry") and the Brain Surgeon was suspected to be [A.J. Yates](/wiki/A.J._Yates "A.J. Yates").
-   They both attempted to reconnect with a long lost relative. Brian wanted to establish a bond with his brother, Dexter, while Oliver left grisly messages for his mother, Evelyn Vogel.
-   They both dated someone who was close to Dexter. Oliver dated his neighbor, [Cassie Jollenston](/wiki/Cassie_Jollenston "Cassie Jollenston"), and Brian dated Debra. They each tried to kill their 'romantic interest.' Oliver was indirectly successful, but Brian was not.
-   Both killed a parent. Brian killed his (not confirmed) [father](/wiki/Joe_Driscoll "Joe Driscoll") and Oliver killed his mother.
-   Like Brian, Oliver could vaguely be seen as a brother to Dexter. While he was Vogel's bio son, Dexter was considered to be her 'spiritual son.' Oliver even told Dexter, "Mother chose the wrong son...again," as he cut Vogel's throat.
-   Each broke into [Dexter's apartment](/wiki/Dexter%27s_Apartment "Dexter's Apartment") to kill someone (Oliver tried to kill Dexter, and Brian tried to kill Debra). Both instances took place in Dexter's bedroom.
-   Both were strapped onto their own kill tables by Dexter, but Dexter chose to spare Oliver so that Debra could him turn him into the police department. This proved to be a big mistake, as Debra was subsequently shot in the gut by Oliver.
-   Both Oliver and Brian used an abandoned hospital to commit crimes. Brian used [one](/wiki/Angel_of_Mercy_Hospital "Angel of Mercy Hospital") to hold Tucci captive (and amputate him), while Oliver used another for murdering people. In Brian's case, he was hoping to trigger memories for Dexter.
-   Both were killed by trauma to their necks. Brian had his throat slashed, and Oliver's neck was stabbed with a pen.
-   Both were killed by Dexter because of [Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan"). Brian was killed to protect her, and Oliver was killed primarily in revenge because he shot her.
-   Both Brian and Saxon were murdered by Dexter and then left for Miami Metro authorities. Brian was set up to look as if he committed suicide using his own [Modus Operandi](/wiki/M.O. "M.O."). Oliver's murder was clearly seen on camera, but Dexter had incited Oliver to stab him first so that he could kill Oliver in apparent self-defense.
