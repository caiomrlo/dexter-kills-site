**Ronald Schmidt:** **The Dark Passenger Killer**

**Ronald Schmidt** aka **Red** (Referred to by the Media as **The Dark Passenger** and **"The Imposter"** by **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")**) is a major antagonist in the **[Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime")** series _**[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")**._

Ronald is a New York serial killer whom stalks and kills [rideshare drivers](/wiki/UrCar "UrCar") throughout **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")**. He's responsible for the deaths of at least twelve drivers and is actively hunting more victims. He is among a selection of **[Serial Killers](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer")** invited by **[Charley Brown](/wiki/Charley_Brown "Charley Brown")** to meet **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** including **[Mia Lapierre](/wiki/Mia_Lapierre "Mia Lapierre")**, [**Lowell Sloane**](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane"), **[Gareth Pike](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")**, and **[Al Jolly](/wiki/Al_Jolly "Al Jolly")**.

Before he can accept the invitation however, his activities catch the attention of **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan")**. Due to his moniker resembling that of the Dexter's personal moniker for his [**need to kill**](/wiki/Dark_Passenger "Dark Passenger") and his "resurrection" as a serial killer, Red becomes **Dexter's First Kill in New York City** - he also assumes (or reclaims) his identity as **The Dark Passenger** and **[pretends to be Red](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt_\(alias\) "Ronald Schmidt (alias)")** to infiltrate Prater's group.

## Description\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Ronald Schmidt is a middle aged Caucasian man with short light brown and hazel eyes. He can often be seen wearing a grey jacket over a black hoodie over a baseball cap and a dark shirt, dark blue jeans and boots. His hood is referred to as a **Camera-Shy Hoodie** that conceals his face from cameras and he is often seen with a silver backpack featuring his company's logo.

## Personality\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Ronald appears to be quiet man that keeps to himself - this solitude extends online where he lacks any kind of online presence (even from online record databases): "a ghost" as Dexter puts it and even his profile at his work lacks a photo in comparison to all of his colleagues. Based on his home he's protective by using a 4-digit keypad lock compared to the conventional keys of most apartments. Despite that security measure he does not have any cameras inside of his home when checked by Charley during her investigation. The interior of his home lacks any photos or artwork on the walls but it would appear he does enjoy some finer things in life as he has a high-end PC at home. He appears to be a vegan considering his fridge contains a pint of "Vegan Ice Cream" (Vanilla flavored). According to Emily at [Bridge Data Network](/wiki/Bridge_Data_Network "Bridge Data Network"), Ronald is thorough at his job, although her tone of voice while talking about him suggests that Ronald's own colleagues don't like him.

In reality he is a dangerous serial killer with a personal vendetta. Based on his interactions with his victims he has a sadistic joy in torturing the drivers, toying with them on whether or not he plans to kill them - all while repeatedly tightening the fiber-wire around their necks cutting into the flesh. He often takes note of the family photos these drivers hang on their dashboards - questioning his prey while forcing their head to look towards the photos. He asks them who will miss them when they're gone...and questions them about their family's names, if they're good people...and then his sadism turns to rage as he brings up his father. The reason for this rage comes from his father's death after ride-share drivers moved into the market (effectively bankrupting his father). He threw himself into the river after his debts became so high and one of the dredgers sliced off his head - which bore a deep hatred inside of Red as he became an adult. At some point he lost himself in it - not caring that they were innocents with families and even took joy in that fact - considering that to him ride-share drivers took his father away...so he's taking them away from their family.

In his brief encounter with Dexter, he has a cold expressionless look to his face (as Dexter says he was in "kill mode") and chooses to keep moving. He looks back to see Dexter's still focused on him and immediately heads into the subway and boards a train, having noticed Dexter make his way down into the subway after him before the train vanishes. Despite this fact he doesn't recognize Dexter when he steps into his [UrCar](/wiki/UrCar "UrCar") and remains confused by many of Dexter's quips before and while he's on the kill table. He's quick to open up about his hatred of ride-share drivers for what he blames his father's death on, but Dexter shuts him down, saying they had nothing to do with it - they were all innocent. When it comes to the moniker he was adorned (Dark Passenger)...Red can't even begin to comprehend what Dexter's talking about as he prepares to kill him. At first he believed he was maybe the one who left the invitation in his house with his trophies but once he begins talking about his own needs...Red, like so many before him, is just left confused and panicked for his life.

## Background\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Ronald Schmidt was born on **October 28th, 1976** in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")** to his parents, Mr. and Mrs. Schmidt. Growing up he also had a brother and he remarks to one of his victims that he was "a good kid too, me and my brother". His father spent his life savings on a **Taxi Medallion**, which eventually would lose all of its value when **Rideshare Drivers** populated the city. With his father being in heavy debt, he threw himself into the East River and he was found by one of the dredgers (a powerful boat equipped with a special machine for excavating the bottom of bodies of water - i.e. harbors, rivers, lakes) searching for him, which had sliced off his head. Ronald spent the rest of his life blaming ride-share drivers for his family's misfortunes and eventually he became a serial killer who targeted them. It's unknown what happened with his brother though he may be alive.

Relationship wise it would appear he was never married and did not appear to have any kids, living a secluded life as he went from one Tech job to the next eventually landing a cyber security technician role in the **[Bridge Data Network](/wiki/Bridge_Data_Network "Bridge Data Network")** in January, 2018, which gives him access to technology which helps shield his identity from cameras while he kills his victims. When he finally decided to enact his vendetta he murdered seven ride share drivers prior to **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")'s** arrival in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")**.

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

One night in **New York City** a **[UrCar Driver](/wiki/UrCar "UrCar")** known as **[Chike](/wiki/Chike "Chike")** picks up a man claiming to be his customer - little did he realize that it was Ronald Schmidt, intent on taking his life. Ronald moved behind him and puts fiber-wire around Chike's neck - telling him where to drive while also tormenting him with questions such as "who will miss you when you're gone?". Chike, intent on staying alive, takes advantage of a green light to suddenly hit the breaks and pull the release on his eat to loosen the chain around his neck. Schmidt is disorientated by the sudden stop and Chike slides under the chain (his ear being slashed by the wire), exiting the car into the busy street. Given the commotion Chike causes by stopping in the middle of traffic, he panics and books it from the cab with his backpack. Chike doesn't report the encounter to the police due to his visa having expired years before.

### **DEXTER: Resurrection - Season One**\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[A Beating Heart...](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_101:_A_Beating_Heart... "Resurrection Episode 101: A Beating Heart...")"

Schmidt is seen exiting his apartment complex during the daytime, placing his ear pods in before making his way to work. Little did he know that a mysterious woman named **[Charley Brown](/wiki/Charley_Brown "Charley Brown")** was watching him - waiting for her chance to enter the complex and break into his home. When she arrives at his door she finds that it's locked with a 4-digit keypad, which causes her to put away her intended lockpick and dig out a gadget that determines the code (while also unlocking the door after success). When she enters his home it can be seen as pretty barren, lacking any photos or paintings on any of the walls. Other than a television set and likely couch for watching as well as his computer desk and high-end PC he lives a minimalistic lifestyle.

Charley's objective is to confirm that Schmidt is a **[Serial Killer](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer")** by locating his **[Trophies](/wiki/Trophies_of_Killers "Trophies of Killers")**, starting first with a wall mounted air conditioning unit (similar to the way **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan")** stored his **[Blood Slide Boxes](/wiki/Blood_Slide_Boxes "Blood Slide Boxes")** back in **[Miami](/wiki/Miami "Miami")**. When the spot yields no results she begins to dig through the rest of the house, being thorough and checking all potential places where a serial killer may hide their trophies - drawers, cabinets, even in the freezer and an ice cream pint. She even goes so far as to unscrew the bathroom mirror-cabinet and check behind it. Eventually her investigation leads to a peculiar set of square markings on the carpet - which she connects with nearby crates holding up a vase with a plant in it. She uses the crates to stand up and lift a panel in the ceiling which holds a zip-lock binder that holds several plastic sheets that contain the driver's licenses of his victims. Each victim's photo is marked out with an X in what appears to be blood...Charley remarks that he's a "psycho" and moves on with her next objective: leaving the invitation.

Charley pulls out a box full of cash (what appears to be five stacks of $10,000 though it could be upwards of $100,000) as well as an envelope with an invitation to meet with her boss - **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")**. She places this box in the hiding spot his trophies sat in and also puts the binder with those trophies on top of it, leaving the apartment while calling Prater to inform him that "the invitation is delivered".

-   "[Camera Shy](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_102:_Camera_Shy "Resurrection Episode 102: Camera Shy")"

One night after **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** arrival in New York, Schmidt approaches a line of UrCar Drivers near a concert that was playing with his hoodie down. He approaches one driver and knocks on his window asking if he's there to pick him up - the driver says that unless he's named Jessica then no. Schmidt moves around a group of people on the next car and then arrives at the third car driven by **[Stanley Touissant](/wiki/Stanley_Touissant "Stanley Touissant")**. Stanley asks if he's "Jack" and Schmidt claims he is, which allows him entry into the back seat of his car - though he makes sure to don his unique hoodie first.

As Stanley drives off and joins the night traffic of New York heading towards "Jack's" destination, he offers him a bottle of water that's available in the back seat and strikes up conversation with him - asking what kind of music he likes. Schmidt says something soothing, which Stanley switches it over to. As the music plays Schmidt looks near the radio and sees photos of Stanley's two children as well as his Taxi and Limousine Commission license. He then addresses Stanley by name, asking where he's from. Stanley smiles saying that he's from **Haiti**, though says that he's been living in New York for many years. Schmidt says that he was born here, lived in New York his whole life and Stanley says "lucky you, mister". Stanley asks about the concert though Stanley remarks that he doesn't know, doesn't like that kind of place - that it's "filled with assholes". Stanley chuckles saying surely they're not all bad but Schmidt tells him "Oh, yeah, they are. You don't remember how it used to be - before it all went to shit". As he says this a chime comes in from Stanley's phone - from the actual "Jack" he was supposed to pick up, which asks where he was and that he was getting further away.

Realizing too late that the man in the cabin with him wasn't actually his customer, Schmidt quickly moves behind him and wraps fiber-wire around his neck - holding it tightly as blood trickles down from the wounds it's creating. He tells him to take a right ahead of them, that they're going to take the **Manhattan Bridge** to a new location. He continues to tighten the wire by pulling it towards him, which causes him to start coughing and he starts to ask - in fear - if he's going to kill him. In a sadistic manner Schmidt completes his sentence, acting as if he's not sure - that "it all depends". He then says his signature question...

"_**Who...is going to miss you when you're gone?"**_"

Stanley doesn't respond which irritates Schmidt and he grabs him by the jack, turning his head towards the photos on the dashboard. "Is it them?". He says yes, they're his children and they'll miss him - Schmidt asks their names and he tells him they're named **Bethany and Daniel**. Schmidt then asks how old - he tells him that Bethany's five and Daniels' 9, Ronald wondering if they're good kids...and Stanley agrees. With that Ronald says that he was a good kid once, he and his brother though it didn't help them - everything was taken from them "by you fucks" which he utters with anger in his voice. "What do you think that feels like? To lose everything?" and a terrified Stanley says that he doesn't know...which causes Schmidt to say "Well....you're about to find out".

The next day Stanley's car is found near a trainyard where a group of teens are spray painting graffiti. One of the kids climbs up to where the car is and approaches it - noticing the body of Stanley, decapitated with his head laying in his lap. This marks Schmidt's seventh victim.

#### **Dexter's Hunt for "The Imposter"**\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Dexter's arrival in New York City is tied to his son **Harrison Morgan'**s potential first murder - given the manner in which the body of **[Ryan Foster](/wiki/Ryan_Foster "Ryan Foster")** was found. After learning that Harrison works at the **[Empire Hotel](/wiki/Empire_Hotel "Empire Hotel")**, he makes his way inside to investigate the scene to see how his son did it - when suddenly **Detective [Claudette Wallace](/wiki/Claudette_Wallace "Claudette Wallace")** and her partner **Detective [Melvin Oliva](/wiki/Melvin_Oliva "Melvin Oliva")** arrive to give the room a once over while she displays her impressive deduction skills. Dexter hides in a nearby closet as manager **[Stefan Pike](/wiki/Stefan_Pike "Stefan Pike")** lets them into the room he brings up the death of a rideshare driver the previous day and asks why they can't just investigate that right now - to which Claudette says "that is not my case". While he was in earshot of this murder, it didn't hold particular interest for him at the time.

That is until following an extended stay in the hotel making sure his son covered his tracks Dexter's truck is towed - with the driver refusing to accept a payoff or even give him a ride to the impound lot: telling him to try a UrCar Driver. Dexter's driver **[Blessing Kamara](/wiki/Blessing_Kamara "Blessing Kamara")** arrives, using caution vs. what Stanley had done and asks Dexter his name rather than call it out. When he confirms and lets him in, he lightens up revealing his good nature and friendly demeanor. As the two talk he eventually apologizes for having to be cautious when picking him up and he tells him that "as you know we have a serial killer who's taking out rideshare drivers". This immediately catches Dexter's attention and he focuses on his story - the killer has claimed seven victims (with the recent death of Stanley) so far and that they call him **The Dark Passenger**. Dexter grimaces at the name - personally feeling that he should have trademarked his alter ego. "A scary name for a scary man. He saws off their heads!" Blessing tells him and that the police don't seem to have a clue, though despite this tale he remarks his own courage by saying that he'll never leave New York.

From this moment on Dexter's own Dark Passenger called to him - he felt the need to hunt and he began investigating the killer. When he checked a newspaper it showed the following information alongside a photograph of the hooded killer:

"_**NYPD are on high alert following the grisly murder of another ride-share driver by the suspected serial killer, who has gained infamy under the alias "Dark Passenger". Late last night, the unidentified figure reportedly attacked and killed the UrCar driver in what law enforcement has described as a "calculated act of terror". According to UrCar, the killer was a passenger picked up through a routine ride request.  
A grisly photograph released by authorities show the Dark passenger, dressed in a hoodie that concealed their identity, brandishing a chain-like saw around the driver's neck. The victim's name has not been released at this time and the death remains under investigation.  
The incident is the latest in a string of similar attacks attributed to the Dark passenger. UrCar is urging drivers and passengers to remain vigilant and report suspicious activity to NYPD immediately.**_"

Another page reveals the photographs of the seven victims and a tidbit about **[Tony Hernandez](/wiki/Tony_Hernandez "Tony Hernandez")** a reported father of three. "He was a family man and loved his little girls. Everything he did, he did for them." with his wife being devastated - wanting justice for her husband's death. NYPD remarks that "these are everyday people simply trying to make a living and now their families are left to pick up the pieces.". Needing to gather more information about the killer he decided to take Blessing up on his offer for the party he was hosting - so that he can meet with his friend, **[Chike](/wiki/Chike "Chike")**. After Blessing introduces Dexter to everyone he brings him over to Chike and Dexter, intending to be a UrCar Driver himself, asks him about the incident - for the "safety" of himself and his passengers. Chike goes over the story and describes him as a white man, same build, same kind of hair as Dexter. He plays this off and says "well I don't wear a hoodie!" before he's offered a chance to look at the video of the entire altercation - how the man entered his car, wrapped a sharp object around his throat and told him where to go.

Dexter notes how they can't see his face for some reason and Blessing's daughter **[Joy Kamara](/wiki/Joy_Kamara "Joy Kamara")** informs him that he's wearing a **Camera-Shy Hoodie**. The hood contains infrared lights aimed at the killer's face which are invisible to the human eye but creates a washed out image over the face completely blocking it out. As the video continues Chike tells him that he did manage to escape by slamming on the brakes in traffic to disorient the killer and slipped out from the wire by releasing his chair though it slashed his right ear. Dexter tells him that he was very smart and brave, which Chike says he just didn't want to die. As Blessing calls for more food and everyone centers around the table Dexter quickly sends the video to his own phone before handing it back to Chike.

With this video Dexter's able to make out the logo on Schmidt's backpack and researches the company - learning about their field technicians and one in particular (Schmidt himself) sticking out in particular with no profile picture. Amidst his discussion with **[Harry Morgan](/wiki/Harry_Morgan/Resurrection "Harry Morgan/Resurrection")** who keeps calling him Dark Passenger, Dexter responds that he refuses to call him that - instead referring to this killer as **The Imposter**. As he looks up his name online he finds nothing - effectively making Schmidt a ghost. He then heads to the **[Bridge Data Network](/wiki/Bridge_Data_Network "Bridge Data Network")** building waiting until he possibly spots Ronald.

Eventually Schmidt does leave from the main entrance and Dexter pursues him, moving ahead along the sidewalk to fake taking a selfie, then turns around snapping photos of Schmidt whom has already put on his hoodie. Dexter's unable to catch his face in the shots and realizes he's gone into "kill mode". Schmidt approaches a driver who asks if he's "Tommy" and again he claims he's the customer - except before he can enter the back seat Dexter approaches quickly and tells him "Hey! Don't take my UrCar". Schmidt looks at him with an empty expression and says "sorry" before he turns to walk off. As he moves down the sidewalk he looks back to see Dexter still watching him then proceeds to head down into the subway.

He descends the stairs quickly, entering the subway car as Dexter gives pursuit. While he tries to make his way down the stairs however someone bumps into his chest (where he had been shot by his son and was still recovering), which causes him immense pain and he has to stop moving while Schmidt looks at him from the train car. He lowers his head as the train rolls away...and Dexter remarks to himself that:

"_**New York may be a big town, but it's not big enough for two Dark Passengers. Ronald Schmidt, my kill table is ready for you."**_"

-   "[Backseat Driver](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_103:_Backseat_Driver "Resurrection Episode 103: Backseat Driver")"

Dexter uses a website to run a background check on Schmidt and locates his apartment. Dexter later sets himself up as Ronald's next victim to lure him out, wearing a metal band around his neck for protection and arms himself with a syringe of [M99](/wiki/Etorphine "Etorphine"). However, Dexter drops the syringe by accident, leaving him vulnerable to Schmidt. Dexter eventually manages to reach the syringe and knock Schmidt out.

Dexter sets up a [Kill Room](/wiki/Kill_Room "Kill Room") in Schmidt's own apartment, but chooses to forgo showing Schmidt his trophies as he thinks that the killer would get a sick thrill out of that. Schmidt reveals that his motive is his father's suicide after rideshare drivers took business away from taxi drivers and mistakes Dexter as the person who had left the invitation to a dinner for serial killers. Dexter stabs Schmidt in the chest, dismembers his body, and burns the pieces in [a furnace](/wiki/Dexter%27s_Favorite_Furnace "Dexter's Favorite Furnace") in [a building](/wiki/Abandoned_Factory "Abandoned Factory") in a part of New York City that rideshare drivers and the NYPD won't go near. Deciding to attend the dinner in Schmidt's place, Dexter retrieves his arm and cuts off Schmidt's thumb as he will need the killer's thumbprint to get in.

#### **Impersonating "Red"**\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Harrison is later questioned by Charley on Prater's orders, but comes up with a story to explain his relation to Ronald Schmidt by telling a version of the truth with Ronald Schmidt replacing Dexter Morgan. Charley is highly amused to learn that the Dark Passenger Killer who targets rideshare drivers is in fact a rideshare driver himself.

However, Dexter's impersonation of Red is finally exposed when Batista approaches Prater and reveals Dexter's true identity to him and Charley. Confronting Dexter, Prater deduces that he had killed the real Ronald Schimdt and stolen his identity.

-   "[And Justice For All...](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_110:_And_Justice_For_All... "Resurrection Episode 110: And Justice For All...")"

Dexter uses Red's thumbprint one last time to try to escape Prater's manor, only to be forced back when Prater kidnaps Harrison. After subduing Prater, Dexter gives his son Red's thumbprint to escape via the elevator while Dexter cuts off Prater's thumb after killing him and uses that instead.

With his murder of Prater, Dexter abandoned his Ronald Schmidt alias for good.

### Motive\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

According to Red his father invested all of his life's savings into a Taxi Medallion, which lost its value once Rideshare Drivers started appearing within the city. Before long his father was in immense debt and he chose to kill himself by jumping in the East River, and he was found by one of the dredgers (a powerful boat equipped with a special machine for excavating the bottom of bodies of water) searching for him, which had sliced off his head. Following his father's death, he grew an immense hatred for Rideshare Drivers that he would eventually turn into his reason for killing. He enjoyed torturing his victims emotionally; forcing them to talk about their families that would miss them. He left the headless bodies for the families and neighbors to find - similar to how his dad's headless body was found.

### Modus Operandi\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

He approaches their vehicle wearing a dark baseball cap and a hoodie (that remains down until he's ready to enter their car). Faking to be whatever customer they're waiting for, he benefits when a driver calls out their customer's name and he claims to be them, allowing easy entry into the back seat. Depending on how he chooses, he either has a conversation with the driver or moves behind them immediately, wrapping a Gigli saw around their neck. Any onboard cameras are useless due to a **Camera-Shy Hoodie** that he wears which contains infrared lasers pointed at his face which distort his face entirely. Once he has them under his control he directs them where to drive - leading them to a kill site of his choosing, at least one of them being somewhere over the Manhattan Bridge.

He enjoys tormenting his captive, toying with their hopes when asked if he's going to kill them. He claims "it all depends"...though he always planned to murder them no matter what. He takes note of family photos in the car if the driver has any - asking them the question "Who is going to miss you when you're gone?". He'll demand information about the driver's family - names of kids by example and if they were good. On one instance he connected this with his childhood, what happened to him and his brother - how everything was taken from them and how he was going to take everything from his victims. Once they reach the kill spot he uses the fiber wire to saw off his victim's heads leaving a bloody mess in the car while the victim's head falls into their own lap. He then leaves the area - after acquiring their driver's license and adds it to his collection.

According to Dexter his kill cycle appeared to be every two or three weeks however after the death of Stanley, his urge to kill has gotten stronger and he already planned on killing again.

### Trophies\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Ronald keeps a black, zip-up binder that holds his trophies. This binder is stored in a ceiling panel in his living room, which can be reached easily with a set of crates he keeps near the window (that normally has a plant ontop of it). Standing on the crates and pushing up on the panel reveals the binder - which contains the words "Sorcery: The Covenant" divided by a symbol and at the bottom "Collector's Book".

Inside the book are pages containing four driver's licenses each for all of his current victims. On each license photo he's marked an X on the picture - possibly in blood from the victim. While it's believed he only murdered 7 people prior to Stanley, each of the licenses is removed and pinned to a plastic sheet revealing 12 total (which would mean 3 sheets of four licenses).

![Ronald Schmidt's Trophies](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)

Dexter presents one of these licenses as an offering when he arrives for the gathering. Prater remarks that it's his first victim **[Tyler Day](/wiki/Tyler_Day "Tyler Day")** and feels honored to be given it - where it's placed in a trophy collection below **Gemini'**s trophies. This, along with the recent addition of **The Canton Clubber'**s nail bat, make two post-humorous additions to Prater's collection albeit without his awareness (...yet).

## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRonald_Schmidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   According to **[Charley](/wiki/Charley "Charley")** in **[Call Me Red](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_104:_Call_Me_Red "Resurrection Episode 104: Call Me Red")** it took her three weeks to find him - that his nonexistent internet presence made him rather elusive for her and Prater. Dexter accomplished this in three days by studying [Chike](/wiki/Chike "Chike")'s dashcam footage and later utilizing a website he learned of from **[Constance Kamara](/wiki/Constance_Kamara "Constance Kamara")** as she used it to look Dexter up.
-   To his credit, he is one of few serial killers to have Dexter in his grasp - however Dexter's prep time allows him to avoid any injury when he faces Red.
    -   [Dexter](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") was well aware of his M.O. and took precautions when he watched Blessing utilize some metallic band to prevent rats from entering his building. Dexter wears the metallic band under a thick black turtle neck sweater so that it completely hides the protective layer of metal. Despite the fact that Red had his serrated fiber wire around his neck, it was unable to cut into his body even when Dexter took a risk by overpowering him later on (without the band it would have killed him).
    -   Had Dexter not taken the acupuncture from **Joy Blessing**, the aches that were hindering him before could have cost him his life if Red found an opportunity to bring his weapon up to Dexter's face. Thanks to the acupuncture therapy he was able to utilize his overwhelming strength to prevent Red from doing any further damage with the wire around his neck until he got his needle.
-   During the story arc involving Red, Dexter notes various similarities between the two of them and the life he once led. Below are several he mentioned as well as some that not stated:
    -   Both are middle aged Caucasian serial killers with reddish brown hair and similar facial features, at least as far as Dexter's observations go.
    -   Both men are intelligent, with Dexter's expertise being in forensics which help cover up his kills. With Red being a technician in the digital era, his use of tech allows him to avoid being captured by security cameras or rideshare cameras, which keeps his identity hidden.
    -   Both men live(d) a secluded life inside of a one bedroom apartment with thick walls, no cameras and privacy with their homes being kept in similar, clean and arranged conditions.
    -   Charley during her hunt for Schmidt's trophies immediately tries a wall-unit air conditioner: the same place that Dexter hid his blood slides at his apartment in Miami. This same first location comes to mind during Dexter's own search however Harry feels that's a little too "coincidental" if so.
    -   Ronald marks an X in the victim's blood on each driver's license photo, similar to Dexter's use of blood slides containing his victim's blood.
    -   Both serial killers kept a neat, tidy collection of their trophies in containers hidden in spaces that took two former Special Forcers veterans (Doakes and Charley) a good while to discover.
    -   Ronald's use of a garrote-like weapon to direct his victims to a kill location mirrors Dexter's method of forcing the choir director **[Mike Donovan](/wiki/Mike_Donovan "Mike Donovan")** to drive to his own kill room from the pilot episode of the original series. However Ronald's was a Gigli saw and used to saw off his victim's necks...though Dexter would use an electrical saw to kill Mike Donovan via decapitation. Ironically Dexter himself is put in the driver's seat by Red, flipping the tables on Dexter's first kill in both the original series and Resurrection - though he prepared for this situation with a special metallic alloy used to prevent the wire from cutting into his neck.
-   Despite their similarities, several glaring differences set Dexter apart from his "Imposter":
    -   Dexter's need to kill was molded by the [Code of Harry](/wiki/Code_of_Harry "Code of Harry"), which meant every person he killed were generally other killers (save for some exceptions). Red's victims were all innocent men who he blamed for his father's death - completely lost in his delusion.
    -   Dexter's ability to recognizes faces far exceeds that of Red - considering their encounter a few nights before, Red had seen Dexter's face clearly at the cab and in the subway station where he disappeared. Despite this fact he doesn't utilize any caution when he goes in for the kill and doesn't seem to recognize Dexter in the car or even on his table.
    -   With his supposed lack of forensic knowledge, the only way Red was able to continue his murder sprees without being caught is due to the more brilliant detectives such as **[Claudette Wallace](/wiki/Claudette_Wallace "Claudette Wallace")** being assigned to cases such as the **Ryan Foster Case**. Had he continued to murder ride-share drivers unchecked (with the number climbing into double digits) it's possible that the case would have landed with someone like her who would be able to deduce his identity via the same method Dexter used (interacting with one of his survivors) or by checking the footage (assuming it wasn't erased by Red) to determine his identity as a Bridge Data Network technician. This is a clear contrast from Dexter who is often methodical and that a body being discovered in the first place is a "mistake". Dexter has a clear ritual and set of rules he has to follow to insure he's not caught - no such caution appeared to be used by Red, who was found out by Prater.
    -   Ronald is vegan unlike Dexter, leading to some rather unappetizing meals for Dexter while he's posing as Ronald.
-   Ronald's murders earned him the title "Dark Passenger" as a hooded serial killer of rideshare drivers. Dark Passenger for the franchise has referred to Dexter's need to kill and the need to kill within others. In something of a comical irritation with someone else having the name, Dexter instead insists on referring to him as "The Imposter".
-   Infamous comedian Garth Owen-Miles auditioned for the role, reimagining the character as a fat, bald chav. Producers laughed after he delivered his monologue, harshly saying it wasn't clear if his chances of getting the role or the manager's job at Droitwich were lower.
-   Despite being invited to join Leon Prater and several other serial killers for a meet and greet, Ronald is not able to attend (due to his death). It's unclear if he intended to accept the invitation considering the offer of selling his trophies would earn him more money.
    -   Dexter, having reclaimed his moniker "The Dark Passenger", assumes Red's identity when he slices off his thumb to verify the fingerprint.
