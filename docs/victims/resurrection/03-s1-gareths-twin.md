**Gareth's Twin a.k.a.****  
The Other Gemini Killer, a Holy Egomaniac.**

**[Gareth Pike](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")'s homonymous identical twin** (who is often referred to as **Gareth 2** by viewers) is a minor antagonist in the first season of the **[Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime")** original series **[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")**.

Alongside his twin brother (who is is often referred to as **Gareth 1** by viewers), he is one of the two **Gemini Killers**.

He is among **[Prater's Serial Killers](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")**, a select group of like-minded individuals that serve as the **Central Antagonists** of **[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")** and the **Third Victim** of **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")** when he is put off by Gemini's arrogance and chooses to target him.

## Description\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

He is a middle aged Caucasian man with short black hair and dark brown eyes, with a pale complexion. His attire consists of a green blazer and green patterned shirt which is tucked into a set of brown pants and black dress shoes.

## Personality\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Unlike the more quiet and reserved [Gareth Pike](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike"), his twin is outspoken, arrogant and egotistical about his intelligence, kills and place on the FBI's Most Wanted list. While Gareth refused to talk about his latest activities, his twin was only too eager to brag to [Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection") and answer all of his questions about the Gemini Killer when Dexter claimed to be a fan who wanted to get to know him better.

Gareth's Twin considers himself to be the smartest person in every room and other serial killers such as the Zodiac Killer to be amateurs in comparison to himself. He also believes that his notoriety means that naming him would get [Mia LaPierre](/wiki/Mia_LaPierre "Mia LaPierre") an immunity deal and that "[Red](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan")" was inspired by his work, showing the extent of his ego.

## Background\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

He and **[his brother](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")** were both born as twins, both being named **Gareth Pike** by their mother.[\[1\]](#cite_note-1) It's unknown what created their need to kill but collectively they created the name **Gemini** - under this name they (acting as one) desired to be the greatest serial killer in history. The two of them would go on to kill at least 32 victims - brothers, sisters, sons and fathers, lovers...in their view the closest you are with someone is in the moments before death.

At some point one of the brothers would meet with **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")**, presenting him photos of their victims. He never revealed the truth about his brother and would go on to fill his collection with 16 photos - with photos of the "moments before" a kill paying double. While his brother seemed to be quieter and more reserved about his enjoyment of the fame, the twin was far more vocal and perhaps had a larger ego than his brother. At some point he would be number one in college, Mensa and grad school before becoming number one on the FBI's Most Wanted list. Gemini has such notoriety that there are four bestsellers and a movie in the works with talks to have one of the Culkin's play Gemini.

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

As the **[10th Annual Gala](/wiki/10th_Annual_Gala "10th Annual Gala")** approaches in March of 2022, Prater hosts his annual dinner party for his **[Serial Killers](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")** and **[Gareth Pike](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")** attends the gathering, where he meets with **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** using the name "Red", as he's assumed the identity of **[Ronald Schmidt](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt "Ronald Schmidt")** - **The Dark Passenger**. Gareth's Twin does not attend this party, though it's currently unknown how they choose which among them attends a particular meeting. At this meeting Dexter meets **[Lowell Sloane](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane")** (whom he later kills) and **[Mia LaPierre](/wiki/Mia_LaPierre "Mia LaPierre")** (whom he later gets arrested) - which dwindle the number of killers down to three remaining (with the secret of Gemini making it four total killers).

He says by the next time they meet everything will be back to normal.

He keeps checking around the corner, likely having already caught a whiff of being followed and he places a "bookmark" in the book, replacing it on the shelf.

When he locates it the chapter it was set in was the Gemini Killer and Dexter sighs "The ego on this guy". An excerpt from the page shown can be viewed below:

"_**  
The Visionary Killer  
The FBI categories the Gemini Killer as a Visionary Killer, as one of the most elusive and terrifying figures in American history. Investigators have struggled to decipher the full scope of the Gemini Killer's methodology. The most unsettling aspect of his murders is the inescapable pattern victims are always killed in pairs. Father and son. Brothers or sisters. Romantic couples. The killer binds them together not just in life, but in death. Their throats are always slashed in a brutal fashion, the wounds deep and deliberate, indicating strength and precision.  
At each crime scene, a chilling signature is left behind - curved smears of blood at the victim's heads and feet. Upon further analysis, these markings were forced to resemble the astrological sign for Gemini, twins holding hands. The deliberate and ritualistic nature of this symbol adds a disturbing layer of meaning to the murders, suggesting that the killer views his work as something more than mere homicide.  
**_"

"_**What's the point in all this spycraft? Who's he meeting that requires a dead drop like he's in some Cold War espionage movie?"  
"An accomplice? A victim?"  
"Well, whoever it is won't be getting the message.**_"

He pockets the ticket while Gemini can be seen behind a book shelf in the distance watching him and then Dexter checks to see if he himself has made it to the "mount rushmore of serial killers" - chuckling to himself when he finds the chapter. "Gareth!- Hey. Welcome to my abode". Dexter offers him a **gin and tonic** with Gemini saying "I'd love a G and T". All while they're talking Dexter prepares the drink, bringing out two glasses and makes use of some of the ice cubes he attained from upstairs to prepare the drinks. After pouring in the gin he moves over to grab a fruit to start cutting up for the drink, after Gemini had remarked about the father and teenage son victims. Dexter asks...

"_**One Question. Who was the note for?"  
"Me."  
"Is it some kind of...Zodiac homage? A way of leaving clues for people to decipher later?"  
"The Zodiac was an amateur."  
"Huh. I figured you were a fan. Yeah, 'cause of all the astrological...didn't you coin the name Gemini yourself?"  
"The name chose me - had nothing to do with-anyone-else"  
"Indulge me with one more question. Why kill in pairs?"  
"This is my gift. They say that we enter this life alone and that's how we leave it. But to die together - that's the ultimate bond. That father is never loved his son more than in the moment before.**_"

Dexter says "Wow"...having already emptied the needle's contents into the right glass, appearing to take it with his right hand as he walks towards Gemini. "You really are smart". He places what appears to be the right glass forward towards Gemini, while he sets him down in his left hand.

"_**Number one in college, number one in Mensa, number one in grad school and now number one on the FBI's serial killer list"  
"For now."  
"You, Red, are number two at best."  
"To meeting your heroes.**_"

The two chuckle as Gemini then swaps glasses with Dexter - having an awareness that he may have done something to his drink. Dexter takes his drink and downs it confidently which gives Gemini the notion to down his - taking first one sip and then another sitting back to smirk at him. Before long however...Gemini starts to feel his body give out and he falls unconscious on the floor, with Dexter doing a spin as he smiles saying "Who's number one now?".

#### Dexter's Third Victim\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Dexter can be seen preparing his scalpel and blood slides as he walks over to seat himself behind Gemini's head, who is laying on the ground in a plastic tarp still in his outfit but his arms and legs duct-taped along with his mouth. Dexter remarks that "this is a view I'm comfortable with" and uses the scalpel to cut into Gemini's left cheek - drawing blood. As Gemini comes to he notices Dexter above him, using a tool to take a droplet of his blood and place it into a plastic blood slide - closing it above Gemini's view. Assuming he knows all about the greats then he can gather who "Red" really is but he still muffles out "Red? Red??" as Dexter slaps his cheek. After seeing the blood slide, he realizes Dexter is the Bay Harbor Butcher and even muffles "You are... You are the Bay Harb-" before his death.

"_**You know what? I don't need to hear any more from Gareth tonight.**_"

Dexter moves to in front of him and Gemini realizes what's about to happen - frantically begging for his life through the duct tape. Dexter raises the blade above his head while Gemini watches the blade come down into his chest, succumbing to the puncture to his heart in seconds.

"_**You're welcome, FBI's Most Wanted list**_"

Before he can savor his death a knock can be heard on his door, with Dexter ripping out the blade as he looks to the door - it's Blessing Kamara wanting to talk with him. Dexter tells him just a moment and quickly puts away all of his tools, the bloodslide, wraps Gareth up in the tarp and drags him to the bathroom where he places him inside the tub. When he closes the shower curtain he accidentally undoes one of the hooks but he doesn't catch it - quickly closing the bathroom door as he lets blessing in. After some discussion and heartfelt tales from his past...Blessing asks if he has an ADVIL to help with his headache.

Dexter, who had gotten lost in the discussion, tells him he has some in his medicine cabinet before he snaps to realizing that he has a body in the bathtub. He tries to tell him he'll get it for him but Blessing makes his way into the bathroom ahead of him, checking the cabinet. Once he retrieves the bottle and closes the cabinet he notices the loose hook on the shower curtain. He moves to adjust it as Dexter watches in anticipation - hoping that he doesn't discover the corpse just on the other side. Thankfully Blessing finishes up in there and leaves, giving Dexter time to do what he needs to.

Once Gemini's body has been cut into 9 pieces and transferred into black bags, he makes his way to the incinerator where he already disposed of **[Ronald Schmidt](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt "Ronald Schmidt")** and **[Lowell Sloane](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane")**, burning his body to ash just like the others.

## Aftermath\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

### The Gemini Killers - Twins\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[Cats and Mouse](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_106:_Cats_and_Mouse "Resurrection Episode 106: Cats and Mouse")"

Dexter had assumed he dealt with Gareth entirely...but underestimated what exactly the Gemini Killer represented. The next day Dexter has breakfast with Harrison, going over how he's going to help his son on his path. Once he departs he gets a call from **[Charley Brown](/wiki/Charley_Brown "Charley Brown")** whom tells him that **[Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** has a surprise for him - to be at Pier 6 in two hours and to bring his trophies as well as his tools. Later that morning **[Mia LaPierre](/wiki/Mia_LaPierre "Mia LaPierre")** is hanging in her cell - an apparent suicide though the man who checked her pulse was paid by Charley to take care of her. This is reported to Dexter as he makes his way to Pier 6 where a helicopter can be seen, Charley and Prater in front of it.

Dexter ponders if they would really kill Mia in prison as he approaches the chopper alongside **[Al Walker](/wiki/Al_Walker "Al Walker")**. "I thought this would be a nice change of pace - just waiting on Gareth" Prater says while Dexter thinks to himself...

"_**Lowell is gone, Mia is dead and Gareth is nine piles of ash in my favorite furnace. I show up, and suddenly these serial killers start dropping like flies - they're gonna realize it's me**_"

Al and Dexter make their way inside the chopper followed by Prater. Charley looks around, trying the phone one more time before entering the chopper herself. Prater looks around saying they'll wait two more minutes and just then from around the corner where Dexter drove in a black SUV rolls in. Dexter's not sure who it could be...until he can see clearly that it's **[Gareth Pike](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")** himself, shocking Dexter to his core while Prater sighs with relief. He makes his way to the chopper, which takes off shortly after he's inside.

"_**Holy Shit - Gemini. How did I not see this coming? The Gemini Killers...Twins. Fuck me**_"

-   "[Course Correction](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_107:_Course_Correction "Resurrection Episode 107: Course Correction")"

While at Prater's retreat, Dexter confronts Gareth, pretending like he had put the pieces together that Gareth couldn't have done it by himself. Gareth reveals that he and his brother had created Gemini together, "the greatest killer to ever terrorize the world." While a lot of people were fans of Gemini, Dexter was the first to figure out their secret. Dexter admits that he had [a serial killer brother](/wiki/Brian_Moser/Resurrection "Brian Moser/Resurrection") too and how he wonders what it would've been like if Dexter and Brian had been able to grow up together. While Gareth can't get into specifics, his and his brother's first rule is never to be seen together, having developed a highly specific system for communication and Dexter admits to having found their dead drop.

"_**I'm curious. They say twins can feel each other's pain. Is that true? Did you feel a sting in your chest last night? Around 8:20?"  
"Why?"  
"Because that's when I stabbed your brother in the chest. Did you feel a hot flash when I threw his body in the furnace? I'm glad that you and your twin found your thing together. Me? I have my own thing. Killing killers.**_"

Gareth furiously attacks Dexter who kills him with a broken wine glass, making it appear to be self-defense to Prater, Charley and Al.

-   "[Touched by an Angel](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_109:_Touched_by_an_Angel "Resurrection Episode 109: Touched by an Angel")"

While telling Harrison about his targeting of Prater's group, Dexter reveals that he had killed the Tattoo Collector and the Gemini Killer.

"_**The Gemini? He -- He's been killing people for years, and you just took care of him?"  
"Them. Twins.**_"

After learning Dexter's true identity from [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection"), Prater mentions that he was Gemini's benefactor and offers to do the same thing for the Bay Harbor Butcher, helping Dexter to get his victims.

## Victims\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother have murdered at least 32 people as based off his trophies in Leon Prater's Trophy Collection. As his photos are of two people each then he's claimed at least 16 pairs of victims though likely has more trophies to his name, as Lowell murdered over 26 and only presented 3 trophies to Prater.

-   The Carpenters
-   The Keller Twins
-   Bianca and Ash Mitchell
-   Michael and Skylar Williams

### Motive\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother consider people dying in pairs to be their gift as it's said that people enter and exit this life alone. Killing people alongside their loved ones creates the ultimate bond and they never love each other more than in that moment.

### Modus Operandi\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother take a pair of related victims such as father and son, brother and sister, or couples and brutally slash their throats before posing them next to each other with the blood arcs at the head and feet resembling the constellation Gemini, pairs bound in life and death. Aside from the posed bodies, Gemini leaves no physical evidence at the scene of the crime as well as a lack of a clear motive or connection, making it impossible for the authorities to locate and identify him.

### Trophies\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother take Polaroid photographs of his victims both dead and before he kills them, with 16 of these photographs being present in **[Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")**. Prater pays them extra for "previews," pictures taken in the moment before the twins kill their victims when they know that it's going to happen. It's unknown how many total he has.

## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth%2527s_Twin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   According to [Lowell](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane"), Gareth was literally voted Most Likely to Become a Serial Killer in his high school yearbook.
-   The actor stated in an interview that Gareth is number 1 on the FBI’s most wanted serial killer list - likely due to the fact that every crime scene is marked with two victims which doubles his body count.
    -   In the same interview, he also stated how the Gemini Killer is very familiar of the Bay Harbor Butcher case and knows all his mistakes.
    -   The Gemini Killer being number 1 on the FBI’s most wanted serial killer list is also mentioned in "[Cats and Mouse](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_106:_Cats_and_Mouse "Resurrection Episode 106: Cats and Mouse")."
    -   This makes Gareth and his twin two of Dexter's most high profile kills due to how badly they were wanted by law enforcement.
-   According to _The Killing Century_, Gareth and his brother are classified as a **Visionary Killer**.
-   Gareth’s twin was the only one in Leon Prater’s club who went to Dexter’s New York home.
-   It's revealed at the end of "[Cats and Mouse](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_106:_Cats_and_Mouse "Resurrection Episode 106: Cats and Mouse")" that Gareth has a twin brother - who Dexter killed and believed was the Gemini Killer alone. Both killers acted together which means their moniker is truly **The Gemini Killers**.
-   Despite having the opportunity to tell the twin that he’s the Bay Harbor Butcher (considering he's had to deal with indulging Gemini’s ego up until that point), Dexter kills him without revealing the truth about who he is.
    -   His choice to not have a talk benefits him as moments after killing him [Blessing Kamara](/wiki/Blessing_Kamara "Blessing Kamara") shows up at his door and had he removed the twin’s duct tape from his mouth it’s possible that he would have heard the knock and screamed for help. As a kill location Dexter’s new apartment IS NOT secure for a kill ritual.
    -   Dexter also chooses to forgo his usual kill ritual with the twin, only taking a blood slide and then stabbing him rather than confronting the twin with his victims.
-   The Gemini Killers appear to operate at least primarily in Kansas as the victims listed on the website that Dexter reads are all in various places in Kansas.
-   Gareth 2 is left-handed while his brother Gareth 1 is right-handed. This allows the viewer to be determine which Gareth is on screen during Episode 4 and 6.
    -   Although Gareth is presumably the twin first seen in "[Call Me Red](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_104:_Call_Me_Red "Resurrection Episode 104: Call Me Red")," he never actually confirms or denies it when Dexter asks him outright himself. Considering the Gareth on that first night utilizes his right hand to drink (as seen during the toast to “new friends”) it would confirm that Gareth 1 is the one in Episode 4.

-   “I’ve been asked about Gareth’s twin’s name—and the answer is… Gareth. Their mother named them both Gareth. Part of their damage. So I guess two Gareths are better than one.” -Scott Reynolds (executive producer and co-showrunner) August 2025
-   When Gareth talks to [Dexter](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") about his nickname "The Gemini Killer," Dexter asks if it's a tribute to [the Zodiac Killer](/wiki/Zodiac_Killer "Zodiac Killer"). Gareth refutes the this comparison of him as an amateur despite the factcaught The Zodiaque is a notorious serial killer who has never been caught.
-   Gareth claims that David Fincher plans to make a film about his life as a serial killer, which is not surprising. Fischer is known for having directed a film about the zodiac in 2007 and more recently several episodes of the Netflix series on the preliminaries of Profiling, Mindhunter.
