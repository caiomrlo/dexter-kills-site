**Lowell Sloane****  
The Tattoo Collector**

**Lowell Sloane** is a character in the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime") series **_[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")_.**[\[1\]](#cite_note-1)

Lowell is said to be a successful anesthesiologist, but with a twisted alter ego known as **The Tattoo Collector**. He marks each homicide with surgical precision by preserving sections of his victims' flesh as souvenirs - carving out their tattoos and cherishing them as **[Trophies](/wiki/Trophies_of_Killers "Trophies of Killers")**.

He is one of **[Prater's Serial Killers](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")**, a select group of like-minded individuals that serve as the **Central Antagonists** of **[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")** and the **Second Victim** of **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")** when he realizes that Lowell's intent on killing someone soon.

## Description\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Lowell is a tall Caucasian man with dark hair and a piercing eyes. He had stubble matching his hair.

## Personality\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Lowell was quite abrasive and impolite as he called Al Jolly a "tub of shit." when he sees him. He also lacked a sense of boundaries since he flirted with Mia due to her new tattoo. He was also fairly nocturnal being annoyed when Dexter called at 10 AM, heavily implying he just woke up.

Unlike most victims, Lowell was completely calm while on Dexter's Kill Table which is because, having been on the other end of it enough times, he recognized the inevitability of what was coming. The only time that Lowell lost his calm demeanor was when Dexter revealed that he was actually the Bay Harbor Butcher rather than [Ronald Schmidt](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt "Ronald Schmidt"), causing Lowell to realize the magnitude of what he was facing.

## Background\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Not much is known about Lowell's early life. He went to medical school and became a successful anesthesiologist. In 2014 or before, he felt "a deep connection to tattoos" and began his path as a serial killer. it is mentioned that he killed 26 people and was still active. Eventually his identity would be found out by **[Charley Brown](/wiki/Charley_Brown "Charley Brown")** on behalf of **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** and he invited Lowell into the **[group](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")**. At least three of his victim's tattoos are in **[Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")**, though it's unknown if any were previously presented during a show-and-tell. He would form a friendship with each of the members in particular **[Al Walker](/wiki/Al_Walker "Al Walker")** though appears to have something of a strained history with **[Mia Lapierre](/wiki/Mia_Lapierre "Mia Lapierre")**.

Sometime near the end of 2021 going into 2022 Lowell began following a woman named **[Jules](/wiki/Jules "Jules")**, who is a tattoo artist at the **[Silver Serpent Tattoo Parlor](/wiki/Silver_Serpent_Tattoo_Parlor "Silver Serpent Tattoo Parlor")**. In February her 15 year old dog passed and on the 16th she posted a sketch of her intended tattoo - intending to get the dog's likeness done on her arm. Lowell waited patiently as she worked to complete the tattoo, leading up to "The Gathering"

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

### **DEXTER: Resurrection - Season One**\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   ['Call Me Red'](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_104:_Call_Me_Red "Resurrection Episode 104: Call Me Red")

After **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** kills **[Ronald Schmidt](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt "Ronald Schmidt")** and assumes his identity as "Red", he follows the instructions on an **[Invitation](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")** and **[Charley](/wiki/Charley "Charley")** brings him to **[Leon Prater's Manor](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Manor "Leon Prater's Manor")**. When Dexter meets Prater he's brought to his **[Trophy Room](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")** where a variety of serial killer trophies fill the large vault. After Dexter revisits old memories with several pieces in the collection he's introduced to **[Rapunzel](/wiki/Al_Walker "Al Walker")'**s area in the vault saying that he'll meet him tonight along with another guest - leading him over to a case containing three different sets of skin where a tattoo was printed (including an entire arm sleeve). Shortly after Prater shows him his "own spot" (as the **Dark Passenger**, his assumed identity) alongside **Lady Vengeance** and **The Gemini Killer**.

Following this Dexter meets with **[Mia Lapierre](/wiki/Mia_Lapierre "Mia Lapierre")** before they're joined by **[Al Walker](/wiki/Al_Walker "Al Walker")** and **Lowell** himself, who makes an entrance from the elevator greeting Al first calling him a "big tub of shit" with a smile as they hug each other. Afterwards he moves on to Mia, though settles for a fist bump from her instead of a hug and finally sets his eyes on Dexter - a smile adorning his face as he talks about the "new guy" he's heard about. "Welcome to the Freak Show!" and Dexter says there's no place he'd rather be.

Later as everyone settles in Lowell talks with Al asking if he sends them away or sharpens them himself (likely talking about his kill tools) and Al says that he handles all of it himself with a stone, disc and belt grinder. **[Gareth Pike](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")** walks in, apologizing saying that he's had trouble attaining a ride-share and Lowell jokes "You have anything to do with that, Red?" and they all chuckle, with Al asking Gareth to catch them up on his year. He denies and Lowell calls him out saying "Oh, Gare-bear, my guy...try not to be such a stereotype" where he calls him out as being the "most likely in high school to become a serial killer" in his school yearbook. Al says they all probably felt that way and Lowell admits "Guilty" for himself, with Dexter adding that he felt like an alien because he wanted to kill people. The directness of it earns some praise among his peers and he goes off to "speak" with **[Harry Morgan](/wiki/Harry_Morgan/Resurrection "Harry Morgan/Resurrection")**.

#### **His Next Piece**\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** informs his guests that dinner is ready and everyone makes their way to the dining hall, with Lowell's seat being that of **V.I.P #1** beside **V.I.Ps #4 and #5** Mia and Dexter with **V.I.Ps #2 and 3** Gareth and Al sitting across from them next to an empty seat for **V.I.P #6 [Keith Bauer](/wiki/Keith_Bauer "Keith Bauer")**. Al makes note of his absence, wondering if he'd be joining them but Prater says that he won't be joining them. He motions for Charley to hand him Keith's nailed bat, saying that they have one rule here - "do not turn against the group". He tells them that these rules are in place to protect them, not to punish them and that unfortunately **The Canton Clubber'**s bat would be joining his collection as "he no longer has use for it". Lowell is among the killers looking around at each other...realizing that despite their arrangement with Prater any one of them could end up the same way if they broke that tenet. After they welcome "Red" to the group with a toast, Lowell digs in by grabbing a slab of prime rib cooked for this meal - Dexter anxious to dig in himself only to be reminded that his assumed identity is a "vegan" and thus his dish...was a vegan one.

After Dexter finishes eating the "meal"...Lowell heads over to a TV screen behind some panels on the wall, dimming the lights. Dexter asks what's going on and Prater refers to it as the "highlight of the evening". In excitement he says "Let's show and tell, Baby!" and tells them that he wanted to whip up something fun for all of them - a bit of a multi-media presentation. Both Dexter and Mia remark to themselves that they dread it being a "vacation slide-show" and Lowell tells them all that tonight he's going to let them into his process. He says that as most of them know he has always "felt a deep connection with tattoos" and Al attests, saying "Yeah, he does!".

"_**So tonight I'm letting you all in on my process. As most of you know, I've always had a deep connection to tattoos. And lately this artist...has been doing some real interesting work. See, it's about the right piece on the right person.**_"

Lowell then pulls up the Instagram page of his next intended victim - **[Jules](/wiki/Jules "Jules")**. He goes over that he's been stalking her for weeks now, with Mia remarking that "if this motherfucker ever tried his shit on me...I'd make him eat his own eyeballs". He then references that Jules' 15 year old dog recently passed and she decided to get some new ink in his memory - a tattoo depicting the dog's face which would go on her arm. The sketch can be seen in the background, with Lowell turning towards it in a trance like state before he turns and explains his urge to kill.

"_**I knew this was my next piece as soon as I laid eyes on it. And what happens next...is inevitable. Because I can't stop it. I feel the pressure rise, becomes so intense...it's dizzying...unstoppable.**_"

Prater smiles, asking where's the tattoo...and Lowell says it's coming that she's been working on it for a bit with a few more sessions to go. He swears that before he leaves this town..."she will be mine" and Dexter says to himself that this just put Lowell at the top of his list. Prater says "You've never had a trophy so fresh" and thanks Lowell for his presentation which causes Lowell to smirk and salute him, before shutting down the feed to the TV. Later as Dexter is busy looking up "Lady Vengeance", Mia's moniker, Lowell walks over a little plastered and asks her to "Top me off, Ms. Sum-ah-lee-air?" to which she smiles - sarcastically and starts pouring him some wine when he notices "someone got new ink" on her left wrist - a tattoo of a corkscrew. He goes in to inspect it and she quickly takes his arm, twisting it behind his back as he screams out in pain - "I'm not one of your pervy art pieces" and he says alright before she lets him go. He takes his wine and walks around her, warning Dexter "Careful with this one, Red". As the night concludes, Lowell receives a cellphone to keep in contact with the rest of the group and bids Prater farewell - promising him the new trophy by their next meeting.

#### **Meeting the Bay Harbor Butcher**\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Dexter was intent on meeting up with Lowell and vetting him on his own time - making a call after he checks up on his son **Harrison Morgan** to Lowell. Half-awake he responds asking "The Fuuuuuck? It's not even 11" and Dexter apologizes saying he forgot he's on vacation. He then asks if he could meet up later and talk more about his...skins. Lowell tells him yeah, sure...though that he's not much of a day guy so he suggests Happy Hour. Dexter agrees and Lowell hangs up after briefly hearing a part about texting him an address. Dexter then goes to meet up with Mia Lapierre, with their connection leading back to her house where they nearly become intimate but he refocuses himself to pursue Lowell so he bids her farewell.

Lowell can be seen waiting at the bar, looking out the window when Dexter arrives. He apologizes for being late and says the next drink's on him though Lowell says he needs to take a rain check since he has to ...get ready. He looks across the street mentioning that Jules has an crazy tolerance to pain - already committed to completing the tattoo of her dog. Dexter then says "You mean...?"

"_**Yeah...Tonight's the night, Baby! Good thing I came prepared, check it out. My day job's anesthesiology but it helps in my nightlife, too. My next piece will be ready in about two hours and then she'll be walking back to her place a few blocks away - in the dark. So if you'll excuse me I kind of have to get in the zone. I love talking shop, but...this next piece is more of a "me" thing than a "we" thing. You get it.**_"

He shows Dexter a canister of anesthesia in his pack complete with a mask and once he tells him he's got to prepare, he looks back out the window with the same kind of look all hunters feel around their prey - unknowing that Dexter's smile behind him turned into a very similar look staring right at Lowell. Dexter heads out from the bar, realizing he's in a tough spot - he didn't bring his kill tools and they're over two hours away...which means he has to improvise.

Later that night Jules can be seen leaving the tattoo shop, headphones on as she dances down the sidewalk. Unfortunately she's oblivious to Lowell who is pursuing her in his kill attire - he reaches inside his pack and turns the valve on the canister, which releases anesthesia into the hose leading up to the mask. His speed picks up and he gets closer to Jules holding the mask until suddenly Dexter appears behind him and forces the mask onto Lowell's face - telling him to "count back from 1" until he's knocked out by his own gas. Dexter then drags his body into the tattoo parlor which conveniently came prepared with everything he needed (minus a killing weapon - that was provided by Lowell himself with a curved knife in his pack) including a case with a scalpel. Dexter's reminded of how Harry always thought his trophies were disgusting but Dexter says he couldn't understand him...he wasn't like him. Then he sits in his chair and spins over to where Lowell's head was resting "Like us" and he cuts into his cheek drawing blood.

As the cut happens Lowell shoots awake and comes to the realization that he's on a kill table - taking a big sigh as he says "Fuck me. You're gonna fuck with Prater's rules after day one. Whoa, ballsy" and chuckles with Dexter adding that "I'll take my chances". He sighs and just rests there, with Dexter saying that this is usually the part where people start screaming and begging for their lives.

"_**Yeah, well, I've been on the other side of this long enough to know when it's inevitable. Sorry to droop your chub.**_"

Lowell chuckles and he asks if he could at least "do him a solid" and knock him out first though Dexter says no - causing him to sigh once more. Dexter gets down closer to him and asks him why he keeps the skins. Lowell is able to open up freely about this, in a way that relates to Dexter's own needs throughout the years.

"_**I like looking at 'em when that pressure starts building. They...calm me. If I didn't have my skins, I'd have killed a lot more often. Got sloppy, got caught. Damn. I'm gonna miss 'em**_"

Dexter smiles, placing a hand on his chest and tells him that he knows how he feels. He then goes over to a photo on the wall and removes it, taking out the glass panels as if they were the blood slides of old. he walks over to Lowell and takes a drop of his blood, placing it between the panels which he closes turning into a circle just like his old slides. Lowell looks at it a little confused saying "that's your trophy? Pretty lame for a someone who saws off heads". Dexter looks at him for a moment and finally decides to open up about who he is.

"_**There's one thing I couldn't be honest about last night."  
"Is that right?"  
"I'm not Ronald Schmidt - I'm Dexter Morgan."  
"Who's that?"  
"The Bay Harbor Butcher.**_"

Lowell realizes who he is and swears as Dexter plunges the knife he brought into his chest, leaving it stuck there as Lowell slowly dies. He looks over at Dexter, saying "You....you..." before he finally dies. As with all his victims...Dexter cuts Lowell up into 9 pieces and transports him into garbage bags in his trunk which he drives down to the same place he dumped Ronald Schmidt. In fact, as he throws Lowell's pieces into the furnace he says "Lowell, meet Red" and closes it up as he's burned to ash.

## **Aftermath**\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**[Harry Morgan](/wiki/Harry_Morgan/Resurrection "Harry Morgan/Resurrection")** talks to Dexter after he disposes of Lowell, cautioning him that this decision was a risky one. It'll be suspicious when he doesn't show up at Prater's next meeting (especially considering he promised him a tattoo) and will likely lead Charley into investigating his whereabouts. He then says that being the new guy makes him the natural suspect and Dexter claims that "he'll figure something out".

-   "[Cats and Mouse](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_106:_Cats_and_Mouse "Resurrection Episode 106: Cats and Mouse")"

After Mia was arrested, Prater calls an emergency meeting of the group which Dexter, [Gareth's Twin](/wiki/Gareth%27s_Twin "Gareth's Twin") and Al attend. Al asks where Lowell is and Prater is forced to admit that he's gone missing. Dexter suggests that Lowell skipped town after getting spooked by Mia's arrest, which seems to convince Prater... for now.

-   "[Course Correction](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_107:_Course_Correction "Resurrection Episode 107: Course Correction")"

After killing Gareth and framing it as self-defense to the rest of the group, Dexter places the blame for Lowell's murder of the Gemini Killer which Al and apparently Prater buys, although Charley is more dubious.

-   "[Touched by an Angel](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_109:_Touched_by_an_Angel "Resurrection Episode 109: Touched by an Angel")"

When confronting Dexter in his vault after having learnt that he's really Dexter Morgan and the Bay Harbor Butcher, Leon surmises that Lowell didn't actually disappear but was murdered by Dexter, along with the real 'Red'.

-   "[And Justice For All](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_110:_And_Justice_For_All... "Resurrection Episode 110: And Justice For All...")"

While locked in Prater's vault, Dexter discovers his files on the various killers he has 'collected' over the years. Lowell's file reveals his surname to be 'Sloane', and is presumably one of the files that Dexter left behind for the NYPD.

## Victims\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Lowell has murdered at least 26 people, though the identities of his victims are unknown and unlike most killers who end up on Dexter's table - none of his trophies are present (due to the impromptu manner of having to capture him before he killed [Jules](/wiki/Jules "Jules")). At least three of his trophies are in Prater's collection and the way he speaks about his "skins" would suggest he has a sizable collection in his home.

-   26 unnamed victims are confirmed via the official showtime website for "Leon Prater's Vault" as well as their cause of death - skinning. [\[2\]](#cite_note-2)

### Motive\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Lowell has always felt a deep connection with tattoos - feeling that it's all about the "right piece on the right person". When he's settled on a victim and the tattoo that "speaks to him", he stalks them online and prepares to add those "pieces" (carved from his victim's flesh) to his collection. Lowell describes how he feels the Pressure as he calls his [Dark Passenger](/wiki/Dark_Passenger "Dark Passenger") rises, becoming intense, dizzying, and unstoppable. According to Lowell, staring at his previous victims' tattoos helps to keep the urges at bay for awhile. Without them, Lowell would kill more often, become sloppy and get caught.

### Modus Operandi\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

As an Anesthesiologist he has access to anesthesia that he can utilize in knocking out his victims, similar to Dexter's use of **[Etorphine](/wiki/Etorphine "Etorphine")** to knock out his own victims. It's assumed he drags them to a discreet location where he cuts off the tattoo and possibly skins them further leading to death via blood-loss, similar to that of **[The Skinner](/wiki/George_King "George King")**.

### Trophies\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Referred to as his "skins", the tattoos of his victims are carved from their bodies. It's unknown where he keeps the skins but there should be roughly 26, though 3 of them including an entire arm sleeve and two smaller pieces are in Leon Prater's collection. According to Lowell, looking at them helps to keep his [Dark Passenger](/wiki/Dark_Passenger "Dark Passenger") at bay without which, Lowell would've killed a lot more often - got sloppy and got caught.

## Attempted Victims\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   [Jules](/wiki/Jules "Jules") - Tattoo artist whom Lowell had been stalking online for awhile and intended as his next victim: which he presented before the group. His need to kill forces him into action earlier than he intended - choosing to kill her the night after the Dinner Party. When Dexter realizes this and being without his kill tools (which are over two hours away) he improvises. ## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLowell_Sloane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   Lowell is one of few killers who remained calm under Dexter's knife, for him knowing that he's been on the other side long enough to realize this was inevitable.
-   Lowell is one of a very select amount of killers who Dexter told his real name to and even admitted that he is the Bay Harbor Butcher.
    -   Lowell seems to react to this fact as if he's aware of BHB, which is likely true for many serial killers - something of a "boogeyman" among them.
-   Lowell carving the skin off his victims is similar to **[George King](/wiki/George_King "George King")**, aka "The Skinner" though George's reasons were tied to finding **[Freebo](/wiki/Fred_Bowman "Fred Bowman")** which was a delusional excuse for his desire to kill. King did not appear to keep the skin...or if he did, the collection was not found and Lowell kept his skins as trophies to admire - to help calm his need to kill.
-   Lowell's use of anesthesia to knock out his victims is similar to Dexter's use of M99, as both methods have essentially the same effect - Dexter even used anesthesia on Lowell himself.
-   **[Gareth's Twin](/wiki/Gareth%27s_Twin "Gareth's Twin")** seems to be familiar with Lowell, suggesting that perhaps they've met in the past with either brother acting as Gemini for the group.
-   Of all of [Prater's Serial Killers](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers"), Lowell is the only one that Dexter willingly revealed his identity to. Mia, Gareth and Gareth's twin never found out that he wasn't Red Schmidt before their deaths, Al fled before he could wind up one of Dexter's victims, and Leon and Charley only found out due to [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista "Angel Batista") telling them Dexter's true identity.
