**Leon Prater:** **Dwarf Billionaire with a Serial Killer Fetish**

**Leon Prater** is a Main Character serving as one of the two **Main Antagonists** (alongside **[Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection")**) of the first season of the **[Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime")** series **_[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")_.**

Prater is a billionaire venture capitalist with an vested interest in **[Serial Killers](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer")**. With the aid of his Head of Security **[Charley Brown](/wiki/Charley_Brown "Charley Brown")** (Former Special OPS operator), he invites "serial slayers" to his **[Manor](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Manor "Leon Prater's Manor")** for **Dinner Parties** and to share their craft: feeling a true connection with the darkness within them. Among his **[Guests](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")** is the elusive **Dark Passenger**, whom **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** impersonates after killing the real **[Ronald Schmidt](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt "Ronald Schmidt")** and joins his parties to start targeting his next victims. As Dexter gradually reduces his killers who **[disappear](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane")**, **[get arrested](/wiki/Mia_LaPierre "Mia LaPierre")** and **[have their carotid artery stabbed](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")**, Prater feels that he's made a true connection with "Red" and ultimately discovers that he has a son: **Harrison Morgan**.

With this discovery, Prater also meets with Batista who has been hellbent on bringing Dexter down for the deaths of **[James Doakes](/wiki/James_Doakes "James Doakes")** and **[Maria LaGuerta](/wiki/Maria_LaGuerta "Maria LaGuerta")** - learning Dexter's identity and that he is the real **Bay Harbor Butcher**. Ecstatic for this opportunity he offers himself as his benefactor, willing to feed him files on various "serial slayers" from across the globe so that he can kill each of them. However, the price of admission is for Dexter to kill Angel Batista. Unable to do that to his old friend, Dexter frees Batista in an attempt to work together against Prater but Batista's vendetta is too strong and he attempts to kill Dexter. Prater shoots Batista dead and even takes a shot at Dexter before leaving him trapped inside his **[Vault](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")** and proceeds with his plans for the **[10th Annual Gala](/wiki/10th_Annual_Gala "10th Annual Gala")**. However, Prater's reign is ended when Harrison frees his father and the two take Prater down. Dexter kills Prater and disposes of his body, leaving evidence tying the billionaire to Batista's murder and taking Prater's serial killer files and yacht for himself.

## Description

Prater is middle aged Caucasian male with greying mid-length dark hair and blue eyes as well as a thick beard. He has achondroplasia, a condition which stunts the growth of his bones so he stands at a height of 4 foot 5 inches. His usual attire consists of a white suit with a button up front from the bottom of his torso up to his neck.

## Personality

Leon Prater is a man who "wears many hats" - on most days he's a venture capitalist that helps start up companies. To the public he's a charitable man, donating millions to the New York Police Department and other organizations while hosting an annual Gala to celebrate them. Based on his past... he was deeply affected by the deaths of his parents at a young age and feels that he got to where he was today for all the people who helped him along the way.

He appears to be a narcissist, one who rose from humble beginnings to become a billionaire that "gives back" and he takes pride in that fact. He even watches an interview he had on _FACE TO FACE with Leslie Stahl_ where he chuckles and smiles to his own jokes while enjoying a meal he prepared for himself. He takes pride in his cooking as well having learned from some of the best and utilizes this when he helps prepare dinner for his "special guests".

When strapped to Dexter's kill table, Prater reveals a great deal of cowardice, begging for his life and offering money if Dexter spares him. However, he shows no remorse for his actions, instead continuing to show glee and fondness for the gatherings he had hosted.

### Interest in Serial Killers

"_**I pride myself in finding the best in every field, so why not death?"**_"

Prater has described himself as a "huge fan" - though despite this fact he claims "I couldn't presume to have the skill set or...imagination to do what you and the others do". This fascination was likely born from a trauma he suffered when his parents were killed, given the similarity to Dexter Morgan's own interest in serial killers. It grew to such an immense level that he created a highly secure vault to house a collection of serial killer memorabilia attained from some of the most infamous killers in history. Despite the cost or risk it took to acquire many of these items he houses them proudly - displaying them as if pieces in a museum with their own name plates referencing who the killer was. One particular killer he talks about is the **[New York Ripper](/wiki/New_York_Ripper "New York Ripper")**, saying that this killer terrorized the streets where he played as a child and that he was never caught.

The collection itself is an "ever expanding" one, as Prater's fascination with killers led to following the stories of active killers - somehow learning about their identities. Once he did he had no issue sending them large amounts of money with an invite and the promise of even more money if they would add their own trophies to his collection. The respect and admiration he has for this collection suggests a deep connection with it - so much so that when he asks these killers to "Show and Tell", he delights at the prospect of watching someone's process and receiving the trophy from their intended victim. He treats the killers like guests of honor - even friends and as a fan would sometimes gushes with excitement over meeting them. Wining and dining them with expertly cooked dishes (which he assists in, even adhering to any allergies or diet plans), planning events for them to partake in...as Dexter said:

"_**I know it takes one to know one...but this guy's fucking nuts**_"

Despite his deep fascination with killers and claims to not be one himself...he is not above allowing Charley to kill any killer who breaks one of his rules. He states there's only one rule with him - do not turn against the group. Charley defines this as asking for money or any kind of help - otherwise you are "uninvited". As punishment their killing tool (if there's a particular one they use) may be relinquished for his collection ...if Charley does not kill them for making threats. He has hired her to "make problems go away", which would be any killer that dared endanger Prater or his legacy - though it's unknown if he has every former guest killed and Mia just may not be aware of their fates.

After killing [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection"), Prater is excited as it awakens in him the same urge to kill that the people he collected have.

### Relationship with Charley

Prater values her as someone who can "make problems go away" but also handle all the field work in regard to tracking killers, confirming their identities and inviting them to meet with him (all of this despite her disgust with serial killers). In his own words he claims that he could not survive without her...which means he values her skill set as a former Spec Ops highly and believes with utmost confidence that she will deal with any threat to his life or his business. He's also aware that she does not "appreciate" his trophy collection.

That said...his tolerance for failure is low and he is not above making the idle threat towards her if she's unable to complete a task. What he lacks in ability to threaten her physically he more than makes up for in keeping her mother **[Mildred Brown](/wiki/Mildred_Brown "Mildred Brown")** alive and as such, she's forced to do whatever he requires - including tracking down serial killers for his "sick-fuck club". However, this ends after Dexter reveals that Prater has been keeping a dossier on Charley's dirty deeds, causing her to sever her relationship with Prater for good.

### Relationship with Dexter

"_**I don't know you just have this...way about you that's unlike the others...yet you live in a shroud of secrecy**_"

  

#### As "Red"

At their first meeting he expresses a deep joy: "[Ronald Schimdt](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt_\(alias\) "Ronald Schmidt (alias)") - Welcome!". He goes in to shake his hand and greets him with a smile, showing his excitement upon finally being able to match the "name with the face". He opens up with "Red" telling him that he's a huge fan of serial killers and shows him the same kind of host-courtesy he does with all his new friends. Even when Dexter asks what he does for a living his surprise is with a smile, curious how off the grid he truly is to not know much about him. That said...he brings Dexter in, like all the other killers, to his vault as he converses with him about his passion for death and offers him a place in his collection if he's willing to part with a trophy. His knowledge about Schmidt seems to extend to the identity of his first victim as the trophy Dexter gives him is that of **[Tyler Day](/wiki/Tyler_Day "Tyler Day")**, which he accepts feeling honored to receive it.

He treats "Red" as an honored guest, the same as his other killers and he expects to get an eye at his process: the raw intensity it takes to kill a man by sawing off their head. After the disappearance of Lowell and the arrest of Mia, Prater calls a meeting for the remaining killers and has Charley check out Red's apartment to see that he hasn't been there in some time. Prater arrives at the partner and calls "Red", asking where he's at. Dexter, needing to get this under control, gets direct with Prater - telling him he's never returning to that place, that being found out required uprooting and vanishing as he always did. Prater, taken aback by his abruptness, stutters his words for a moment before telling him he expects nothing less from the elusive Dark Passenger, though gets firm in requesting his audience at his manor. As Dexter's among the remaining killers, Gareth's paranoia over Mia's arrest leads Dexter stepping in to claim that Prater says this is a safe place and that he doesn't doubt him on that. It defuses the situation and Prater smiles, glad that "Red" was able to boost his ego a bit.

At the retreat Dexter gives a presentation as himself - which confuses Gareth and seemingly entertains Al but for Prater he remains silent as Dexter explains his "Red Schmidt" costume as a Mask he has to wear for everyone else because the true part of him, his **[Dark Passenger](/wiki/Dark_Passenger "Dark Passenger")**, is the one who truly controls everything he does. It's likely that Prater connected with this due to his own Mask he has to wear, as Dexter put it during his presentation "how heavy that Mask is" wears on Prater for not being able to express who he truly is. After the meeting he talks to "Red" privately and reveals the truth about his background - his deep fascination with serial killers starting with the man who killed his parents, **[Cooper Morris](/wiki/Cooper_Morris "Cooper Morris")**. He loved to hear about what they felt, witness it with his own eyes...and that it gave him power: knowing their story made him feel like he would "never be that scared little boy" again who had lost his parents. He also tells him that unlike the others, he can trust him...even shows him his very first trophy, a letter written by Cooper Morris in response to Prater when he was just a boy. This trust carries over after Dexter orchestrates a public kill by having Gareth attack him and he turns his weapon on him, killing Gareth by stabbing him in his corroded artery. He tells everyone that Gareth was talking crazy, that his ego went to his head and he was going to kill the other killers including him, Lowell and Al. Prater believes in Red - believing that he did them all a big favor by doing this and is thankful him.

After they return to the city, Charley's suspicions about "Red" begin to conflict with Prater's new found trust in him - though it does lead him to the truth that "Red" apparently has a son - Harrison. Prater starts to let his mask finally slip as he reveals a an arrogance, claiming that among anyone he invites into his inner circle he knows just about everything there is about them but with "Red"? Surprises, more and more surprises at every turn. He lists off some things but relaxes a bit - feeling that their connection at the presentation allows the two of them to converse freely. Prater brings up Charley's theory that Dexter is responsible for all of their recent misfortunes and Dexter responds claiming if he wanted to "fuck with your little gathering, you never would have seen me coming". He wants "Red" to trust him...despite the idle threats involving his son and tells him that he's shared more of his personal history with him more than anyone else, serial killer or not.

Prater wants to set up a meeting so that Dexter can tell him everything about who he is - his background, his parents and childhood and why he does what he does - that if he can share this with him that maybe Charley won't have to tail him anymore. Dexter agrees but as long as Harrison remains off limits - which Prater claims he'll do though this is a lie. In reality he orders Charley to meet Harrison and tell her what he can about his father - all the way down to the gun on the table to threaten him. This sets Dexter off, who tracks down Charley and utilizes the Dark Passenger's M.O. to get her to talk: learning that Prater ordered every single thing she did, down to threatening his son. He leaves her with a cut around her neck from the fiber wire he had wrapped around her neck and when Prater later examines in, he shows deep excitement over the fact that she survived an encounter with a serial killer. The fact that she wasn't makes him feel that "The Dark passenger is Mine" and even shows excitement when he receives a call from him. He claims to want to have that talk, reveal everything about the two of them but wants to do it alone, in Prater's manor. Prater agrees and Dexter prepares to finally put an end to him...but at that time his old friend and now nemesis **[Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection")** reaches out to Prater assuming that his life is in danger (as he had seen Prater and Dexter converse on the street).

He reveals the truth about who Dexter is...which changes everything.

#### As Dexter Morgan, The Bay Harbor Butcher

In learning the truth about who "Red" really was, Dexter keeps to the meeting they had arranged around 10 A.M. at his manor. When Dexter arrives Prater leads him to the wine room and tells him that he knows his real name - before opening his vault to lead him to Batista strapped to **[Brian Moser's](/wiki/Brian_Moser/Resurrection "Brian Moser/Resurrection")** kill table. Prater's excitement reaches new level as he tells Dexter that he loves having the actual Bay Harbor Butcher here. He notes that he found it a bit odd how drawn he was to the blood slides during their first visit - though when Batista told him the truth it all became clear. He smiles as he talks about Lowell and the actual Red, curious how he took 'em out but by this point Dexter's intent on ending Prater's life: not talking shop. He doesn't want to talk about any of this in front of Batista and Prater plays around covering Angel's ears for a moment before he talks about how he placed all the blame on Doakes and how that must have "fucked with you a just a little bit, didn't it? That stolen valor of all yours kills going to Doakes?".

Dexter - less inclined to talk about this than he normally would - asks what he's doing here with Prater and Prater tells him his proposal: he wishes to be his benefactor, despite having "collected" so many killers over the years he's willing to offer each of them up to the living breathing Bay Harbor Butcher even willing to host more of these dinner parties so he can do what he does. Dexter seems like he even considers it for a moment but the elephant in the room...strapped to table at that...is what happens to Batista. Prater, fully committed to seeing how the Butcher works in person, wants him to kill Batista - offering him a blade that was sitting beside his first blood slide box. He's convinced that Dexter will go along with him, even having Charley stand down despite her desire to put him down if he tries anything...and takes the gun from her as he stands by to watch Dexter do what he does.

"_**I must admit, I've always wanted to see someone like you at work.**_"

Dexter acts like he's going to do the kill but frees Batista, in hopes that maybe they could work together at least one last time. However, Batista's rage overcomes him and he tackles Dexter, strangling him for Doakes and Maria. Before he can kill him he's shot in the back five times by Prater who stands back first with a sinister smirk and then his smirk fades to a daze, lost in the act of having murdered someone at last. As Charley grabs him to get him out of the room he aims at Dexter and takes another shot, though it misses hitting some fine China next to him. He waves to Dexter as the vault closes him in and intends to leave Dexter to die due to how dangerous he is.

After Dexter's escape with Harrison's help, Prater kidnaps the young man as leverage to force Dexter back and expresses an intention to kill them both. Prater reveals that Batista's murder, while impulsive, has awakened his own [Dark Passenger](/wiki/Dark_Passenger "Dark Passenger") and Prater is all too eager to kill someone non-impulsively for the first time. However, Harrison is able to take Prater down with an [M99](/wiki/Etorphine "Etorphine") syringe given to him by Dexter earlier for protection.

Upon awakening on Dexter's kill table, Prater instantly displays a great deal of cowardice, begging for his life and offering Dexter an enormous amount of money if Dexter will spare him. However, Prater shows no remorse for his actions, complaining that none of this would've happened if Dexter had killed Batista when he was supposed to. Dexter explains his deception as Red and the revelation about himself that he had in the process. Although frightened, Prater is clearly disappointed when Dexter chooses not to take a blood slide before killing him, thus not adding Prater to his own collection, but mistakenly believes that it means that Dexter is going to spare his life after all.

"_**I'm not going in the blood slide box?"  
"Nope. You're not."  
"Oh, God. Oh, God. (Prater laughs) Yes. I knew you'd see it my way. Nobody can turn down that kind of money. No."  
"Oh. No. I am going to kill you. I keep trophies to remember, and I don't wanna remember you.**_"

Dexter then kills Prater, chops up his body and dumps it into New York Harbor. Before leaving with most of Prater's serial killer files and his yacht, Dexter triggers the vault's alarms so that the NYPD will find it and learn the truth about Prater, leaving evidence tying him to Batista's murder for the cops to find, thus exposing the real Leon Prater to the world.

## Background

### Early Life

Leon Prater was born to **Mr. Prater** and **Mrs. Prater** in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")**. According to Leon he was still a young boy when he tragically lost both of his parents in an accident caused by [Cooper Morris](/wiki/Cooper_Morris "Cooper Morris"), while he sat in the back seat. The event likely traumatized him from an early age as he refers to it (through a choked up voice) that "it was a formative time in my life". Police officers were able to save Prater from the back seat and he gained an admiration for them - which would be paid back later in his life. He was placed in foster care and eventually wound up in a house he claims to have spent most of his teenage years in (with pride). Around this time the **[New York Ripper](/wiki/New_York_Ripper "New York Ripper")** was active in his neighborhood and this may connect to his interest later in life.

During his life, he sent letters to Cooper, the man showing no remorse and stating he purposefully killed them after an argument with his girlfriend. The man also told Prater that he killed before. Prater noted that he felt an extreme amount of control. According to him, his killer was a sort of father figure, but he died before his 50th birthday. He also sent letters to Prater talking about his time in prison, including watching as a man burned alive.

After he became an adult his career path is a bit of a mystery - as he went from someone from humble beginnings to a billionaire. He says that "he wears many hats" and that most days he's a venture capitalist who focuses on incubating and curating start-up companies. To get there he says that he had help along the way and when he made his first million he paid off the mortgage on his last foster home as well as the mortgages of every family who adopted a child from that home. Around 2012 he began hosting an annual charitable gala for the **[New York Police Department](/wiki/New_York_Police_Department "New York Police Department")**, where his donations to the men and women who saved him as a child are met with both praise for his altruism and criticism given the general public's view of police departments over the recent decades.

At some point he met **[Charley](/wiki/Charley "Charley")**, a former soldier from Spec Ops and hired her as his Head of Security, though her duties would be that of a "right hand woman" involved in his...secret life.

### Fan of Serial Killers

Unbeknownst to the general public however is an extreme fascination with death - as well experts in the field: **[Serial Killers](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer")**. With his immense wealth he purchased an entire city block in Lower Manhattan where he had his **[Mansion](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Manor "Leon Prater's Manor")** constructed. A massive white manor containing an indoor courtyard to park multiple vehicles with elevator access taking guests up to an exquisite home containing various expensive works of art and an impressive wine collection. That wine cellar however contains a secret vault - surrounded by five inches of solid steel behind limestone and protected by an alarmed security system (as well as security personnel trained to kill on sight).

The contents of this vault? **[Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")**. Inside over the years he's acquired various pieces of evidence including **[Trophies of Killers](/wiki/Trophies_of_Killers "Trophies of Killers")** for some of the most notorious serial killers to ever live. Sometime in the 2010's Prater began hosting a secret Gathering where he invited serial killers he was able to identify to "Dinner Parties". These events allowed like-minded individuals the chance to commune with others like them and enjoy fine dining as well as share in their process with the rest of the group. According to **[Mia LaPierre](/wiki/Mia_LaPierre "Mia LaPierre")** Prater has hosted these events for several years prior to her first year and that she's seen killers come-and-go if they weren't invited back. She did know that one guy was "uninvited" while another was arrested - then committed suicide that same night (allegedly).

#### The Tale of Cooper Morris

As Dexter finishes his presentation Prater's gaze at him is quiet, unusual for his normal sadistic smirking when watching a killer's process while Dexter imitates a puppet being manipulated by strings. After the presentation Dexter heads outside with the other killers to eat under the tent when Gareth begins expressing his suspicions about Dexter (considering all the things that have gone wrong since he joined). (Prater looks back to see if anyone's nearby) I know most of my guests are here only for the money - Avaricious braggarts (chuckles) That's why I so appreciated your honesty today. ## Attempted Victims

Prater intended to kill two people, his motivation likely being to protect his legacy from the Butcher and his son’s meddling. In addition, after killing [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection"), Prater discovered his own [Dark Passenger](/wiki/Dark_Passenger "Dark Passenger") and an urge to kill.

-   [Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan "Dexter Morgan") - Prater attempts to kill Dexter 3 times.
    -   After discovering that he was the Bay Harbor Butcher, he planned to work with Dexter as his benefactor: feed him the files on numerous killers throughout the world so that he can do what he does and add them to his collection (or rather, Prater's). In order to seal this deal however - he has to kill Angel Batista and hands him a knife to do so. When Dexter refuses and frees Batista, the two have a struggle where Batista pins him and tries to strangle him for what he did to Doakes and Maria. Before he can kill Dexter, Prater mortally wounds Batista who slumps over while he's left with a grin. His grin fades into a daze as he stands there and raises the gun to shoot at Dexter, only for Charley to pull him back at that moment which causes the shot to miss hitting the China Set of the Golden State Killer.
    -   After locking Dexter in the vault, he intended to keep Dexter trapped in there until he died of dehydration. It’s likely that when Dexter chose to free Batista that Prater decided that he couldn’t risk keeping him alive - that the Butcher was too dangerous to make use of. By all accounts from Charley and the stakes of Harrison had chosen to leave Dexter where he was, Prater’s plan was to have him die as a shriveled corpse within his vault for however long it took. Dexter did work out what the code was however and opened the vault.
    -   Once Charley chooses to quit on Prater and reveals she knows about a file on her inside his vault, he spots Harrison and captures him - calling Dexter up to witness his first actual murder. When Dexter appeals to him to take his life instead of his son’s, Prater seems to brush it off saying he can’t risk a witness. As he stood ready to shoot Harrison, Dexter hints to his son about the “protection” he provided which was a needle of [M99](/wiki/Etorphine "Etorphine") and Prater makes up his mind to take Dexter up on his offer: he draws his gun to shoot Dexter in the chest but is injected with the needle, knocked out as the gun fires towards the ceiling.
-   [Harrison Morgan](/wiki/Harrison_Morgan "Harrison Morgan") - After Charley had quit on him and he realized Harrison was at his event, he decided to leave the gala to pursue the boy - informing his security to first find him on the camera and then shut off all cameras. He then demanded his guard’s gun and went off to pursue Harrison himself - capturing him before he can escape the premises. He calls Dexter to join him in the wine room, where he has a gun planted on the back of Harrison’s skull with a smile. He wants to show Dexter his first non-impulsive kill, intending to take Dexter’s son from him. When Dexter appeals saying to take his life instead, Prater insists that despite that he can’t leave a witness. Seemingly ready to shoot Harrison first, he instead opts to aim at Dexter which gives Harrison time to inject him with an M99 needle which knocks him out, saving both their lives.

## Notes

-   Leon Prater is among the wealthiest characters in the franchise, a self-made billionaire from humble beginnings in foster care.
-   Leon Prater is the second antagonist to kill another main antagonist, namely [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection"). The first to have this feat is [Lila West](/wiki/Lila_West "Lila West"), who killed [James Doakes](/wiki/James_Doakes "James Doakes").
-   Like Dexter and Brian Moser, Leon Prater's parents were killed. While he claims it was a drunk driver in an interview, there may be more to that story considering his fascination with various serial killers - including the [New York Ripper](/wiki/New_York_Ripper "New York Ripper").
    -   All three were placed in foster care, with Dexter staying with the Morgan family. Brian was bounced foster home to foster home due to his anger issues and wound up in a mental institution where he grew up isolated, angry at his life. Prater remarks that his time in foster care was pleasant having spent most of his teenage years there - even turning around to pay the mortgages of the foster home he was in and all the mortgages of families that adopted out of there.
-   Perhaps in trying to relate with Brian, he tells Dexter (who is disguised as "Red") that if Brian Moser had someone to talk to, someone like him, that maybe he would be with them today. Had Dexter not killed his brother and let him go...it's possible that he would have enjoyed a place like what Prater offered. In fact, Dexter if he let go of the "kernel of good" and his attachment to normal people that he found, would also love the arrangement and the friendship offered by the rest.
-   Prater's fascination with serial killers is similar to Dexter's, as seen in **[Dexter: Original Sin](/wiki/Dexter:_Original_Sin "Dexter: Original Sin")**. He used to idolize "the others", following their stories with his own little scrapbook collection. When Dexter sees the collection a similar amazement overcomes him, likely something Prater may not have gathered from the others unless they too were fans of "the greats".
-   He is currently the shortest adult male main character within the franchise at 4 feet and 5 inches tall due to actor Peter Dinklage’s medical condition achondroplasia which stunted the growth in his limbs.
-   He is the first major character to express a fascination in utilizing Serial Killers without being one himself (unless confirmed later in the series) and seeks to use them for some unknown purpose.
-   In August 2025, English rapper Conna "Three Chainz" Haines revealed he had auditioned for the role unsuccessfully. Haines, standing at 5 feet and 3 inches, released a diss track called "Smaller Than Me".
-   It’s ironic that Prater held Galas celebrating the New York Police Department and law enforcement in general yet his only kill is that of a cop, Angel Batista.
    -   Dexter remarks that Prater will be remembered as a cop killer with a serial killer fetish, which is exactly what happens.
-   It's also ironic that Prater says early on that he would not be able to survive without Charley - this is confirmed by her quitting, leaving him at Dexter’s mercy and ultimately being caught by surprise.
    -   Had he not kept a file on her with all the other killers then it’s possible that she would have killed Harrison and left Dexter to die in the vault per Prater’s plan.
-   Prater was always a killer at heart but lacked the ability to carry out a murder himself until he spontaneously shot Batista in the back. Once he got a taste of it he felt at home, having connected with the sick fascination of other killers throughout his life.
    -   This, unfortunately for him, meant he now fit the Code which made him worthy of the Butcher's table.
    -   Prater is unable to grasp the type of person Dexter is in much the same way as other killers: believing that satisfying his Urge was what mattered most to him. He was completely unprepared for a killer with a conscience - one trained by his father to kill the bad guys.
    -   From Prater's description, Batista's murder unlocked Prater's own [Dark Passenger](/wiki/Dark_Passenger "Dark Passenger").
-   Prater ultimately did get a front row seat to the Butcher works - by being one of his victims. He did not have a blood slide taken, as Dexter did not want to remember him like he did the others. This could be due to the threat on his son's life or simply a final insult by not making Prater a part of his own "collection." Notably, the only time that Prater wasn't terrified was when he asked with obvious disappointment that he wasn't going in the [Blood Slide Box](/wiki/Blood_Slide_Boxes "Blood Slide Boxes").
-   Prater's body was dismembered, placed into three garbage bags by Dexter and dumped in New York Harbor near the Statue of Liberty.
