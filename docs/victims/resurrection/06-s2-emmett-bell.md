**Emmett Bell**[\[1\]](#cite_note-1) (better known as the **Midnight Mangler**) is a Character in the **[Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime")** original series _**[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")**_.

The Midnight Mangler is a **[Serial Killer](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer")** whom **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** had among his extensive files on "Serial Slayers". Following **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")'**s escape from **[Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")**, he kept a selection of files that Prater kept stored in filing cabinets within his Vault. Among these killers are **[Al Walker](/wiki/Al_Walker "Al Walker")** and the **[Yonkers Slayer](/wiki/R.H._Martin "R.H. Martin")**.

He is among several targets Dexter kills going into **[Resurrection Season Two](/wiki/Resurrection_Season_Two "Resurrection Season Two")**.

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEmmett_Bell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Following the events of the **[10th Annual Gala](/wiki/10th_Annual_Gala "10th Annual Gala")**, Dexter began targeting the remaining five killers in Prater's "boogeyman" club to finally put this group of killers to rest.

### DEXTER: Resurrection Season 2\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEmmett_Bell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   '**[Risky Business](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_201:_Risky_Business "Resurrection Episode 201: Risky Business")'**

Dexter began hunting down the killers and starts with the **[Midnight Mangler](/wiki/Midnight_Mangler "Midnight Mangler")**, which Dexter goes over his file revealing photos of his broken and mangled victims. From Prater's notes this killer captured his victims and held them for two weeks, breaking their bones one by one before finally killing them at Midnight as part of his ritual. According to Dexter his reign of terror lasted 8 years claiming the lives of 32 victims, where his cruelty knew no bounds until Dexter manages to track him down in **Seattle** as he's stalking a woman on the streets. As he moves in for the kill Dexter slips behind him with a syringe and injects it into his neck, catching his body which gives him a bit of trouble due to his size.

The Mangler is next on a kill table, strapped to it by plastic as he searches the room asking what's going on and tries to free himself to no avail. Dexter pushed his head down with one hand and did his signature finger press on his forehead, which terrifies the man as he begins to beg for his life. He tells Dexter that he can't do this but Dexter remarks "Oh but I think I can", pulling out a hatchet which he plunges into the Mangler's chest, killing the man on the spot. With his death a montage of another potential 32 victims plays as Dexter states that taking him out spared their lives.

  

## Victims\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEmmett_Bell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Emmett has murdered at least 32 people according to the file _[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")_ had on him, though the identities of these victims remains unknown. It's unknown if he collected any trophies of his victims as none were present in **[Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")**.

### Motive\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEmmett_Bell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Little is known of his motive outside of how Dexter described him. He's a particularly cruel man who tortured his victims by breaking each of their bones one by one, so it's likely he was a sadist who reveled in their agony. Why he chose to kill them at exactly Midnight is unknown.

### Modus Operandi\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEmmett_Bell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

From what was shown of the Mangler before he was captured, he finds victims isolated in areas such as city streets at night and approaches them with the intent of capturing them. Once he has them, he moves them to an unknown location where he holds them for two weeks and actively breaks each of their bones one by one until he's decided to kill them. How he kills them is unknown, though it's possible he breaks their necks given how two of the victims were shown with their heads twisted at an angle. Their bodies are then left in what appears to be the same location, laying on concrete with some repair done to the ground via sealant.

## Attempted Victims\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEmmett_Bell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   Young Woman - One night in Seattle this woman can be seen walking down the sidewalk before moving onto the street looking around, while Emmett has been following her at a distance. Before he can approach her, Dexter sneaks up on him from behind and injects him with M99, sparing the woman's life.

## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEmmett_Bell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   Dexter had already cut the man's cheek for his bloodslide by the time he's seen on the table and has one of the briefest kill table sequences alongside the **[Yonkers Slayer](/wiki/R.H._Martin "R.H. Martin")**. It's unknown if there was a conversation prior to this kill.
-   Dexter utilizes a unique method to kill him, choosing a hatchet over his standard knife.
-   Chris Jericho took to social media to express excitement over being part of the DexterVerse, though laments that his character had to die stating "Also, The Mangler might’ve had a better chance if that rat bastard Dexter hadn’t ambushed him from behind… F\*\*k You Dexter Morgan!! #EmmettBellWasRobbed".
-   Dexter saves a young woman Emmett was stalking in a similar manner to how he stopped **[Lowell Sloane](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane")** by approaching them from behind before they were able to grab their intended victims. Both women were unaware their lives were in danger as well.
    -   Due to his equipment being over an hour away from Lowell, Dexter had to utilize Lowell's own anesthesia mask to knock him out while with Emmett he had access to a syringe with M99 that he used to inject him with.
    -   Both killers had followed their intended victims at night intending to grab them in the otherwise deserted city streets, though it's unknown how Emmett planned to subdue her.
