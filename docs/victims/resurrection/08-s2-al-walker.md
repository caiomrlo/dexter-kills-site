**Albert "Al" Walker** (also known as **Al Jolly**[\[1\]](#cite_note-1) and nicknamed **Rapunzel**) is a character in the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime") series **_[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")_.**[\[2\]](#cite_note-2) He is a serial killer who kills female victims and cuts off their ponytails for his collection. His need to kill is called "The Need". He was among **[Prater's Serial Killers](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")**, a group of loyal serial killers that were bankrolled by the billionaire venture capitalist **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** and served as one of the Central Antagonists of Season 1. Following **[Gareth](/wiki/Gareth_Pike "Gareth Pike")'**s death, he is the only surviving member of the group outside of its apex predator **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection").**

Unlike the other serial killers, Al appears to be a well-off family man with pride in his family as he goes about his business trips guaranteeing that special "Prater bonus". That bonus comes from his secret life - being the infamous serial killer known as Rapunzel. For a serial killer - he's surprisingly "Jolly" and his friendly demeanor is an unusual trait - almost as if the raw-aggressive side he possesses needs to kill has been separated from who he is outside of it. Despite his demeanor, he also appears to be a relatively well-adjusted man who happens to be a serial killer, marking a dark parallel to what Dexter could have become if not for **[Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell")**. Mia suggests that Al's mommy issues are the root of his killing spree as he targets women with hair the same as his mother.

Al is Dexter's last intended victim of the group, but he returns home to Wisconsin before Dexter can kill him. As he leaves, Al throws away the phone that Prater had given him, although he expressed an intention to return the next year if Prater were to continue the gatherings. However, Dexter later finds Al's file amongst [Prater's files](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Files "Leon Prater's Files") and takes it, giving him another shot at Al. Three years later, Dexter finally catches up to Al and puts and end to his killing spree.

## Description\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Al is a middle aged Caucasian man with greying hair, glasses and a heavyset appearance. He has been seen wearing standard clothes for a father of four - such as long sleeved polos and dress pants.

## Personality\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Al Jolly is literally a Jolly man - uncharismatic for someone responsible for the deaths of 18 women. He carries himself as a proud family man who absolutely adores his children and wife. This positive energy has rubbed off on his fellow serial killers as they all seem invested in seeing updates on his family - despite the fact that they're murderers responsible for killing innocents themselves. He brings an energy to the whole thing - brightening an otherwise morbid reality. He values his fellow serial killers as friends and hugs them as such - almost resembling the kind of passion someone like **[Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista "Angel Batista")** has shown his friends.

As someone enjoying a dinner party, he's a big talker that wants to see how everyone's been doing and also praises his fellow killers for certain attributes - such as chiming in on **[Lowell Sloane](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane")'**s presentation to support his love of tattoos. He's a bit quirky even feeling bad for a recently deceased dog of Lowell's intended victim but shows admiration for his chosen tattoo piece. He also takes note of the absence of one of their own and his expression even drops when he learns that **[Keith Bauer](/wiki/Keith_Bauer "Keith Bauer")** is dead.

As a killer...while it hasn't been shown yet, he expresses his desire to kill as "The Need"...and even feels it urge up as Lowell talks about the "power" of his own need. To him it appears that the "Need" allows him to murder someone, finding release to get out all of his anger, aggression and whatever else to get a satisfaction from their death.

When he is seen killing a victim a video presentation to Prater, he is seen giddily laughing while strangling his next victim, showing how sadistic he is.

Al is squeamish around blood which is why he strangles his victims.

Belying his friendliness to the rest of the group is a deep-seated sense of self-preservation and caution that Al only really shows once he's the only remaining member of the group, other than Dexter. While he was unaware that Dexter was killing the members of the group (sans Mia, who was killed by Prater and Charlie as a precautionary measure) or his true identity since he knew him as 'Red', he showed reluctance to meet up with him by saying he was eager to get home. While having made plans to meet up with Dexter after he was going to see Hamilton, he instead fled New York at the intermission. When Dexter called him and then tried to arrange a meeting in Wisconsin, he said he didn't want to mix the Prater world with his family life and once he was done talking to Dexter, he was quick to ditch the phone given to him by Prater despite saying he would come back next year if the gatherings continued.

Observing Al, [Harry Morgan](/wiki/Harry_Morgan/Resurrection "Harry Morgan/Resurrection") compares him to [Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell/Resurrection "Arthur Mitchell/Resurrection"), confused as to how a man who can do such awful things also be the family man that Al presents himself as. After witnessing Al's dark side come out when he spots a possible victim before switching back when Al's wife draws his attention, Harry comments that it's as easy as flipping a switch for Al to switch between his family man and serial killer personas.

When asked about how he gets his family to go along with his lifestyle, Al coldly states that he basically owns them and they have to do whatever he wants. In reality, Al could care less about his wife and children's feelings and believes that they're lucky to have a provider like him.

## Background\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Al Walker was born on **September 5th, 1972** in **Wisconsin** to his Mid-Western parents. He grew up with "mommy issues" as **[Mia LaPierre](/wiki/Mia_LaPierre "Mia LaPierre")** would suggest - saying that his mother wore her hair in a ponytail. It's likely that she abused him and created a trauma within that manifested as he got older into a killing urge. When he started killing is unknown - though at some point he met and married a woman named **[Bonnie Walker](/wiki/Bonnie_Walker "Bonnie Walker")**, with whom he had four children. \`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\` His murders were eventually traced to him by **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** and he was invited to be apart of his **[Serial Killer Group](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")** which he took to with excitement. Throughout the course of his meetings he's provided **six ponytails** to Prater to join his **[Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")** and his family enjoys the hefty "Prater bonus" from these additions. In regard to his family - Al's good natured personality is refreshing among other serial killers, as he shows off his family to everyone despite the fact that they're all murderers. He formed a friendship with every killer he's met - the one who seems to like him the most being **[Mia LaPierre](/wiki/Mia_LaPierre "Mia LaPierre")**.

In their last meeting in 2021 his wife Bonnie was still pregnant with their fourth child **[Mabel Walker](/wiki/Mabel_Walker "Mabel Walker")** and she was later born that July. By March of 2022 he's killed at least 18 women, strangling them to death for an as of yet unknown reason.

"_**It kills me to let Al go free...for now.**_"

#### Aftermath of Al's Escape\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

"_**My Dark Passenger anticipated having Al on my table. Now what?**_"

With Al's escape, Dexter looks into the corrupt landlord giving Elsa's son Dante so much trouble with the mold issue. After a rough run-in that has him remark on the strength Harrison must have had to not kill this guy - Dexter later captures Vinny and utilizes the kill room he had prepared for Al. Unlike most of his victims he only intended to scare him in this situation so he left his clothes on under the plastic wrap and wore a Stocking Mask to conceal his identity. Dexter threatens Vinny, a knife to his throat and even holds his mouth and nose shut so he's unable to breath for a good minute as he asks if he can change which causes Vinny to nod. While threatening Vinny if he fails to do change, Angel Batista had tracked Dexter to this location and breaks into the shop which Dexter hears. He frees Vinny, allowing him to be "someone else's problem" as he charges towards the front door where Batista came in knocking him over. Dexter escapes...and Batista goes over the kill room, having no idea that it was meant for a serial killer like Al. When he ropes in Detectives **[Claudette Wallace](/wiki/Claudette_Wallace "Claudette Wallace")** and **[Melvin Oliva](/wiki/Melvin_Oliva "Melvin Oliva")**, they don't believe his theories about the Bay Harbor Butcher and look at him as if he's crazy.

After destroying the tracker in his car, Dexter later meets with his son who tells him that Vinny has completely turned over a new leaf - fixing the mold, treating his tenets better and he asks what he said to him. Dexter claims he just told him some spiel about his actions having consequences though Harrison smiles realizing he scared him and Dexter admits to it. As they continue talking...**[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** walks into the restaurant and calls to Dexter, the last remaining killer as part of his dinner party and gives him a smile after saying he "didn't know you had a son".

-   "[And Justice For All...](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_110:_And_Justice_For_All... "Resurrection Episode 110: And Justice For All...")"

While trapped in Prater's vault, Dexter finds Prater's extensive serial killer files, including the one on Al. After killing Prater, Dexter takes most of the files with him as well as Prater's yacht. Al's file is on top of the pile, suggesting that Dexter intends to go after him first using the information that Prater had gathered on the man.

  

### DEXTER: Resurrection - Season Two\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

#### Another Shot\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   "[Risky Business](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_201:_Risky_Business "Resurrection Episode 201: Risky Business")"

Following Prater's death, Al goes on an extended family vacation across the country for the next few years. However, Dexter sees a news report about another Rapunzel killing in Worlds End State Park just outside of Forksville, Pennsylvania. The news report mentions four other Rapunzel killings in state parks across the Midwest and the Eastern Seaboard. Near every state park where they found a Rapunzel victim have been several campgrounds. Dexter investigated a victim in Hawk's Nest State Park the year before and discovered that Al Walker and his family had stayed at a KOA nearby, but Dexter just missed catching up to Al.

Despite [Harry](/wiki/Harry_Morgan/Resurrection "Harry Morgan/Resurrection") urging caution, Dexter travels to Pennsylvania to kill Al before he can escape again. Dexter finds Al and his family at a KOA just a few miles from the state park. While Al goes biking with his family, Dexter sneaks into his "motor mansion" and finds Al's trophies hidden beneath a false bottom in a dresser drawer. Observing Al from a distance when he returns, Dexter sees Al's interest being drawn to a future victim. In his guise as [Red](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt_\(alias\) "Ronald Schmidt (alias)"), Dexter shows himself to Al, claiming that he had wanted to connect after seeing Al's latest work. Al warns Dexter to stay away from his family, but Dexter promises that he isn't there to mix Al's two worlds, claiming that he's been scared since Prater disappeared and wanted to talk to someone who understands. Al agrees to Dexter's request to catch up later at an old body shop nearby. Unlike their previous interactions, Al is noticeably more guarded and less jovial around Dexter.

That night, Al arrives at the body shop where Dexter offers him a beer. Heading inside, Al comments that the previous owner just died and his grandson will be taking over. The two make small talk and Dexter admits that he admires Al for being able to be both a serial killer and a family man, something that Dexter has been struggling to do. Dexter asks how Al gets his family to go along with his lifestyle and Al coldly tells Dexter that he basically owns his family who must do whatever Al tells them. As Dexter prepares his [M99](/wiki/Etorphine "Etorphine"), Al reveals that he's onto him, having noticed his missing trophies. Al forces Dexter to drop the syringe and smashes it, beginning a brutal fight between the two men. Dexter reveals his true identity to Al, stating that he kills serial killers like Al. However, Al proves to be stronger than Dexter anticipated, overpowering him and nearly suffocating Dexter with the plastic sheeting he had set up for the [kill room](/wiki/Dexter%27s_Kill_Room "Dexter's Kill Room") before Dexter manages to break free. As Al strangles him, Dexter wraps a hoist chain around his neck and uses it to lift Al into the air, effectively hanging him to death.

Afterwards, Dexter collects a [blood slide](/wiki/Blood_Slide_Boxes "Blood Slide Boxes"), but chooses against disposing of Al's body. Instead, Dexter leaves Al hanging in the garage with a note outside for the person who finds it to not enter and call 911, wanting to let the world know that Rapunzel is no longer a threat. However, a hallucination of [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection") asks how the cops are supposed to know that Al is Rapunzel. When Dexter gets home, he does a search on Rapunzel's death, but comes up with no results, suggesting that Al's body has yet to be found or that he hasn't been connected with Rapunzel.

## Victims\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Al is responsible for tracking down and strangling 24 women, cutting off their ponytails as trophies. Their bodies are left where he strangles them, many of which are found along trucking routes across the country however at least six of his victims have been found in state parks situated near camping grounds. Only one of his victims has been seen thus far and six trophies were provided to Prater for his vault with another seven kept by Al in his RV:

-   [Jogger](/wiki/Ponytail_Jogger "Ponytail Jogger") found in Black Hollow State Park; Wisconsin
-   Three unidentified victims found in State Parks between the Midwest and Eastern Seaboards.
-   Unidentified Female found in Hawks Nest State Park; West Virginia (2025)
-   Unidentified Female Hiker found in the Worlds End State Park; outside Forksville, Pennslyvania (June 2026)

### Motive\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

[Mia Lapierre](/wiki/Mia_Lapierre "Mia Lapierre") suggests that Al's motive is his mommy issues as all of Al's victims wear their hair the same way that his mother did. It's possible that he was abused throughout his childhood which drove a primal desire he refers to as "The Need" to track down women who visually resembled his mother and strangle the life out of them.

### Modus Operandi\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Al pretends to be an injured biker in the park and when his victims approach to help, Al takes them to the ground and strangles them to death while covering their mouths with his other hand. He then cuts off their hair as a trophy with the comment "Pop-Pop says it's time for a haircut."

### Trophies\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Al cuts off the ponytails of his victims, earning him the moniker "Rapunzel." Six of these trophies were provided to Leon Prater for his collection, while seven of them were kept with him during his "extended family vacation" across the country. It's possible his other trophies are stored at his home address in Madison, Wisconsin.

## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAl_Walker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D24&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   He is possibly a homage to serial killer [Dennis Rader](/wiki/Dennis_Rader "Dennis Rader"), an American serial killer and salesman known for coming across as friendly, as well as being a family man.
-   Al, like **[Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell")**, is a family man serial killer. Children of his own, he appears to be in a loving relationship with his family as opposed to the kind of monster Arthur was to his own.
    -   In "[Risky Business](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_201:_Risky_Business "Resurrection Episode 201: Risky Business")," [Harry Morgan](/wiki/Harry_Morgan/Resurrection "Harry Morgan/Resurrection") compares the two, noting that at first glance, Al doesn't seem anything like Mitchell.
-   Despite Al's jolly demeanor when he converses with Prater and the other killers, he says a few things that he never follows up on:
    -   After the first dinner party he claims that he's going to start up a group chat to maybe go on tour with them to Ellis Island. Given Lowell's disappearance and Mia's arrest however, it's possible he didn't get around to it.
    -   While friendly with Mia when they interacted at the dinner party, and showing some sadness when learning of her 'suicide', Al was quick to agree with Gareth's (really Gareth's Twin) proclamation that girls shouldn't be involved in the group, as well as believing that Mia could potentially give up information on 'Gemini'. This also shows a hidden misogyny that Al had previously hidden.
    -   Al claims that he intended to reach out to Dexter following the Gareth incident but never did. He also claims that he intended to give Dexter a call after he left during the intermission of Hamilton but did not follow through on this. Given the fact that he's choosing to book it from the group it's possible he just wants to cut all ties with them until whatever's going on cools down.
    -   Despite otherwise being friendly with Gareth, he's quick to change up and call him out as insane or a psycho following his death and his apparent desire to take out everyone including Al himself.
-   Al’s last name has not officially been stated in the show, “Al Jolly” comes from pre-release information relayed by various websites such as dexterdaily.com.
    -   However, his name is confirmed via **Final Cut** for episode 8, where Eric Stonestreet confirms the name.
    -   In "[Risky Business](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_201:_Risky_Business "Resurrection Episode 201: Risky Business")," Dexter calls him Al Walker.
-   Al is the only killer to escape Dexter effectively - leaving the state before he can be captured.
    -   Lila West left the country and went to Paris though Dexter tracked her down to kill her. Dexter would be unable to leave New York City to track Al and has no idea where he lives in Wisconsin to find him.
    -   Dexter was unable to meet with and confront Al, let alone inject him with M99. Al’s cautious nature had him leave a play during its intermission and immediately got to driving back to Wisconsin.
        -   Al's cautiousness is further shown in that as soon as he was done speaking to Dexter on the phone provided by Prater, he tossed it out the window and let it smash.
    -   Al doesn’t actually know Dexter intended to kill him, even had a kill room set up for him nor his true identity, believing him to still be “Red” by the time he’s left.
        -   This benefited Dexter as he couldn’t have let Al go free if Batista came in - he hid his face from Vinny but given his personal nature with the other killers he would have shown himself to Al. Either he commits the kill or frees him and ends up in hot water.
    -   Al is able to evade Dexter for three years before Dexter finally catches up to and kills him. During that time, Dexter has a near-miss where he arrives after Al has already left the area.
-   Dexter only chose to go after Al after all the rest had been dealt with.
    -   With Lowell he took immediate action due to his active target [Jules](/wiki/Jules "Jules")
    -   With Mia he initially wanted to get close to her as a fellow killer with a code but learned she has no code, which leads him to framing her for the murder of Ryan Foster to spare his son. His other reason for sparing Mia was that he was concerned about suspicion falling upon him if too many of the other killers disappeared in quick succession.
    -   With Gareth, his arrogance irritated Dexter enough during the second meeting that he couldn't help targeting him now: likely not his original intention but he had no choice, especially because of how close this brother got to his life by quickly stalking him and finding his home address. When he kills Gareth he also has to contend with his brother whom he chooses to target immediately - especially after he reveals he's got his own suspicions.
    -   With Al it seems like he didn't really want to kill him - until he witnessed his presentation video. Perhaps there was a part of him thinking of just letting it go but he knew he was responsible for murdering innocent people so he likely would have targeted him anyways.
    -   If Dexter had chosen to wait on killing Gareth the Lowell situation would not have been resolved (along with the threat Gareth could have created if he didn't take the chance) and instead went after Al first, then it's possible he could have killed all of the serial killers while in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")** instead of one of them getting away. However, with the Lowell situation not being resolved and Gareth possibly stalking Dexter, it would have put people around him in danger given Gemini's M.O. Ultimately Dexter made the correct decision in taking out Gareth when he did - Al doesn't pose an immediate threat to him or his loved ones, at least not at present.
-   Al’s odd giggling and speech during his kills suggests a return to a mental state from his childhood, similar to Arthur Mitchell reliving his position as a boy around each of his victims.
-   Dexter refers to Al’s “mask”, which in comparison to other killers is the most normal of the group. He comes off as a happy, loving family man who cares about everyone else around him even among killers. If this mask were to come off, he appears to have a cruel yet raged scowl which differs significantly from his happy go lucky smiles.
    -   He even cautions Al that if his family were to ever see it then it would be nothing but darkness. While he's telling him the truth and remembering a similar family man with **[Arthur Mitchell](/wiki/Arthur_Mitchell "Arthur Mitchell")**, he could also be referring to himself and the risks he faced while married to Rita.
-   Al’s one of the greedier members of the group, as seen when he runs over and immediately pockets extra money offered by Prater. That said he adheres to the rules and hasn’t asked for more than what’s given him.
-   While Al is a heavy-set man, he is shown to be quite athletic, regularly cycling with his family and he later showed great physical strength as he was able to overpower Dexter in a direct confrontation and came close to strangling Dexter to death before Dexter outwitted him with a hoist chain.
