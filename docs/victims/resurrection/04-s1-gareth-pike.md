**Gareth Pike****  
The Gemini Killer, the quiet man of Leon Prater's group.**

**Gareth Pike** (also referred to as **Gareth 1**) is a major antagonist in the first season of the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime") series **_[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")_.**[\[2\]](#cite_note-2)

Alongside his **[homonymous identical twin](/wiki/Gareth%27s_Twin "Gareth's Twin")** (who is often referred to as **Gareth 2** by viewers), he is one of the two **Gemini Killers**.

Perhaps unlike his brother, he's far more verbose and concerned about his image - believing that Gemini is the greatest serial killer in history having earned their place on the top of the **[Federal Bureau of Investigation](/wiki/Federal_Bureau_of_Investigation "Federal Bureau of Investigation")'**s most wanted list. Both killers are responsible for killing their victims in pairs - with the belief that a victim is never closer with someone else than at each other's deaths.

He is among **[Prater's Serial Killers](/wiki/Prater%27s_Serial_Killers "Prater's Serial Killers")**, a group of loyal serial killers that are bankrolled by the billionaire venture capitalist **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** and serves as one of the Central Antagonists of the series. Following his brother, he is the **Fourth Victim** of **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")** and targeted due to the same reasons as his brother.

## Personality\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

"_**Meet the guy voted "Most Likely to Become a Serial Killer" in his high school year book  
~Lowell**_"

From what has been seen thus far, Gareth is a quiet man - differing from the more extroverted nature Al or Lowell have demonstrated. As Lowell puts it, he's a "walking stereotype" of a serial killer - keeps to himself, doesn't share his life with the rest. That said...he's polite to his fellow colleagues, being shown Al's children which he seems to take an interest in just like the rest. Of interest when he encounters Prater he nods out of respect to him, vs a traditional goodbye - which may indicate he's more of a "professional" than the rest considering his sizable contributions to Prater's trophy collection which show 6 pairs of victims. That said, perhaps unlike the rest, he's been around long enough to understand the rules clearly and when **[Keith Bauer](/wiki/Keith_Bauer "Keith Bauer")** doesn't show he lacks any reaction to the news like the rest of the table.

When it comes to "Red" however...Gareth seems to be suspicious of him before the rest, watching with a fixated glare at various points throughout their first meeting. It could be due to him being the "new guy" and he doesn't trust him...or it's possible that he knows that he's an imposter. His first apparent grievance with Dexter starts at their very first encounter - where he can be seen staring at him for some time after his comment about "I felt like an alien - because I wanted to kill people", when everyone was talking about their awkward high school years. When he's offered Dexter's seat so he can go "speak" with Harry, Gareth follows him with his eyes while the rest banter about how freely Dexter can talk about this. At the dinner party he continues to watch Dexter, first at a toast to "Red" joining them and again before Lowell's presentation starts - following two more times as Lowell talks about his need to kill and after Dexter confirms the name of his need to kill as "The Urge".

In reference to Gareth's own need to kill, he calls this "The Great Yearning" - suggesting that he glorifies his murderous intent. As a killer his modus operandi is currently unknown and what drives him to kill is also a mystery, though one thing is clear: he's exceptionally dangerous.

Despite being quieter and more reserved compared to his twin brother, Gareth has the same arrogance and much of the same ego... he is simply better at hiding it. When Dexter privately confronts Gareth with his discovery that Gemini is actually twins and flatters him enough, Gareth brags about it to him, sure that no one would ever believe Dexter if he told them. Gareth is more cautious nonetheless, only giving an outline of how he and his twin communicated in secret rather than revealing the details to Dexter.

Gareth's calm exterior only breaks once Dexter reveals that he had murdered Gareth's brother, causing Gareth to snap and try to kill Dexter in a rage. Despite his initial suspicions of Dexter at the party, Gareth clearly had no idea that he was actually killing the group until Dexter revealed it to him. Gareth's attack enables Dexter to frame his murder as self-defense and Gareth as an unhinged, egotistical serial killer who was planning on taking out the whole group as his masterpiece.

## Background\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

He and **[his brother](/wiki/Gareth%27s_Twin "Gareth's Twin")** were both born as twins, both being named **Gareth** by their mother.[\[3\]](#cite_note-3) It's unknown what created their need to kill but collectively they created the name **Gemini** - under this name they (acting as one) desired to be the greatest serial killer in history. The two of them would go on to kill at least 32 victims - brothers, sisters, sons and fathers, lovers...in their view the closest you are with someone is in the moments before death.

At some point one of the brothers would meet with **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")**, presenting him photos of their victims. He never revealed the truth about his brother and would go on to fill his collection with 16 photos - with photos of the "moments before" a kill paying double. Money and fame would impact his brother's ego, though Gareth did enjoy relishing in the infamy himself.

### DEXTER: Resurrection - Season One\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth arrives in **[New York City](/wiki/New_York_City "New York City")** to attend a series of **Gatherings** hosted by **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")**, where he meets **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")** for the first time.

#### Meeting "Red"\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

After Dexter effectively infiltrates **[Leon Prater's Manor](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Manor "Leon Prater's Manor")** under the guise of "Ronald Schmidt", going by "Red", he begins to meet several of the killers one by one starting with **[Mia Lapierre](/wiki/Mia_Lapierre "Mia Lapierre")**. As they talk it's clear that their mutual interest in one another is leading to them coming closer, which is followed up by the arrival of **[Al Walker](/wiki/Al_Walker "Al Walker")** who warmly greets both of them and the party-animal **[Lowell Sloane](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane")** who does the same. Once they all settle in and start talking about their craft, **Gareth** arrives apologizing that he had difficulty obtaining a ride-share. Lowell jokes that maybe "Red" had something to do with that, given the original killer's obsession with killing ride-share drivers and Dexter laughs. Al looks to Gareth, excited as he introduces him to Dexter saying that he's **The Gemini Killer** and asks how his year's been. Gareth declines, saying he'd rather not...and Lowell chimes in "Oh Gare-bear, my guy...try not to be such a stereotype". He then tells "Red" that he's met the guy who was voted "Most Likely to Become a Serial Killer" in his high school year book, with Gareth wearing a small smirk. Everyone else chimes in, followed by Dexter who remarks that he "felt like an alien - because I wanted to kill people". After he chuckles and everyone follows, Gareth can be seen staring down at Dexter for awhile until Dexter excuses himself to go "speak" with **[Harry Morgan](/wiki/Harry_Morgan/Resurrection "Harry Morgan/Resurrection")** by the snack bar. He offers Gareth his seat which he takes though he follows Dexter with his eyes, before turning his attention to the others and their discussion.

For most of the night Gareth remains relatively quiet, even when it's announced that **[Keith Bauer](/wiki/Keith_Bauer "Keith Bauer")**, one of their members, would not be joining them as he violated the one rule - do not turn against the group. While everyone else looks on with varying emotional reactions or concern as they look among each other, Gareth remains calm and poised - focusing on Prater until he's done speaking then resumes his glare on Dexter. As the dinner rolls on his focus keeps shifting to Dexter, staring at him in almost the same way Dexter and Lowell look at their victims...and when Lowell's presentation starts he focuses on everything Lowell says until they start discussing their need to kill. Each killer names their desire...with Gareth saying proudly that it's "The Great Yearning" for him and he glares at Dexter as he reveals his need being "The Urge", though Mia is also eying Dexter at this time.

Following the dinner, Gareth can be seen sitting with Al being shown photos of his newborn **[Mabel Walker](/wiki/Mabel_Walker "Mabel Walker")** and is later joined by Lowell until **[Charley Brown](/wiki/Charley_Brown "Charley Brown")** arrives with phones for each of them so they can "stay in touch" over the next couple weeks. As they get set to leave Gareth turns his gaze towards Dexter one more time - who as of yet has not noticed how much Gareth has been staring him down and he heads out, giving Leon Prater a nod of respect which is returned in kind before he departs.

"I had a [brother](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser"). He was... like us."  
"Did he know about what you do?"  
"For a little while there, I thought...maybe we'd have what you two have. An honest relationship. I think about that sometimes...how things might have been - if we hadn't been strangers most of our lives. If we'd been able to work together."  
"What went wrong?"  
"We found each other in our 30's...little late for brotherly bonding.**_"

Dexter meets his honesty with truth about his own brother...and while he didn't say it directly, that "what went wrong" between them is killing his brother to protect his foster sister **[Debra Morgan](/wiki/Debra_Morgan "Debra Morgan")**. Dexter then asks him how he does it - kept his secret all these years and Gareth tells him that while he can't get into specifics - there are rules they follow.

"_**Okay, rule number one - never be seen together. We've developed a highly specific system for communication""  
"You mean, like leaving notes at bookstores? I was so starstruck being in the same room with my idol, I couldn't resist and I followed you. I'm curious...they say twins can feel each other's pain, is that true? Did you feel a sting in your chest last night - around 8:20?"  
"Why?"  
"Because that's when I stabbed your brother in the chest. Did you feel a hot flash when I threw his body in the furnace? I'm glad that you and your twin found your thing together. Me? I have my own thing - Killing Killers.**_"

As Dexter reveals the truth that he killed **[Gareth's Twin](/wiki/Gareth%27s_Twin "Gareth's Twin")**, Gareth is overcome with rage and he breaks his wine glass on the bench as Dexter stands up ready for him. He goes in swiping twice, managing to cut open Dexter's right arm before attempting a third jab towards his chest. Unfortunately for Gareth, Dexter's martial arts training pays off as he dodges to the side, grabs hold of Gareth's arm with the glass which is then flipped around and jabbed into his carotid artery causing him to start bleeding out. As he falls to the floor he weakly says in gasps saying "He k-killed my brother". Dexter, realizing that he needs to die now turns around calling for help then uses that as an excuse to grab a shard of glass which he jabs deep into Gareth's neck, causing him to drown in his own blood as Dexter says..

"_**Shhh...your secret is safe with me**_"

## Aftermath\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Following his death, Dexter puts the bloody wine glass shard he used to finish the job in his pocket for later and calls out again for help. When they arrive he is told to step away from the body by Charley as Prater and Al walks up shocked by the bloody scene. Dexter says that he couldn't stop the bleeding as Prater asks...

"_**What the hell happened? Why did he go after you like that?"  
"He was acting weird, saying all kinds of crazy shit. He-he was calling this his ultimate share."  
"I knew this guy was insane!" (Al)  
"He was going on about how he's gonna prove he was a visionary by taking out Lowell and me and Al."  
"Put the gun away, Charley. This is my fault - I encouraged his delusions of grandeur. Always told him his work was extraordinary. Obviously, my patronage over the years only made him feel more entitled."  
"And more Psycho!**_"

Charley calls the situation into doubt saying that the Gemini killer only kills in pairs yet now he's picking people off one by one with "a wine glass. It doesn't make sense". Dexter defends himself saying he came at him what was he supposed to do and Al's skittish behavior prompts Prater to tell him that he doesn't want this to disrupt the rest of his time with them. He says that "We've taken care of a big problem, thanks to Red. The worst is behind us" and tells him that Charley will look after the cut on his arm.

"_**Gareth may have been the favorite son around here but today Prater saw the Gareth I wanted him to see: a lunatic who got too big for his britches. And the cherry on top? Aside from killing twins, the Lowell problem is officially solved. Three birds, one stone.**_"

Later on in a room of the castle she bandages up the wound and tells him that just an inch to the left and Gareth would have cut the radial artery - Dexter would have bled out and been dead in two minutes. Bandaged up he returns home and pulls out his blood slide box, where the slide containing his Twin's blood sample is pulled out revealing it's somewhat off center leaving room on the side. Dexter then adds blood from the wine glass shard next to it and places The Gemini Killers in slot one of his new blood slide box.

-   "[Touched by an Angel](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_109:_Touched_by_an_Angel "Resurrection Episode 109: Touched by an Angel")"

While telling Harrison about his targeting of Prater's group, Dexter reveals that he had killed the Tattoo Collector and the Gemini Killer.

"_**The Gemini? He -- He's been killing people for years, and you just took care of him?"  
"Them. Twins.**_"

After learning Dexter's true identity from [Angel Batista](/wiki/Angel_Batista/Resurrection "Angel Batista/Resurrection"), Prater mentions that he was Gemini's benefactor and offers to do the same thing for the Bay Harbor Butcher, helping Dexter to get his victims.

## Victims\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother have murdered at least 12 people as based off his trophies in Leon Prater's Trophy Collection. Some of the pictures show the same victims but either closer up or farther away, though they likely has more trophies to his name, as Lowell murdered over 26 and only presented 3 trophies to Prater.

-   The Carpenters
-   The Keller Twins
-   Bianca and Ash Mitchell
-   Michael and Skylar Williams

### Motive\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother consider people dying in pairs to be their gift as it's said that people enter and exit this life alone. Killing people alongside their loved ones creates the ultimate bond and they never love each other more than in that moment. In addition to this view of their killings they also desired to become the greatest serial killer in history operating under one name - this secret remains guarded however, with neither killer revealing the truth about a twin.

### Modus Operandi\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother take a pair of related victims such as father and son, brother and sister or couples and tie them up. They then separately brutally slash their throats before posing them next to each other with the blood arcs at the head and feet resembling the constellation Gemini, pairs bound in life and death. Aside from the posed bodies, Gemini leaves no physical evidence at the scene of the crime as well as a lack of a clear motive or connection, making it impossible for the authorities to locate and identify him.

### Trophies\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Gareth and his brother take polaroid photographs of his victims both dead and before he kills them, with 16 of these photographs being present in **[Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")**. Prater pays them extra for "previews," pictures taken in the moment before the twins kill their victims when they know that it's going to happen. It's unknown how many total he has.

## Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGareth_Pike%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   A sample of his blood is added to the slide already containing his brother's, so that they are "together" in death.
    -   This makes Gemini one of only two known Blood Slides that contain the joined blood of relatives. The first case is [The Lindquists](/wiki/The_Lindquists "The Lindquists"), though their blood was combined to make one big blood slide.
-   Unlike his other victims in the season thus far, Dexter does not dispose of Gareth's body in the furnace (as he was unable to) - instead it is left for presumably Charley to deal with.
-   Similar to **[Oliver Saxon](/wiki/Oliver_Saxon "Oliver Saxon")**, Dexter chooses to stage a scene where Gareth attacks him.
    -   Both killers manage to wound Dexter, with Saxon stabbing his left shoulder (he was aiming for his heart but Dexter quickly shifted his body) and Gareth cutting his left arm - an inch to the left wound have been fatal.
    -   Both killers were disarmed and stabbed in the neck with the weapon they tried to use on Dexter, drowning in their blood.
    -   Both kills were public events, staged as "self-defense" from a crazed attacker. Unlike the security footage which showed Dexter's calm mannerisms and ruthless efficiency in taking out Saxon, there was only the eye witness accounts seeing the altercation between Dexter and Gareth from afar.
-   Gareth is one of the only people he has told about his brother **[Brian Moser](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser")** in confidence - connecting with him on a level with his own brother. He laments that he sometimes thinks what their lives would have been like had they not been separated and strangers to one another in their 30's.
    -   One of the flashback glimpses is from [Episode 607: Nebraska](/wiki/Episode_607:_Nebraska "Episode 607: Nebraska") where Brian returned as a "ghost" to accompany Dexter throughout the episode as he left town in search of **[Jonah Mitchell](/wiki/Jonah_Mitchell "Jonah Mitchell")**. This episode displayed what might have been between the two brothers had they worked together instead of how things played, which connects with his speech to Gareth about how he envys what he he had with his own brother.
-   The actor stated in an interview that Gareth is number 1 on the FBI's most wanted serial killer list - likely due to the fact that every crime scene is marked with two victims which doubles his body count.
    -   In the same interview, he also stated how the Gemini Killer is very familiar of the Bay Harbor Butcher case and knows all his mistakes.
    -   The Gemini Killer being number 1 on the FBI’s Most Wanted serial killer list is also mentioned in "[Cats and Mouse](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_106:_Cats_and_Mouse "Resurrection Episode 106: Cats and Mouse")."
    -   This makes Gareth and his twin two of Dexter's most high profile kills due to how badly they were wanted by law enforcement.
-   According to _The Killing Century_, Gareth and his brother are classified as a **Visionary Killer**.
-   It's revealed at the end of "[Cats and Mouse](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_106:_Cats_and_Mouse "Resurrection Episode 106: Cats and Mouse")" that Gareth has a [twin brother](/wiki/Gareth%27s_Twin "Gareth's Twin") - who Dexter killed and believed was the Gemini Killer alone. Both killers acted together which means their moniker is truly **The Gemini Killers**.
-   The Gemini Killers appear to operate at least primarily in Kansas as the victims listed on the website that Dexter reads are all in various places in Kansas. Actor David Dastmalchian is from Kansas.
-   Coincidentally, The Gemini Killers shared the simillar nickname with [Anthony Senter and Joseph Testa](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Senter_and_Joseph_Testa) a.k.a "The Gemini Twins" (referring to The Gemini Longue, their frequent hangout spot) a serial killer duo that worked as hitmen for Gambino Crime Family captain, Roy Demeo in the 1980s. The difference is, Senter and Testa are not related.
-   Gareth 1 is right-handed while his brother Gareth 2 is left-handed.
    -   Although Gareth is presumably the twin first seen in "[Call Me Red](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_104:_Call_Me_Red "Resurrection Episode 104: Call Me Red")," he never actually confirms or denies it when Dexter asks him outright himself. However considering he takes a glass to toast “to new friends” with his right hand it confirms that this Gareth was present during Episode 4.
    -   Additionally this Gareth has been seen both times wearing a dark suit jacket over a matching shirt while Gareth 2 wore lighter colors in comparison.
