**R.H. Martin** **The Yonkers Slayer, Self-Proclaimed Satanist**

**R.H. Martin** [\[1\]](#cite_note-1) (better known as **The Yonkers Slayer**) is a Character in the **[Showtime](/wiki/Showtime "Showtime")** original series _**[Dexter: Resurrection](/wiki/Dexter:_Resurrection "Dexter: Resurrection")**_.

The Yonkers Slayer is a **[Serial Killer](/wiki/Serial_Killer "Serial Killer")** whom **[Leon Prater](/wiki/Leon_Prater "Leon Prater")** had among his extensive files on "Serial Slayers". Following **[Dexter Morgan](/wiki/Dexter_Morgan/Resurrection "Dexter Morgan/Resurrection")'**s escape from **[Leon Prater's Trophy Collection](/wiki/Leon_Prater%27s_Trophy_Collection "Leon Prater's Trophy Collection")**, he kept a selection of files that Prater kept stored in filing cabinets within his Vault. Among these killers are **[Al Walker](/wiki/Al_Walker "Al Walker")** and the **[Midnight Mangler](/wiki/Midnight_Mangler "Midnight Mangler")**.

He is one of several targets Dexter kills going into **[Resurrection Season Two](/wiki/Resurrection_Season_Two "Resurrection Season Two")**.

## Summary\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FR.H._Martin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Following the events of the **[10th Annual Gala](/wiki/10th_Annual_Gala "10th Annual Gala")**, Dexter began targeting the remaining five killers in Prater's "boogeyman" club to finally put this group of killers to rest.

### DEXTER: Resurrection Season 2\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FR.H._Martin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   '**[Risky Business](/wiki/Resurrection_Episode_201:_Risky_Business "Resurrection Episode 201: Risky Business")'**

In early 2025, Dexter began hunting down the killers and started with the **[Midnight Mangler](/wiki/Midnight_Mangler "Midnight Mangler")**, whom he captured before his abduction of a woman and summarily disposed of with an axe to the chest. In belief that had he not killed the Mangler, at least another 32 innocent victims could have been murdered. With the first killer out of the way he set his sights on the **Yonkers Slayer**, a man obsessed with ritualistic killing as a self proclaimed satanist who targeted at least 20 people prior to Dexter capturing him. Dexter flips through his folder revealing at least two of his victims placed on altars, with a bloody cross painted on their foreheads stripped down and covered in red sheets.

Dexter is shown to have already captured the killer and tied him with plastic wrap to a sewer pipe while proclaiming that "the Devil made me do it!". Dexter stands before him and leans in with a hand to the pipe, telling him that "it must be nice...to blame your actions on someone other than yourself". He takes a knife and stabs it into the man's chest, who stares at Dexter's face before falling limp from blood loss. Before Dexter departs, he flicks the stylized goatee on the man's face and remarks to himself that thanks to his actions saved at least another 20 people. When disposed of, the Slayer is chopped up and thrown into the furnace at the **[Abandoned Factory](/wiki/Abandoned_Factory "Abandoned Factory")** where Dexter tells his burning corpse "Welcome to Hell, Mr. Slayer" before slamming the doors shut for the last time.

### Notes\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fdexter.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FR.H._Martin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

-   The Slayer is the last victim Dexter disposes of at the Abandoned Factory, which he used to dispose of fellow Prater "boogeymen" **[Ronald Schmidt](/wiki/Ronald_Schmidt "Ronald Schmidt")**, **[Lowell Sloane](/wiki/Lowell_Sloane "Lowell Sloane")** and **[Gareth's Twin](/wiki/Gareth%27s_Twin "Gareth's Twin")**. It's possible that he also disposed of the Midnight Mangler here prior to the Slayer. The reason that he had to stop using this location is due to "gentrification" which would turn the building into a Whole Foods.
-   The Slayer is the 13th victim in Dexter's new bloodslide box, with a unique design of a pentagram drawn into the bloodslide (due to his religious beliefs as a self proclaimed Satanist).
    -   The last time something unique was drawn into a bloodslide was by **[Brian Moser](/wiki/Brian_Moser "Brian Moser")** using **[Valerie Castillo](/wiki/Valerie_Castillo "Valerie Castillo")'**s bloodslide, in which he drew a smiley face as part of the game in making Dexter remember his past.
